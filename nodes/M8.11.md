# M8.11 Continuous Conditional Density / 连续条件密度

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M8.10](M8.10.md), [M5.1](M5.1.md)

**Source anchors**: $f_{X\mid Y}=f(x,y)/f_Y(y)$; conditional expectation as integral.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Given $Y=y$, describe the conditional law of continuous $X$ via a **conditional density**, and compute $E(X\mid Y=y)$.

已知 $Y=y$ 时，用**条件密度**描述连续 $X$，并算 $E(X\mid Y=y)$。

---

## Step 1: Definition / 第一步：定义

$$f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)=\frac{f_{X,Y}(x,y)}{f_Y(y)}\quad(f_Y(y)>0).$$

---

## Step 2: Conditional expectation / 第二步：条件期望

$$E(X\mid Y=y)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}x\,f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)\,dx.$$

---

## Step 3: Independence / 第三步：独立

If $X,Y$ independent, $f_{X\mid Y}=f_X$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Conditional density is joint over marginal (same pattern as discrete).  
2. Must have $f_Y(y)>0$; otherwise undefined.  
3. Independence ⇔ conditional = marginal.  
4. $E(X\mid Y)$ is a random variable; $E(X\mid Y=y)$ is a number.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Dividing by $f_X$ instead of $f_Y$ | Conditioning variable in the denominator |
| Treating $f_{X\mid Y}$ as a joint | Integrates to 1 in $x$ for each $y$ |
| Confusing $E(X\mid Y)$ with $E(X)$ | Conditioning changes the value |

---
