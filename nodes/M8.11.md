# M8.11 Continuous Conditional Density / 连续条件密度

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M8.10](M8.10.md), [M5.1](M5.1.md)

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Given $Y=y$, the conditional density of $X$.

---

## Formula / 公式

$$f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)=\frac{f(x,y)}{f_Y(y)} \quad (f_Y(y)>0).$$

Then $E(X\mid Y=y)=\int x f_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)\,dx$.

---

## Practice / 练习

Independence implies $f_{X\mid Y}=f_X$. True/false?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>True.</details>
