# M2.14 Digit Permutation Sums / 数字排列求和

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.3](M0.3.md)

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Sum of all numbers formed by permuting given digits: each digit appears equally often in each place.

---

## Practice / 练习

Digits 1,2,3: each place sum $2\times(1+2+3)=12$, total $12\times 111=1332$.
