# M2.6 Partitions of Distinct Objects / 不同物体的分堆

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.3](M0.3.md), [M2.4](M2.4.md)

**Source anchors**: multinomial coefficient $n!/(n_1!\cdots n_k!)$; labeled groups.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Divide $n$ **distinct** objects into **labeled** groups of sizes $n_1,\ldots,n_k$ (with $n_1+\cdots+n_k=n$).

把 $n$ 个**不同**物体分到**有标签**、大小为 $n_1,\ldots,n_k$ 的组。

---

## Step 1: Formula / 第一步：公式

$$\frac{n!}{n_1!\,n_2!\cdots n_k!}.$$

Choose $n_1$ for group 1, then $n_2$ for group 2, … — the factorials in the denominator cancel the internal order of each group (groups are unordered sets).

---

## Step 2: Labeled vs unlabeled / 第二步：有标签 vs 无标签

If groups are **indistinguishable** (same size), divide further by the number of ways to permute those identical-size groups.

Example: 10 people into labeled rooms of sizes 4,3,3: $10!/(4!3!3!)$.  
If the two size-3 rooms were unlabeled, divide by $2!$.

---

## Step 3: Hand checks / 第三步：手算

$10!/(4!3!3!)=4200$.  
Binomial $\binom{n}{k}=n!/(k!(n-k)!)$ is the two-group case.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Distinct objects + labeled group sizes → multinomial coefficient.  
2. Denominator factorials remove order inside each group.  
3. Extra division if some groups are indistinguishable.  
4. Links to multinomial probabilities in [M2.15](M2.15.md).

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Forgetting denominators when groups are sets | Divide by $n_i!$ |
| Treating unlabeled same-size groups as labeled | Extra symmetry factor |
| Using $n^{k}$ | Wrong model |

---

**1.** 10 people into labeled rooms of sizes 4,3,3. Count:

A. $\binom{10}{4}$

D. $P(10,4)$

Multinomial.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**2.** Multinomial $n!/(n_1!\cdots n_k!)$ assumes groups are

A. unlabeled always  
B. labeled (distinguishable)  
C. ordered sequences  
D. with replacement

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Labeled bins.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**4.** If two groups of size 3 are **unlabeled**, take $10!/(4!3!3!)$ and

A. multiply by $2$

D. use $10^{3}$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Answer: **B**.
</details>

**6.** Denominator $n_i!$ removes
