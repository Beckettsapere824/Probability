# M2.6 Partitions of Distinct Objects / 不同物体的分堆

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.3](M0.3.md)

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Divide $n$ distinct objects into labeled groups of sizes $n_1,\ldots,n_k$: multinomial $n!/(n_1!\cdots n_k!)$.

---

## Practice / 练习

10 people into labeled rooms of 4,3,3?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>$10!/(4!3!3!)$.</details>
