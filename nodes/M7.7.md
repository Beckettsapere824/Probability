# M7.7 Reciprocal Moments (special identities) / 倒数矩恒等式

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M4.2](M4.2.md), [M3.2](M3.2.md)

**Source anchors**: $E[1/(X+1)]$ for binomial / Poisson; integral representation $1/(k+1)=\int_0^1 t^k\,dt$.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Expectations of the form $E[1/(X+1)]$ appear in occupancy and coupon problems. Closed forms use integral tricks or combinatorial identities rather than summing $p(k)/(k+1)$ by brute force.

$E[1/(X+1)]$ 型期望用积分表示或组合恒等式处理。

---

## Step 1: Integral identity / 第一步：积分恒等式

For integer $k\ge 0$,
$$\frac{1}{k+1}=\int_0^1 t^k\,dt.$$
Thus $E[1/(X+1)]=\int_0^1 E[t^X]\,dt$ when the switch is justified.

---

## Step 2: Binomial example / 第二步：二项例子

$X\sim\mathrm{Bin}(n,p)$: $E[t^X]=(q+pt)^n$, so
$$E\Bigl[\frac{1}{X+1}\Bigr]=\int_0^1(q+pt)^n\,dt=\frac{1-q^{n+1}}{(n+1)p}\quad(p>0).$$

---

## Step 3: Hand check / 第三步：手算

$n=1$, $p=1/2$: $E[1/(X+1)]=\frac12\cdot1+\frac12\cdot\frac12=\frac34$, and $(1-(1/2)^2)/(2\cdot1/2)=3/4$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Reciprocal of a count is not $1/E(X)$.  
2. Integral representation turns the sum into an ordinary generating function integral.  
3. Same idea extends to $E[1/\binom{X+a}{b}]$ style identities.  
4. Always check $p=0$ edge separately.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Replacing $E[1/(X+1)]$ by $1/(E(X)+1)$ | Jensen / nonlinearity |
| Forgetting $p=0$ case | Direct computation |
| Integrating outside $[0,1]$ | Match the identity |

**1.** $\dfrac{1}{k+1}=$

Standard integral.

**2.** $E[1/(X+1)]$ equals $1/(E(X)+1)$
