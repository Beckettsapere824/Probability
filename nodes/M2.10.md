# M2.10 Pairings, Couples, Socks and Shoes / 配对、夫妻、袜子鞋子计数

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M2.9 Multivariate Hypergeometric](M2.9.md)

---

## Content / 内容

Typical counting pattern: first choose complete pairs, then choose single items from the remaining pairs. Distinguish carefully whether objects of the same color/style are indistinguishable (socks) or whether left/right are distinguishable while the pairing structure is the same (shoes).

典型计数模式：先选完整配对，再从剩余配对中各选一只。仔细区分同色同款是否不可区分（袜子）还是左右可区分但配对结构相同（鞋子）。

For the classic “exactly one married couple” problem one first chooses the couple that is fully selected, then chooses one person from each of the remaining couples while ensuring no additional full couple is formed.

经典“恰好一对夫妻”问题：先选被完整选中的那一对，再从其余夫妻中各选一人并保证不再形成新的完整夫妻对。

Always separate the events “exactly one pair” and “at least one pair”.

始终区分“恰好一对”与“至少一对”。

## Example / 例子

Four married couples. Choose 4 people so that exactly one married couple is included: C(4,1) ways to choose the couple, then C(3,2) ways to choose two more couples and from each of those two couples choose 1 person out of 2, giving 2·2 = 4, but subtract the cases that accidentally form another couple (none if only one person from each). Final count C(4,1)·C(3,2)·2·2.

四对夫妻。选 4 人恰含一对夫妻：C(4,1) 选那一对，再 C(3,2) 选另外两对并从每对中选 1 人（各 2 种），得 C(4,1)·C(3,2)·2·2。

## Practice Problem / 练习题

A drawer contains 10 red socks and 10 blue socks (indistinguishable within color). Two socks are drawn at random. Probability they form a pair (same color)?

抽屉有 10 红袜 10 蓝袜（同色不可区分）。随机抽两只。它们成对（同色）的概率？

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

Total ways C(20,2). Same color: C(10,2)+C(10,2). Probability = 2·C(10,2)/C(20,2).

总方式 C(20,2)。同色：C(10,2)+C(10,2)。概率 = 2·C(10,2)/C(20,2)。

</details>
