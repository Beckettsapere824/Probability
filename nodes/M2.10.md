# M2.10 Pairings (socks, couples) / 配对计数

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.3](M0.3.md)

**Source anchors**: number of perfect matchings of $2n$ points: $(2n)!/(2^n n!)$.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Number of ways to partition $2n$ distinct people into $n$ unordered pairs.

将 $2n$ 个不同的人分成 $n$ 个无序对的方案数。

---

## Step 1: Count / 第一步：计数

$$\frac{(2n)!}{2^n n!}.$$

---

## Step 2: Double factorial / 第二步：双阶乘

$(2n-1)!!=1\cdot3\cdot5\cdots(2n-1)$ equals the same count.

---

## Step 3: Hand check / 第三步：手算

4 people: $4!/(4\cdot2)=3$ pairings.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Divide out internal pair order and order of pairs.  
2. Same count as perfect matchings in $K_{2n}$.  
3. Socks / handshakes / couples use this number.  
4. Pairs are unlabeled.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Forgetting ÷ $n!$ | Pairs unordered |
| Forgetting ÷ $2^n$ | Within-pair order |
| Using $\binom{2n}{2}$ only once | Need sequential partition |

---

## Practice / 练习（选择题）

**1.** Number of perfect pairings of $2n$ people is

A. $(2n)!$  
B. $(2n)!/(2^n n!)$  
C. $\binom{2n}{2}$  
D. $n!$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Standard.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**2.** 4 people into 2 unordered pairs:

A. $1$  
B. $3$  
C. $6$  
D. $24$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$24/(4\cdot2)=3$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**3.** $(2n-1)!!$ equals

A. $(2n)!$  
B. number of pairings  
C. $2^n$  
D. $n!$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Double factorial identity.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**4.** Divide by $2^n$ because

A. pairs are ordered  
B. order inside each pair is irrelevant  
C. $n$ is even  
D. continuous

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Unordered within pair.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**5.** Divide by $n!$ because

A. people are identical  
B. the collection of pairs is unordered  
C. $2n$  
D. injections

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Unordered set of pairs.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**6.** $\binom{2n}{2}\binom{2n-2}{2}\cdots\binom{2}{2}$ still needs

A. nothing  
B. ÷ $n!$ if pairs are unordered  
C. × $n!$  
D. only $2^n$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Sequential choice orders the pairs.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**7.** Handshakes among $2n$ people if each has one partner

A. $2n$  
B. same pairing count  
C. $(2n)^2$  
D. $0$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Perfect matching.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**8.** This node counts

A. only cycles  
B. unordered perfect matchings  
C. only derangements  
D. continuous paths

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Pair partitions.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>
