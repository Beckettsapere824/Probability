# M5.1 Conditional Distributions (Discrete) / 离散条件分布

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M1.3 Conditional Probability](M1.3.md), [M1.10 Joint discrete](M1.10.md)

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Given that $Y=y$, what is the law of $X$? That **conditional distribution** is the right input to conditional expectation, prediction, and Bayesian updates after an observation.

给定 $Y=y$，$X$ 的律是什么？**条件分布**是条件期望、预测与观测后贝叶斯更新的输入。

---

## Step 1: Conditional PMF / 第一步：条件 PMF

If $\Pr(Y=y)>0$,

$$p_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)=\Pr(X=x\mid Y=y)=\frac{p_{X,Y}(x,y)}{p_Y(y)}.$$

As a function of $x$ (for fixed $y$) this is a PMF: non-negative and $\sum_x p_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)=1$.  
Interpretation: renormalise the slice of the joint table where $Y=y$ so that the slice sums to 1.

对固定 $y$，作为 $x$ 的函数仍是 PMF：把联合表中 $Y=y$ 的那一行（列）重新归一化。

---

## Step 2: Conditional expectation from the conditional PMF / 第二步：由条件 PMF 得条件期望

$$E(X\mid Y=y)=\sum_x x\,p_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y).$$

The random variable $E(X\mid Y)$ takes the value $E(X\mid Y=y)$ when $Y=y$.  
Adam’s law ([M3.11](M3.11.md)): $E(X)=E\bigl[E(X\mid Y)\bigr]$.

---

## Step 3: Bayesian update sketch / 第三步：贝叶斯更新示意

Prior $p_X$ × likelihood $\Pr(\text{data}\mid X=x)$ → unnormalised posterior.  
Normaliser = $\Pr(\text{data})$ via total probability.  
Result is the conditional PMF of $X$ given the data — same formula as Step 1 with $Y$ = “data occurred”.

先验 × 似然 → 未归一后验；用全概率归一。结果就是给定数据后 $X$ 的条件 PMF。

---

## Step 4: Hand example / 第四步：手算

Joint: $p(0,0)=0.1$, $p(0,1)=0.2$, $p(1,0)=0.3$, $p(1,1)=0.4$.  
$p_Y(0)=0.4$, so

$$p_{X\mid Y}(1\mid 0)=\frac{0.3}{0.4}=0.75, \qquad p_{X\mid Y}(0\mid 0)=\frac{0.1}{0.4}=0.25.$$

$E(X\mid Y=0)=1\cdot 0.75+0\cdot 0.25=0.75$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Conditional PMF = joint slice renormalised by the marginal of the conditioner.  
2. $E(X\mid Y=y)$ is a number; $E(X\mid Y)$ is a random variable.  
3. Bayes posterior is a conditional distribution.  
4. Adam’s law averages the conditional means.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Dividing by $p_X$ instead of $p_Y$ | Denominator = marginal of the **given** variable |
| Treating the joint table as already conditional | Must divide by $p_Y(y)$ |
| Confusing $E(X\mid Y=y)$ with $E(X\mid Y)$ | Number vs random variable |
| Forgetting the slice must sum to 1 after division | Check $\sum_x p(x\mid y)=1$ |

---

## Worked exam-style example / 考试风格例题

**Problem.** Same joint table. Find $p_{X\mid Y}(1\mid 0)$ and $E(X\mid Y=0)$.

**Solution.** $0.75$ and $0.75$ (as above).

## Practice / 练习

1. Same joint. $p_{X\mid Y}(0\mid 1)$?

2. If $X,Y$ independent, what is $p_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)$?

3. Why is $E(X\mid Y)$ a random variable?

<details>
<summary>Solution sketches / 解答提示</summary>

1. $p_Y(1)=0.6$, $p(0,1)=0.2$ → $0.2/0.6=1/3$.

2. $p_X(x)$ — conditioning on $Y$ does not change the law of $X$.

3. Its value depends on the realised value of $Y$.

</details>
