# M5.1 Conditional Distributions (Discrete) / 离散条件分布

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M1.3 Conditional Probability](M1.3.md), [M1.10 Joint discrete](M1.10.md)

**Source / 来源**: SDST2601A Chapter 8 (discrete part); continuous conditional in [M8.11](M8.11.md)

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Given $Y=y$, what is the law of $X$? That conditional distribution is the right input to conditional expectation, prediction, and Bayesian updates after an observation.

给定 $Y=y$，$X$ 的律是什么？条件分布是条件期望、预测与观测后贝叶斯更新的输入。

---

## Step 1: Conditional PMF / 第一步：条件 PMF

If $\Pr(Y=y)>0$,

$$p_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)=\Pr(X=x\mid Y=y)=\frac{p_{X,Y}(x,y)}{p_Y(y)}.$$

As a function of $x$ (for fixed $y$) it is a PMF: non-negative and sums to 1.

对固定 $y$，作为 $x$ 的函数仍是 PMF。

---

## Step 2: Bayesian update sketch / 第二步：贝叶斯更新示意

Prior $p_X$ × likelihood $\Pr(E\mid X=x)$ → posterior $p_{X\mid E}$ with normaliser $\Pr(E)$ (total probability).

先验 × 似然 → 后验；归一化用 $\Pr(E)$。

---

## Step 3: Link to Adam / 第三步：接到全期望

$E(X\mid Y=y)=\sum_x x\,p_{X\mid Y}(x\mid y)$, then $E(X)=E[E(X\mid Y)]$ as in [M3.11](M3.11.md).

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

- Dividing by $p_X$ instead of $p_Y$.  
- Treating the joint table as already conditional.

---

## Practice / 练习

Joint: $p(0,0)=0.1$, $p(0,1)=0.2$, $p(1,0)=0.3$, $p(1,1)=0.4$. Find $p_{X\mid Y}(1\mid 0)$.

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

$p_Y(0)=0.1+0.3=0.4$, $p(1,0)=0.3$, so $0.3/0.4=0.75$.

</details>
