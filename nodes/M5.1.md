# M5.1 Conditional and Posterior Distributions / 条件分布与后验分布

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M1.5 Bayes’ Theorem](M1.5.md), [M1.8 Random Variables, PMF, CDF](M1.8.md)

---

## Content / 内容

After an observation E is made, the distribution of a discrete random variable X is updated to the posterior P(X=i|E) ∝ P(E|X=i) P(X=i). The normalising constant is exactly the marginal probability P(E). The posterior is then used to compute conditional expectations and to predict the next observation.

观测到 E 之后，离散随机变量 X 的分布更新为后验 P(X=i|E) ∝ P(E|X=i) P(X=i)。归一化常数恰好是边际概率 P(E)。后验随后用于计算条件期望并预测下一次观测。

The schematic is prior × likelihood → posterior. The same mechanism underlies Bayesian updating of an urn composition after a colour is observed.

示意流程为先验 × 似然 → 后验。同一机制支撑在观察到颜色后对瓮组成的贝叶斯更新。

## Example / 例子

X ~ B(2,1/2). Observe one red ball drawn from an urn that contained X red balls (and 2−X white). The likelihood P(R|X=i)=i/2. Posterior probabilities are proportional to (i/2)·C(2,i)(1/2)^2 and must be renormalised.

X ~ B(2,1/2)。从含 X 红球（及 2−X 白球）的瓮中抽到一红球。似然 P(R|X=i)=i/2。后验概率与 (i/2)·C(2,i)(1/2)^2 成正比，需重新归一化。

## Practice Problem / 练习题

Prior P(X=0)=P(X=1)=1/2. Likelihood P(E|X=0)=1/4, P(E|X=1)=3/4. Find the posterior P(X=1|E).

先验 P(X=0)=P(X=1)=1/2。似然 P(E|X=0)=1/4，P(E|X=1)=3/4。求后验 P(X=1|E)。

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

P(E)= (1/2)(1/4)+(1/2)(3/4)=1/2. P(X=1|E)=(3/8)/(1/2)=3/4.

P(E)= (1/2)(1/4)+(1/2)(3/4)=1/2。P(X=1|E)=(3/8)/(1/2)=3/4。

</details>
