# M4.5a Negative Binomial as Sum of Geometrics / 负二项=几何和

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M4.5](M4.5.md), [M4.4](M4.4.md)

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Waiting for $r$-th success = sum of $r$ i.i.d. geometric waits → mean $r/p$.

---

## Practice / 练习

Variance adds under independence: $r(1-p)/p^{2}$.
