# M4.5a Negative Binomial as Sum of Geometrics / 负二项=几何和

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M4.5](M4.5.md), [M4.4](M4.4.md)

**Source anchors**: waiting for $r$-th success; sum of i.i.d. geometrics; mean $r/p$.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Time until the $r$-th success in independent Bernoulli trials equals the sum of $r$ i.i.d. geometric waits (inter-success times).

到第 $r$ 次成功的等待 = $r$ 个 i.i.d. 几何等待之和。

---

## Step 1: Representation / 第一步：表示

$T_r=G_1+\cdots+G_r$ with $G_i$ i.i.d. geometric (trials version). Then $E(T_r)=r/p$, $\mathrm{Var}(T_r)=r q/p^{2}$.

---

## Step 2: Why useful / 第二步：用途

Linearity gives the mean without summing the NB PMF; independence gives variance additivity.

---

## Step 3: Convention / 第三步：约定

If geometric is failures-version, NB mean becomes $r q/p$ for total failures before $r$-th success.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. NB is a sum of geometrics under the right convention.  
2. Mean scales linearly with $r$.  
3. Variance also scales with $r$ (i.i.d. sum).  
4. Same logic as Erlang = sum of exponentials in continuous time.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Adding means with mismatched geometric conventions | Align supports |
| Using $np$ as if binomial | Different experiment |
| Forgetting variance factor $r$ | $r$ i.i.d. copies |
