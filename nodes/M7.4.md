# M7.4 Maximum Ball Number Order Statistic / 最大球号次序统计

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M3.7 Maximum PMF](M3.7.md), [M3.8 Order-Statistic Expectation](M3.8.md)

---

## Content / 内容

When n balls numbered 1…N are drawn without replacement the maximum Y has PMF
P(Y=y)=C(y−1,n−1)/C(N,n), y=n,…,N.
Expectation and variance are obtained by summing against this PMF and applying combinatorial identities. The support begins at n. The PMF sums to 1 by the hockey-stick identity. Variance is computed from E(Y^2)−[E(Y)]^2.

n 个编号 1…N 的球无放回抽取时，最大值 Y 的 PMF 为
P(Y=y)=C(y−1,n−1)/C(N,n)，y=n,…,N。
期望与方差通过对该 PMF 求和并应用组合恒等式得到。支撑从 n 开始。PMF 由 hockey-stick 恒等式和为 1。方差由 E(Y^2)−[E(Y)]^2 计算。

## Example / 例子

n=2, N=5. P(Y=4)=C(3,1)/C(5,2)=3/10. E(Y)=(2·6)/3=4.

n=2，N=5。P(Y=4)=C(3,1)/C(5,2)=3/10。E(Y)=(2·6)/3=4。

## Practice Problem / 练习题

From {1,…,6} draw 2 without replacement. Find P(Y=5).

从 {1,…,6} 无放回抽 2。求 P(Y=5)。
