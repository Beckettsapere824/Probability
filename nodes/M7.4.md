# M7.4 Maximum as Order Statistic / 最大值次序统计

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M1.8](M1.8.md), [M1.6](M1.6.md)

**Source anchors**: $M=\max(X_1,\ldots,X_n)$; CDF product under i.i.d.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

For i.i.d. discrete $X_i$, the maximum $M$ has
$$\Pr(M\le k)=\Pr(X_1\le k)^{n}.$$

独立同分布离散样本的最大值 CDF 是单变量 CDF 的 $n$ 次方。

---

## Step 1: Event representation / 第一步：事件表示

$\{M\le k\}=\{X_1\le k\}\cap\cdots\cap\{X_n\le k\}$. Independence ⇒ product of probabilities.

---

## Step 2: PMF of $M$ / 第二步：最大值的 PMF

$$\Pr(M=k)=\Pr(M\le k)-\Pr(M\le k-1)=F(k)^{n}-F(k-1)^{n}.$$

---

## Step 3: Hand check / 第三步：手算

Fair die, $n=2$. $\Pr(M\le 3)=(1/2)^{2}=1/4$. $\Pr(M=6)=1-(5/6)^{2}=11/36$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Max CDF = product of marginal CDFs under i.i.d.  
2. Min uses $1-(1-F)^{n}$.  
3. Dependence breaks the product formula.  
4. Continuous analogue: $F_{M}(x)=F(x)^{n}$ same algebra.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Using $nF(k)$ instead of $F(k)^{n}$ | Intersection not union |
| Applying product without independence | Need i.i.d. (or independent) |
| PMF as $f(k)^{n}$ | Difference of CDF powers |

---

## Practice / 练习（选择题）

**1.** i.i.d. $X_i$, $M=\max_i X_i$. $\Pr(M\le k)=$

A. $n\Pr(X_1\le k)$  
B. $\Pr(X_1\le k)^{n}$  
C. $\Pr(X_1\le k)/n$  
D. $1-\Pr(X_1\le k)$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Intersection under independence.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**2.** $\Pr(M=k)=$

A. $F(k)^{n}$  
B. $F(k)^{n}-F(k-1)^{n}$  
C. $nf(k)$  
D. $1-F(k)$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

CDF jump.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**3.** Fair die, $n=2$. $\Pr(M\le 3)=$

A. $1/2$  
B. $1/4$  
C. $1/6$  
D. $1$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$(3/6)^{2}=1/4$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**4.** Fair die, $n=2$. $\Pr(M=6)=$

A. $1/36$  
B. $11/36$  
C. $1/6$  
D. $1/2$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$1-(5/6)^{2}=11/36$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**5.** For the minimum $m=\min X_i$,

A. $\Pr(m\le k)=F(k)^{n}$  
B. $\Pr(m>k)=(1-F(k))^{n}$  
C. always $0$  
D. same as max

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

All exceed $k$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**6.** Product formula fails when

Independence used.

</details>
