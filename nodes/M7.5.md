# M7.5 Matching / Secretary Letter / 匹配问题

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M2.11](M2.11.md), [M3.1a](M3.1a.md)

**Source anchors**: indicator variables; $E(\#\text{fixed points})=1$; $\Pr(\text{none})\to 1/e$.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Random permutation of $n$ letters / hats. Expected number of fixed points equals $1$; probability of no fixed point tends to $1/e$.

随机排列的固定点数期望为 1；全不固定的概率趋于 $1/e$。

---

## Step 1: Indicators / 第一步：示性变量

$I_j=1$ if position $j$ is a fixed point. $X=\sum_{j=1}^{n}I_j$.  
$\Pr(\pi(j)=j)=1/n$, so $E(X)=1$.

---

## Step 2: None fixed / 第二步：全不固定

$!n/n!\to 1/e$. Series: $\Pr(X=0)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}(-1)^{k}/k!\to e^{-1}$.

---

## Step 3: Hand check / 第三步：手算

$n=2$: $\Pr(X=0)=1/2$, $E(X)=1$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Linearity gives $E(X)=1$ even though $I_j$ are dependent.  
2. Poisson paradigm: $X\approx\mathrm{Poisson}(1)$ for large $n$.  
3. Derangement probability $\sim 1/e$.  
4. Indicators are the standard tool for counting matches.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Thinking dependence blocks $E\sum I_j$ | Linearity ignores dependence |
| Using $1/n!$ for one fixed point prob | $\Pr(I_j=1)=1/n$ |
| Exact derangement vs limit | Series truncation |

---

## Practice / 练习（选择题）

**1.** Expected number of fixed points in a random $n$-permutation is

A. $0$  
B. $1$  
C. $n$  
D. $1/e$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$n\cdot(1/n)=1$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**2.** $\Pr(\pi(j)=j)=$

A. $1$  
B. $1/n$  
C. $1/n!$  
D. $1/2$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$(n-1)!/n!$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**3.** $\Pr(\text{no fixed points})\to$

A. $0$  
B. $1$  
C. $1/e$  
D. $1/2$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Derangement limit.  
Answer: **C**.
</details>

**4.** Linearity of $E\sum I_j$ requires

A. independence of $I_j$  
B. only integrability  
C. $n\to\infty$  
D. Poisson

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

No independence needed.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**5.** For large $n$, number of fixed points is approximately

A. $\mathrm{Binomial}(n,1)$  
B. $\mathrm{Poisson}(1)$  
C. Normal with mean $n$  
D. degenerate at 0

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Poisson paradigm.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**6.** $n=2$, $\Pr(X=0)=$

A. $0$  
B. $1/2$  
C. $1/e$  
D. $1$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

One of two permutations is a swap.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**7.** Indicator $I_j$ equals 1 when

A. $\pi(j)\neq j$  
B. $\pi(j)=j$  
C. $\pi$ is id only  
D. $j=1$ only

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Fixed point definition.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**8.** Derangement count $!n$ satisfies $!n/n!\approx$

A. $e$  
B. $1/e$  
C. $0$  
D. $n$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Same limit.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>
