# M8.2 Normalizing a pdf / pdf 归一化练习

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M8.1](M8.1.md)

**Source anchors**: unnormalized nonnegative $g$; constant $c$; interval probabilities via CDF.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Given a nonnegative shape $g$ on a support, find $c$ so that $f=cg$ integrates to 1, then compute probabilities of intervals.

给定非负形状 $g$，求 $c$ 使 $f=cg$ 积分为 1，再算区间概率。

---

## Step 1: Normalise / 第一步：归一化

$$\int_{\mathrm{supp}} g(x)\,dx = \frac{1}{c}\quad\Rightarrow\quad c=\Bigl(\int g\Bigr)^{-1}.$$

Require $0<\int g<\infty$.

---

## Step 2: CDF and intervals / 第二步：CDF 与区间

$F(x)=\int_{-\infty}^{x}f(t)\,dt$. For continuous $X$, $\Pr(a<X<b)=F(b)-F(a)$.

---

## Step 3: Hand check / 第三步：手算

$g(x)=x$ on $(0,2)$: $\int_0^2 x\,dx=2$, so $c=1/2$, $f(x)=x/2$. $\Pr(0<X<1)=\int_0^1 x/2\,dx=1/4$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Any integrable nonnegative shape can be a pdf after scaling.  
2. The constant is global — one $c$ for the whole support.  
3. Endpoints of continuous intervals do not matter for probability.  
4. Always verify $\int f=1$ after writing $f$.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Forgetting to integrate only on the stated support | Limits matter |
| Using $c$ as if $f=g+c$ | Multiplicative normalisation |
| $\Pr(X=a)>0$ for continuous $f$ | Point mass is zero |

---
