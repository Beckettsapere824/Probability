# M7.1 Linear Cost of a Lifetime / 寿命的线性成本

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M3.4](M3.4.md)

**Source anchors**: linearity of expectation; $E(a+bT)=a+bE(T)$.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Cost is affine in a random lifetime $T$: $\mathrm{cost}=a+bT$. Expectation passes through without needing the full law of $T$.

成本对寿命线性：$E(\mathrm{cost})=a+bE(T)$，不必先求 $T$ 的全部分布。

---

## Step 1: Linearity / 第一步：线性性

$$E(a+bT)=a+bE(T)$$

for constants $a,b$ (no independence assumption).

---

## Step 2: When full law is still needed / 第二步：何时仍需全分布

$\mathrm{Var}(a+bT)=b^{2}\mathrm{Var}(T)$; quantiles of cost need the law of $T$. Expectation alone does not give $\Pr(\mathrm{cost}>c)$.

---

## Step 3: Hand check / 第三步：手算

$E(T)=10$, $a=100$, $b=5$ ⇒ $E(\mathrm{cost})=150$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Linearity never needs independence.  
2. Affine transforms scale expectation by the slope.  
3. Variance scales by $b^{2}$; probabilities need more than $E(T)$.  
4. Useful for warranty / premium calculations when only mean lifetime is known.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Thinking linearity needs i.i.d. | Identity on sums |
| Using $E(T)$ alone for $\Pr(T>t)$ | Need tail / CDF |
| Forgetting $b$ can be negative | Formula still holds |
