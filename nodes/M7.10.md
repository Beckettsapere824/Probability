# M7.10 Ball-Selection Generating Function / 取球生成函数

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M2.12 Generating Functions](M2.12.md), [M2.12a Factor Distinctions](M2.12a.md)

---

## Content / 内容

Each colour contributes a generating-function factor according to the permitted numbers:
- red (0 or 1): 1+x
- blue (0,1 or 2): 1+x+x^2
- green (any even non-negative): 1/(1−x^2)
- orange (any positive odd): x/(1−x^2)
- purple (any non-negative multiple of 3): 1/(1−x^3)
The product is the overall generating function; the desired count is the coefficient of x^9 (or whichever power is required). Write the product first, then extract the coefficient by series expansion or by systematic enumeration of the admissible exponent combinations. Distinguish carefully between “at least one” and “non-negative” factors.

每种颜色按允许个数贡献生成函数因子：
- 红（0 或 1）：1+x
- 蓝（0、1 或 2）：1+x+x^2
- 绿（任意非负偶数）：1/(1−x^2)
- 橙（任意正奇数）：x/(1−x^2)
- 紫（任意非负 3 的倍数）：1/(1−x^3)
乘积是总生成函数；所求计数是 x^9（或所需幂次）的系数。先写乘积，再通过级数展开或系统枚举合法指数组合提取系数。仔细区分“至少一个”与“非负”因子。

## Example / 例子

The factor for orange that must be a positive odd number is x/(1−x^2), not 1/(1−x^2).

橙球必须为正奇数的因子是 x/(1−x^2)，不是 1/(1−x^2)。

## Practice Problem / 练习题

Write the generating function for red (0 or 1) and green (any even non-negative) and find the coefficient of x^2.

写出红（0 或 1）与绿（任意非负偶数）的生成函数并求 x^2 的系数。

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

(1+x)/(1−x^2). Coefficient of x^2 is 1 (from the constant term of the first factor times x^2 of the second).

(1+x)/(1−x^2)。x^2 系数为 1。

</details>
