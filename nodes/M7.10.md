# M7.10 Ball-Selection Generating Function / 取球生成函数

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M2.12](M2.12.md), [M2.5](M2.5.md)

**Source anchors**: ordinary GF product for independent colour constraints; coefficient extraction.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Constrained selections of balls (by colour, parity, upper bounds) encode as a **product of generating functions**; the desired count is a coefficient.

按颜色/奇偶/上界等约束的取球，写成**生成函数乘积**，所求个数是某个系数。

---

## Step 1: One colour / 第一步：单色

If colour $i$ may appear $0,1,\ldots,m_i$ times, its factor is $1+x+\cdots+x^{m_i}=(1-x^{m_i+1})/(1-x)$.  
Unlimited: $1/(1-x)$.

---

## Step 2: Product / 第二步：乘积

Independent constraints on colours ⇒ multiply factors. Extract $[x^n]$ of the product for total $n$ balls.

---

## Step 3: Hand check / 第三步：手算

Red 0–2, blue 0–1, total 2: $(1+x+x^2)(1+x)=1+2x+2x^2+x^3$ ⇒ $[x^2]=2$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Each constraint type → one GF factor.  
2. Product encodes independent choices across colours.  
3. Answer is a coefficient, not the whole polynomial.  
4. Unlimited vs capped changes the factor.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Adding GFs instead of multiplying | Independent colours multiply |
| Forgetting the $n$ in $[x^n]$ | Match total count |
| Using exponential GF when order does not matter | OGF for combinations |
