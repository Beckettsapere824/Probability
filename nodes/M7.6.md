# M7.6 Bayes Update of Urn Composition / 瓮组成的贝叶斯更新

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M1.5](M1.5.md), [M4.2](M4.2.md)

**Source anchors**: prior on number of red balls; posterior after observing a colour; predictive next draw.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Prior uncertainty on the urn’s composition (e.g. number of red balls). After drawing a ball, update the prior to a posterior, then predict the next draw.

先验不确定瓮的组成；观察到颜色后更新后验，再预测下一次抽取。

---

## Step 1: Prior and likelihood / 第一步：先验与似然

Let $R=$ number of red in an urn of size $N$. Prior $\Pr(R=r)$. Likelihood $\Pr(\text{red}\mid R=r)=r/N$.

---

## Step 2: Posterior / 第二步：后验

$$\Pr(R=r\mid\text{red})\propto \Pr(\text{red}\mid R=r)\Pr(R=r).$$

---

## Step 3: Predictive / 第三步：预测

$$\Pr(\text{next red}\mid\text{data})=\sum_r\Pr(\text{next red}\mid R=r,\text{data})\Pr(R=r\mid\text{data}).$$

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Composition is a latent discrete parameter.  
2. Bayes: posterior $\propto$ likelihood × prior.  
3. Predictive averages likelihood under the posterior.  
4. Without replacement builds dependence across draws.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Using prior as posterior | Must multiply by likelihood |
| Forgetting to normalise | Divide by $\Pr(\text{data})$ |
| With vs without replacement in predictive | Update counts |

---

## Practice / 练习（选择题）

**1.** Posterior $\Pr(R=r\mid\text{red})$ is proportional to

A. prior only  
B. $(r/N)\times\Pr(R=r)$  
C. $1/r$  
D. $N$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Likelihood × prior.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**2.** Predictive probability of next red averages

A. only the MLE  
B. $r/N$ under the posterior of $R$  
C. the prior mean only without update  
D. $0$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Posterior mixture.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**3.** If prior is uniform on $R$ and one red is seen (with replacement), posterior mass shifts

D. to $r=N$ only with probability 1

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

</details>
