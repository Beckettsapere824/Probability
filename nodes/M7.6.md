# M7.6 Three-Ball Red-White: Binomial Prior + Posterior after a Draw / 三球红白：先验二项+抽球后验

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M4.2 Binomial](M4.2.md), [M5.1 Conditional and Posterior Distributions](M5.1.md), [M5.6 Posterior Prediction](M5.6.md)

---

## Content / 内容

The number X of red balls among three balls follows X~B(3,p). After a red ball is drawn the posterior P(X=i|R) is proportional to P(R|X=i)P(X=i). The probability that the next draw is again red is the posterior predictive expectation E[X/3|R] (or the appropriate remaining total). The computational order is: form the posterior, normalise, then take the conditional expectation. The law of total expectation supplies an independent verification of the numerical result.

三球中红球数 X 服从 X~B(3,p)。抽到一红球后，后验 P(X=i|R) 与 P(R|X=i)P(X=i) 成正比。下一次再抽到红球的概率是后验预测期望 E[X/3|R]（或相应剩余总数）。计算顺序为：形成后验、归一化、再取条件期望。全期望公式提供数值结果的独立验证。

## Example / 例子

After observing a red, the posterior predictive probability of another red is a rational function of p that can be written in closed form.

观察到红球后，再抽到红球的后验预测概率是 p 的有理函数，可写成闭式。

## Practice Problem / 练习题

X~B(2,1/2). One ball is drawn and is red. Find the posterior distribution of X and the predictive probability of a second red (sampling without replacement from the remaining ball).

X~B(2,1/2)。抽一球为红。求 X 的后验分布以及（无放回从剩余球中）第二次为红的预测概率。

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

Likelihood P(R|X=i)=i/2. Posterior ∝ (i/2)·C(2,i)(1/2)^2. After normalisation P(X=1|R)=2/3, P(X=2|R)=1/3. Predictive = (1/2)(2/3)+(1/1)(1/3)=2/3.

似然 P(R|X=i)=i/2。后验 ∝ (i/2)·C(2,i)(1/2)^2。归一化后 P(X=1|R)=2/3，P(X=2|R)=1/3。预测 = (1/2)(2/3)+(1/1)(1/3)=2/3。

</details>
