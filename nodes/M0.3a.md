# M0.3a Binomial Coefficient Boundaries / 组合数边界

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.3](M0.3.md)

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

When is $\binom{n}{k}$ defined as zero so formulas stay clean?

何时把 $\binom{n}{k}$ 视为 0，使公式整齐？

---

## Content in steps / 分步

$\binom{n}{k}=0$ if $k<0$ or $k>n$ (for $n\ge 0$ integer). $\binom{n}{0}=\binom{n}{n}=1$.

---
