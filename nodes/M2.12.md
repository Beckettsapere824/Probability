# M2.12 Ordinary Generating Functions / 普通生成函数

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.5](M0.5.md), [M2.5](M2.5.md)

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Encode counting sequences as coefficients of $\sum a_n x^{n}$.

---

## Practice / 练习

Coefficient of $x^{2}$ in $(1+x+x^{2}/2!+\cdots)^{2}$ relates to counting — extract via series product.
