# M2.12 Ordinary Generating Functions for Counting / 普通生成函数计数

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.5 Sums and Series](M0.5.md), [M2.1 Sampling Models](M2.1.md)

---

## Content / 内容

Each type of constraint corresponds to a factor in the generating function:
- 0 or 1 of an item → 1 + x
- 0 through m of an item → 1 + x + … + x^m
- any even number → 1/(1-x^2)
- any positive odd number → x/(1-x^2)
- any multiple of 3 → 1/(1-x^3)

The product of all factors is the overall generating function; the desired count is the coefficient of x^n. Coefficients may be extracted by the binomial theorem, geometric series, or direct expansion.

每类约束对应生成函数中的一个因子：
- 0 或 1 个 → 1 + x
- 0 到 m 个 → 1 + x + … + x^m
- 任意偶数 → 1/(1-x^2)
- 任意正奇数 → x/(1-x^2)
- 3 的任意倍数 → 1/(1-x^3)

所有因子的乘积是总生成函数；所求计数是 x^n 的系数。系数可用二项式定理、几何级数或直接展开提取。

## Example / 例子

Number of ways to obtain a total of 4 when each of two dice shows at most 3: coefficient of x^4 in (1+x+x^2+x^3)^2.

两枚骰子每枚至多 3 点、总和为 4 的方式数：(1+x+x^2+x^3)^2 中 x^4 的系数。

## Practice Problem / 练习题

Find the coefficient of x^3 in (1+x)^2 (1+x+x^2).

求 (1+x)^2 (1+x+x^2) 中 x^3 的系数。

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

(1+2x+x^2)(1+x+x^2) = … + (1·x^2 + 2x·x + x^2·1) + higher = 1+2+1 = 4 for the x^3 term (more carefully: expand fully).

展开后 x^3 项系数为 4。

</details>
