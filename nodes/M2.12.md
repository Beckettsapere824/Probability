# M2.12 Ordinary Generating Functions / 普通生成函数

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.5](M0.5.md), [M2.5](M2.5.md)

**Source anchors**: $\sum a_n x^n$; product for convolution; coefficient extraction.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Encode a counting sequence $(a_n)$ as the power series $A(x)=\sum_{n\ge0}a_n x^n$. Products of generating functions encode convolutions.

把计数序列编码为幂级数；乘积对应卷积。

---

## Step 1: Definition / 第一步：定义

Ordinary GF (OGF): $A(x)=\sum a_n x^n$. Coefficient $[x^n]A(x)=a_n$.

---

## Step 2: Product / 第二步：乘积

$$A(x)B(x)=\sum_n\Bigl(\sum_{k=0}^{n}a_k b_{n-k}\Bigr)x^n.$$

---

## Step 3: Hand idea / 第三步：思路

Ways to write $n$ as ordered sum of two parts with weights $a,b$ is the convolution coefficient.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. OGF is a formal bookkeeping device; $x$ marks size.  
2. Product ↔ convolution of sequences.  
3. $1/(1-x)=\sum x^n$ for unrestricted number of a type.  
4. Exponential GFs are used when labels matter — different node.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Treating GF as always convergent numerically | Formal series first |
| Using EGF rules on OGF problems | Match the flavour |
| Forgetting $[x^0]$ constant term | $n=0$ counts |
