# M8.5 Gamma and Chi-Squared / Gamma 与卡方分布

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M8.4 Exponential](M8.4.md)

**Source / 来源**: SDST2601A Chapter 5 §5.2.3 Definitions 5.5–5.7

---

## Content / 内容

### Gamma function (Definition 5.5)

\[
\Gamma(\alpha) = \int_0^\infty x^{\alpha-1} e^{-x}\,dx, \quad \alpha > 0.
\]

Properties: \(\Gamma(1)=1\); \(\Gamma(\alpha)=(\alpha-1)\Gamma(\alpha-1)\) for \(\alpha>1\); \(\Gamma(n)=(n-1)!\) for integer \(n\ge 1\); \(\Gamma(1/2)=\sqrt{\pi}\).

### Gamma distribution (Definition 5.6)

\[
f(x) = \begin{cases} \dfrac{\lambda^\alpha}{\Gamma(\alpha)} x^{\alpha-1} e^{-\lambda x} & x>0, \\ 0 & x\le 0, \end{cases}
\quad \alpha>0,\ \lambda>0.
\]

Write \(X \sim \Gamma(\alpha,\lambda)\) (shape–rate). Then

\[
E(X)=\frac{\alpha}{\lambda}, \qquad \mathrm{Var}(X)=\frac{\alpha}{\lambda^2}, \qquad
M_X(t)=\Bigl(\frac{\lambda}{\lambda-t}\Bigr)^\alpha\ (t<\lambda).
\]

Special case: \(\Gamma(1,\lambda)=\mathrm{Exp}(\lambda)\).

### Chi-squared (Definition 5.7 in notes)

If \(Y \sim \Gamma(r/2,\ 1/2)\) for positive integer \(r\), then \(Y \sim \chi^2(r)\) (\(r\) degrees of freedom).

\[
E(Y)=r, \qquad \mathrm{Var}(Y)=2r.
\]

Waiting-time interpretation: sum of \(n\) i.i.d. Exp(\(\lambda\)) interarrival times is \(\Gamma(n,\lambda)\).

---

## Example / 例子

If \(T_8 \sim \Gamma(8,20)\) and \(Y=40 T_8\), the notes identify a chi-squared relation via scale change (shape fixed, rate transformed) — always track whether a problem uses **rate** or **scale** parametrization before reading off means.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

- Shape–rate vs shape–scale: mean is \(\alpha/\lambda\) (rate) or \(\alpha\theta\) (scale \(\theta=1/\lambda\)).
- \(\chi^2(r)\) is **not** “normal squared” without stating i.i.d. standard normals sum of squares.

---

## Practice Problem / 练习题

\(X \sim \Gamma(3,2)\) (rate). Find \(E(X)\), \(\mathrm{Var}(X)\), and \(M_X(t)\) for \(t<2\).

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

\(E=3/2\), \(\mathrm{Var}=3/4\), \(M(t)=(2/(2-t))^3\).

</details>
