# M8.5 Gamma and Chi-Squared / Gamma 与卡方分布

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M8.4 Exponential](M8.4.md)

**Source anchors**: SDST2601A Ch5 Def 5.5–5.7; sum of i.i.d. Exp; shape–rate vs shape–scale; $\chi^{2}$ as special Gamma.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

One exponential clock models a single wait. The time until the $n$-th event in a Poisson process is a **sum** of $n$ i.i.d. exponential interarrival times. That sum is no longer exponential; it is **Gamma**. A special shape–rate choice recovers the **chi-squared** tables used in inference.

一个指数时钟描述一次等待。泊松过程中等到第 $n$ 次事件的时间是 $n$ 个 i.i.d. 指数间隔之和。和不再是指数，而是 **Gamma**。特定 shape–rate 回到推断里的 **卡方** 表。

---

## Step 1: Gamma function / 第一步：Gamma 函数

$$\Gamma(\alpha)=\int_0^{\infty} x^{\alpha-1}e^{-x}\,dx, \qquad \alpha>0.$$

Key identities:

- $\Gamma(1)=1$.  
- $\Gamma(\alpha)=(\alpha-1)\Gamma(\alpha-1)$ for $\alpha>1$.  
- $\Gamma(n)=(n-1)!$ for integer $n\ge 1$.  
- $\Gamma(1/2)=\sqrt{\pi}$.

These are used to normalise the Gamma density and to simplify factorials in continuous problems.

---

## Step 2: Gamma density (rate form) / 第二步：Gamma 密度（rate 形式）

$$f(x)=\begin{cases} \dfrac{\lambda^{\alpha}}{\Gamma(\alpha)} x^{\alpha-1}e^{-\lambda x} & x>0, \\ 0 & x\le 0, \end{cases}$$

with shape $\alpha>0$ and rate $\lambda>0$. Write $X\sim\Gamma(\alpha,\lambda)$.

$$E(X)=\frac{\alpha}{\lambda}, \qquad \mathrm{Var}(X)=\frac{\alpha}{\lambda^{2}}, \qquad M_X(t)=\Bigl(\frac{\lambda}{\lambda-t}\Bigr)^{\alpha}\ (t<\lambda).$$

**Special case:** $\Gamma(1,\lambda)=\mathrm{Exp}(\lambda)$.

**Waiting-time picture:** sum of $n$ i.i.d. $\mathrm{Exp}(\lambda)$ is $\Gamma(n,\lambda)$ (integer shape).

---

## Step 3: Rate vs scale / 第三步：rate 与 scale

Some texts use scale $\theta=1/\lambda$ and write

$$f(x)=\frac{1}{\Gamma(\alpha)\theta^{\alpha}}x^{\alpha-1}e^{-x/\theta}.$$

Then $E(X)=\alpha\theta$, $\mathrm{Var}(X)=\alpha\theta^{2}$.  
**Always check** whether a problem’s second parameter is rate or scale before plugging into mean formulas.

有的书用 scale $\theta=1/\lambda$。代入均值前先分清参数约定。

---

## Step 4: Chi-squared / 第四步：卡方

If $X\sim\Gamma(r/2,\,1/2)$ for positive integer $r$, write $X\sim\chi^{2}(r)$ (chi-squared with $r$ degrees of freedom).

$$E(X)=r, \qquad \mathrm{Var}(X)=2r.$$

Equivalently: if $Z_1,\ldots,Z_r$ are i.i.d. $N(0,1)$, then $\sum_{i=1}^{r}Z_i^{2}\sim\chi^{2}(r)$.  
This sum-of-squares representation is the bridge to sampling distributions in inference.

等价于 $r$ 个独立标准正态平方和。

---

## Step 5: Hand example / 第五步：手算

$X\sim\Gamma(3,2)$ (rate).  
$E(X)=3/2$, $\mathrm{Var}(X)=3/4$, $M_X(t)=(2/(2-t))^{3}$ for $t<2$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Gamma = sum of i.i.d. Exp when shape is integer; generalises to non-integer shape.  
2. Rate $\lambda$ vs scale $\theta=1/\lambda$ — name which one.  
3. $\chi^{2}(r)=\Gamma(r/2,1/2)$ and also = sum of $r$ independent standard-normal squares.  
4. Exp is Gamma with shape 1.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Mixing rate and scale in the mean formula | Rate: $\alpha/\lambda$; scale: $\alpha\theta$ |
| Treating $\chi^{2}(r)$ as a single $Z^{2}$ for $r>1$ | Sum of $r$ squares |
| Forgetting $\Gamma(n)=(n-1)!$ | Integer shape simplifies |
| Applying memorylessness to Gamma with $\alpha\neq 1$ | Only Exp is memoryless |

---

## Worked exam-style example / 考试风格例题

**Problem.** $X\sim\Gamma(3,2)$ (rate). Find $E(X)$, $\mathrm{Var}(X)$, and $M_X(t)$ for $t<2$.

**Solution.** $3/2$, $3/4$, $(2/(2-t))^{3}$.

**Link.** Waiting time until the 3rd event in a rate-2 Poisson process is $\Gamma(3,2)$.

## Practice / 练习

1. $X\sim\Gamma(2,1)$ (rate). $E(X)$ and $\mathrm{Var}(X)$?

2. Express $\chi^{2}(4)$ as a Gamma law (rate form).

3. Why is $\mathrm{Exp}(\lambda)$ the same as $\Gamma(1,\lambda)$?

<details>
<summary>Solution sketches / 解答提示</summary>

1. $E=2$, $\mathrm{Var}=2$.

2. $\Gamma(2,1/2)$.

3. Plug $\alpha=1$ into the Gamma density: recovers $\lambda e^{-\lambda x}$.

</details>
