# M8.5 Gamma and Chi-Squared / Gamma 与卡方分布

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M8.4 Exponential](M8.4.md)

**Source / 来源**: SDST2601A Chapter 5 §5.2.3 Definitions 5.5–5.7

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

One exponential clock models a single wait. The time until the $n$-th event in a Poisson process is a **sum** of $n$ i.i.d. exponential waits. That sum is no longer exponential; it is **Gamma**.

一个指数时钟描述一次等待。泊松过程中等到第 $n$ 次事件的时间是 $n$ 个 i.i.d. 指数等待之和。和不再是指数，而是 **Gamma**。

A special shape–rate choice recovers the **chi-squared** tables used later in inference.

特定的 shape–rate 选择会回到推断里常用的 **卡方** 表。

---

## Step 1: Gamma function (Def 5.5) / 第一步：Gamma 函数

$$
\Gamma(\alpha)=\int_{0}^{\infty} x^{\alpha-1}e^{-x}\,dx, \quad \alpha>0.
$$

$\Gamma(1)=1$; $\Gamma(\alpha)=(\alpha-1)\Gamma(\alpha-1)$ for $\alpha>1$; $\Gamma(n)=(n-1)!$ for integer $n\ge 1$; $\Gamma(1/2)=\sqrt{\pi}$.

---

## Step 2: Gamma density (Def 5.6, rate form) / 第二步：Gamma 密度

$$
f(x)=\begin{cases}
\dfrac{\lambda^{\alpha}}{\Gamma(\alpha)} x^{\alpha-1}e^{-\lambda x} & x>0, \\
0 & x\le 0,
\end{cases}
\qquad \alpha>0,\ \lambda>0.
$$

Write $X\sim\Gamma(\alpha,\lambda)$ (shape $\alpha$, rate $\lambda$). Then

$$
E(X)=\frac{\alpha}{\lambda}, \quad \mathrm{Var}(X)=\frac{\alpha}{\lambda^{2}}, \quad
M_{X}(t)=\Bigl(\frac{\lambda}{\lambda-t}\Bigr)^{\alpha}\ (t<\lambda).
$$

Special case: $\Gamma(1,\lambda)=\mathrm{Exp}(\lambda)$.

Waiting-time picture: sum of $n$ i.i.d. $\mathrm{Exp}(\lambda)$ interarrivals is $\Gamma(n,\lambda)$.

---

## Step 3: Chi-squared (Def 5.7) / 第三步：卡方

If $X\sim\Gamma(r/2,\,1/2)$ for positive integer $r$, write $X\sim\chi^{2}(r)$.

$$
E(X)=r, \qquad \mathrm{Var}(X)=2r.
$$

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

- Shape–rate vs shape–scale: mean is $\alpha/\lambda$ (rate) or $\alpha\theta$ (scale $\theta=1/\lambda$).  
- Treating $\chi^{2}(r)$ as “one normal squared” without the full sum-of-squares story.

---

## Practice / 练习

$X\sim\Gamma(3,2)$ (rate). Find $E(X)$, $\mathrm{Var}(X)$, and $M_{X}(t)$ for $t<2$.

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

$E=3/2$, $\mathrm{Var}=3/4$, $M(t)=(2/(2-t))^{3}$.

</details>
