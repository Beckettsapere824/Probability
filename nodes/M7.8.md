# M7.8 Circuit-Board Testing: Total Probability + Bayes + Negative Binomial / 电路板测试：全概率+贝叶斯+负二项

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M1.4 Total Probability](M1.4.md), [M1.5 Bayes](M1.5.md), [M4.5 Negative Binomial](M4.5.md), [M4.5a Definition Distinction](M4.5a.md), [M4.3 Binomial Tails](M4.3.md)

## Content / 内容

A board is defective with probability θ. A defective board buzzes with probability 0.1, a good board buzzes with probability 0.9 (or the complementary numbers given in the concrete problem). The marginal buzz probability is the total-probability mixture q=θ·0.1+(1−θ)·0.9. The event that the second buzz occurs on the 10th board is a negative-binomial probability. The event of at least two buzzes among ten boards is a binomial complement. The posterior probability that a buzzing board is defective is obtained by Bayes. Solving a numerical threshold for θ yields the concrete value 81/82 in the original data set. Always write the four conditional probabilities P(B|D), P(B|D'), P(B'|D), P(B'|D') explicitly; the conditional complement P(B|D')=1−θ appears when the problem uses that parametrisation.

电路板以概率 θ 为次品。次品板以概率 0.1 buzz，合格板以概率 0.9 buzz（或题目给出的互补数字）。边际 buzz 概率是全概率混合 q=θ·0.1+(1−θ)·0.9。第 10 块板出现第二次 buzz 的事件是负二项概率。十块板中至少两次 buzz 的事件是二项补。buzz 板为次品的后验概率由贝叶斯得到。对 θ 解数值阈值在原始数据中得到具体值 81/82。始终显式写出四个条件概率 P(B|D)、P(B|D')、P(B'|D)、P(B'|D')；当题目采用该参数化时出现条件补 P(B|D')=1−θ。

## Example / 例子

The marginal q is linear in θ. Setting a posterior equal to a threshold and clearing the denominator produces a linear equation for θ.

边际 q 对 θ 线性。令后验等于阈值并去分母，得到 θ 的线性方程。

## Practice Problem / 练习题

If P(B|D)=0.1, P(B|D')=0.9 and the posterior P(D|B) is required to be at least 1/2, find the minimal θ.

若 P(B|D)=0.1，P(B|D')=0.9 且要求后验 P(D|B) 至少为 1/2，求最小 θ。

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

P(D|B)=0.1θ/(0.1θ+0.9(1−θ))≥1/2 → 0.1θ≥0.9−0.9θ → θ≥0.9.

P(D|B)=0.1θ/(0.1θ+0.9(1−θ))≥1/2 → 0.1θ≥0.9−0.9θ → θ≥0.9。

</details>
