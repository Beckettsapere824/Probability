# M3.9 Moment Generating Function / 矩母函数

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M3.1](M3.1.md), [M0.6](M0.6.md)

**Source anchors**: $M_X(t)=E(e^{tX})$; $E(X^{r})=M^{(r)}(0)$.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Package moments into $M_X(t)=E(e^{tX})$. Derivatives at 0 recover moments; product of MGFs for independent sums.

用 $M_X(t)=E(e^{tX})$ 打包矩；独立和的 MGF 相乘。

---

## Step 1: Definition / 第一步：定义

$$M_X(t)=E(e^{tX})$$
near $t=0$ when finite.

---

## Step 2: Moments / 第二步：矩

$M(0)=1$, $M'(0)=E(X)$, $M''(0)=E(X^{2})$.

---

## Step 3: Examples / 第三步：例子

Bernoulli($p$): $M(t)=1-p+pe^{t}$. Independent ⇒ $M_{X+Y}=M_X M_Y$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Not every distribution has an MGF near 0.  
2. MGF determines the law when it exists in an open interval.  
3. Independent sums ↔ product of MGFs.  
4. Chernoff bounds use $E(e^{tX})$.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Differentiating under $E$ without justification | Need neighbourhood conditions |
| Assuming every law has an MGF | Heavy tails may fail |
| Product of MGFs without independence | Hypothesis |
