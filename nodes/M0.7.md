## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Bounds and triangle inequality appear in concentration and metric arguments.

---

## Facts / 要点

$|a+b|\le|a|+|b|$; $|x|\ge 0$; solving $|x-a|<c$ as interval $(a-c,a+c)$.
