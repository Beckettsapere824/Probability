# M7.9 Bridge Network Probability / 桥式网络

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M5.4](M5.4.md)

**Source anchors**: condition on the bridge edge; series-parallel decomposition.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

A network with a **bridge** edge is neither pure series nor pure parallel. Condition on whether the bridge works to split the reliability calculation into simpler cases.

桥边既非纯串联也非纯并联；对桥边是否工作条件化，拆成简单情形。

---

## Step 1: Condition on the bridge / 第一步：对桥条件化

Let $B$ be the bridge event (edge up).  
$\Pr(\text{source–sink connected})=\Pr(\text{conn}\mid B)\Pr(B)+\Pr(\text{conn}\mid B^{c})\Pr(B^{c})$.

---

## Step 2: Each branch is series/parallel / 第二步：各支为串并联

Given $B$, remaining structure often reduces to parallel of two series paths. Given $B^{c}$, read the residual graph.

---

## Step 3: Independence of edges / 第三步：边独立

Usually edges fail independently; multiply along paths; use inclusion carefully for unions of paths that share edges.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Bridge ⇒ condition, do not force a single series/parallel formula.  
2. Total probability over the bridge state.  
3. Edge independence makes path probabilities products.  
4. Draw the two residual graphs after fixing the bridge.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Treating the whole graph as parallel of all paths without IE | Paths share edges |
| Forgetting $\Pr(B^{c})$ term | Full total probability |
| Assuming connectivity when bridge is down without checking | Residual graph |

---

## Practice / 练习（选择题）

**1.** Reliability with a bridge is handled by

A. ignoring the bridge  
B. conditioning on the bridge state  
C. only min-cut with no probability  
D. $\pi P=\pi$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Total probability on the bridge.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**2.** $\Pr(\text{conn})=$

A. $\Pr(\text{conn}\mid B)$ only  
B. $\Pr(\text{conn}\mid B)\Pr(B)+\Pr(\text{conn}\mid B^{c})\Pr(B^{c})$  
C. $\Pr(B)$  
D. $1$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Law of total probability.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**3.** Independent edges mean path probability is

A. sum of edge probs  
B. product of edge up-probabilities along the path  
C. max edge prob  
D. $1/2$ always

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Independence.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**4.** When the bridge is down, connectivity

A. is always preserved  
B. depends on residual paths  
C. is always lost  
D. equals $\Pr(B)$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Read residual graph.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**5.** Series of two edges up-probability

A. $p_1+p_2$  
B. $p_1p_2$  
C. $1-(1-p_1)(1-p_2)$  
D. $\min(p_1,p_2)$ only as bound

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Both must work.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**6.** Parallel of two independent edges up-probability

A. $p_1p_2$

C. $p_1-p_2$  
D. $0$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

At least one works.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

Dependence of path indicators.

</details>
