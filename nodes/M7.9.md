# M7.9 Bridge Network Conditional Probability / 桥网络条件概率

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M5.4 Network Reliability](M5.4.md)

---

## Content / 内容

The elementary connecting configurations are: bridge 3 open, or the upper path (bridges 1 and 4 both open), or the lower path (bridges 2 and 5 both open). Under independence the probability of each configuration is the product of the individual open probabilities. The conditional probability that bridge 3 is open given East-West connectivity equals P(bridge 3 open and connected)/P(connected). The denominator must include every path that realises connectivity; complement arguments often simplify the algebra.

基本连通配置为：桥 3 开启，或上路径（桥 1 与 4 都开启），或下路径（桥 2 与 5 都开启）。独立下每种配置的概率是各桥开启概率的乘积。给定东西连通时桥 3 开启的条件概率等于 P(桥 3 开且连通)/P(连通)。分母必须包含实现连通的所有路径；补事件常可简化代数。

## Example / 例子

With equal open probability p the connectivity probability is obtained by inclusion-exclusion on the three path events.

各桥开启概率同为 p 时，连通概率通过对三路径事件做包含排除得到。

## Practice Problem / 练习题

Three independent bridges in parallel, each open with probability 1/2. Find P(bridge 1 open | at least one bridge open).

三座独立并联桥，各以概率 1/2 开启。求 P(桥 1 开 | 至少一桥开)。

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

P(bridge 1 open and at least one open)=P(bridge 1 open)=1/2. P(at least one open)=1−(1/2)^3=7/8. Conditional probability (1/2)/(7/8)=4/7.

P(桥 1 开且至少一开)=P(桥 1 开)=1/2。P(至少一开)=1−(1/2)^3=7/8。条件概率 (1/2)/(7/8)=4/7。

</details>
