# M0.2 Product and Sum Rules / 乘法原理与加法原理

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.1](M0.1.md)

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

How many ways to form a sequence of choices, or to choose among disjoint options?

多步选择有多少种方式？互斥方案如何合计？

---

## Step 1: Product rule / 乘法原理

If task 1 has $n_1$ ways and task 2 has $n_2$ ways (independent of the first), total $n_1 n_2$.

---

## Step 2: Sum rule / 加法原理

If options are disjoint, add the counts.

---

## Practice / 练习

3 shirts, 2 pants. Outfits? Password 2 letters then 1 digit (26 letters, 10 digits)?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>$3\cdot 2=6$; $26^{2}\cdot 10$.</details>
