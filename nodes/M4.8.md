# M4.8 Logarithmic Series Distribution / 对数级数分布

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.5](M0.5.md), [M3.9](M3.9.md)

**Source anchors**: PMF $\propto \alpha^{k}/k$ on $\{1,2,\ldots\}$; specialized discrete family.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

A less common discrete law on positive integers with weights $\alpha^{k}/k$. Core exam track prioritises binomial/Poisson/geometric/hypergeometric — treat as reference.

支撑在正整数上、权重 $\propto\alpha^{k}/k$ 的专用离散族；核心考试以二项/泊松/几何/超几何为主。

---

## Step 1: PMF / 第一步：PMF

For $0<\alpha<1$,
$$p(k)=\frac{-\alpha^{k}}{k\log(1-\alpha)},\quad k=1,2,\ldots.$$

---

## Step 2: Mean / 第二步：期望

$$E(X)=\frac{\alpha}{-(1-\alpha)\log(1-\alpha)}.$$

---

## Step 3: Scope / 第三步：范围

Know the name and the series identity; deep calculations optional unless assigned.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Built from the Taylor series of $-\log(1-\alpha)$.  
2. Support starts at 1 (no zero mass).  
3. Not a default exam model unless named.  
4. Parameter $\alpha$ controls tail heaviness.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Using it when binomial was intended | Read the problem family |
| Forgetting the normalising $-\log(1-\alpha)$ | Series identity |
| Allowing $k=0$ | Support starts at 1 |
