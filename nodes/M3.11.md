# M3.11 Law of Total Expectation and Total Variance (Adam & Eve) / 全期望与全方差

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M3.1 Expectation](M3.1.md), [M3.3 Variance](M3.3.md), [M1.3 Conditional](M1.3.md)

**Source anchors**: SDST2601A Ch8 Theorems 8.1–8.2 (Adam’s Law, Eve’s Law); urn reinforcement; mixture models; prediction error decomposition.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Sometimes $X$ is hard to average directly, but **given** another variable $Y$ the conditional mean $E(X\mid Y)$ is simple. Total expectation rebuilds $E(X)$ from those conditional pieces. Total variance splits $\mathrm{Var}(X)$ into “average noise inside each slice” plus “how much the slice means jump”.

有时 $X$ 不好直接平均，但**给定** $Y$ 后条件均值很简单。全期望用条件块拼回 $E(X)$。全方差把 $\mathrm{Var}(X)$ 拆成「切片内平均噪声」+「切片均值的跳动」。

---

## Step 1: Conditional expectation as a random variable / 第一步：条件期望是随机变量

$E(X\mid Y=y)$ is a number (the average of $X$ on the slice $Y=y$).  
$E(X\mid Y)$ is the **random variable** that takes the value $E(X\mid Y=y)$ when $Y=y$.

Mixing the two is a common source of algebra errors: one is a number, the other is a function of $Y$.

$E(X\mid Y=y)$ 是数；$E(X\mid Y)$ 是随机变量（在 $Y=y$ 时取值 $E(X\mid Y=y)$）。二者混用是常见代数错误。

---

## Step 2: Adam’s Law (total expectation) / 第二步：全期望（Adam）

$$E(X)=E\bigl[E(X\mid Y)\bigr].$$

Discrete: $E(X)=\sum_y E(X\mid Y=y)\Pr(Y=y)$.  
Continuous: $E(X)=\int E(X\mid Y=y)f_Y(y)\,dy$.

More generally $E[u(X)]=E\bigl\{E[u(X)\mid Y]\bigr\}$ for suitable $u$.

**Why it works:** partition the sample space by the values of $Y$, average $X$ inside each cell, then average those cell averages with the weights $\Pr(Y=y)$.

按 $Y$ 的取值划分样本空间，先在每个格子内平均 $X$，再用 $\Pr(Y=y)$ 平均这些格子均值。

---

## Step 3: Eve’s Law (total variance) / 第三步：全方差（Eve）

$$\mathrm{Var}(X)=E\bigl[\mathrm{Var}(X\mid Y)\bigr]+\mathrm{Var}\bigl[E(X\mid Y)\bigr].$$

- $E[\mathrm{Var}(X\mid Y)]$ = average **within-slice** variance (noise you cannot remove by knowing $Y$).  
- $\mathrm{Var}[E(X\mid Y)]$ = variance of the **slice means** (spread explained by $Y$).

This is the same decomposition as ANOVA / law of total variance in prediction: total error = irreducible noise + variance of the predictor $E(X\mid Y)$.

第一项：知道 $Y$ 后仍剩的平均噪声；第二项：$Y$ 能解释的切片均值波动。与预测误差分解同一结构。

---

## Step 4: Hand numbers / 第四步：数字手算

$E(X\mid Y=1)=3$, $E(X\mid Y=2)=5$, $\Pr(Y=1)=0.4$, $\Pr(Y=2)=0.6$.  
$E(X)=3\cdot 0.4+5\cdot 0.6=4.2$.

Also $\mathrm{Var}(X\mid Y=1)=2$, $\mathrm{Var}(X\mid Y=2)=4$. Then

$$E[\mathrm{Var}(X\mid Y)]=2\cdot 0.4+4\cdot 0.6=3.2,$$

$$\mathrm{Var}[E(X\mid Y)]=E\bigl[(E(X\mid Y))^{2}\bigr]-(EX)^{2}=9\cdot 0.4+25\cdot 0.6-4.2^{2}=0.96,$$

$$\mathrm{Var}(X)=3.2+0.96=4.16.$$

---

## Step 5: Classic urn (exam style) / 第五步：经典瓮

Start with 1 red, 1 blue. Draw one; put it back **together with an extra ball of the same colour**. Let $X=1$ if the second draw is red.

Condition on the first colour $Y$:

- First red (prob $1/2$): then 2 red, 1 blue → $E(X\mid\text{first red})=2/3$.  
- First blue (prob $1/2$): then 1 red, 2 blue → $E(X\mid\text{first blue})=1/3$.

Adam: $E(X)=\tfrac12\cdot\tfrac23+\tfrac12\cdot\tfrac13=\tfrac12$.

(The unconditional probability the second is red remains $1/2$ — a martingale-type conservation that exams like.)

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. $E(X\mid Y)$ is a random variable; $E(X\mid Y=y)$ is a number.  
2. Adam: average the conditional averages.  
3. Eve: total Var = average conditional Var + Var of conditional means.  
4. Neither law requires independence of $X$ and $Y$.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Using only $E[\mathrm{Var}(X\mid Y)]$ for $\mathrm{Var}(X)$ | Must add $\mathrm{Var}[E(X\mid Y)]$ |
| Thinking Adam/Eve need independence | They do not |
| Treating $E(X\mid Y)$ as a number in algebra | It is a function of $Y$ |
| Forgetting to square when computing $\mathrm{Var}[E(X\mid Y)]$ | $E[m(Y)^{2}]-(Em)^{2}$ |

---

## Worked exam-style example / 考试风格例题

**Problem.** $Y\sim\mathrm{Bernoulli}(1/3)$. Given $Y=0$, $X\sim\mathrm{Po}(2)$; given $Y=1$, $X\sim\mathrm{Po}(5)$. Find $E(X)$ and $\mathrm{Var}(X)$.

**Solution.**  
$E(X\mid Y)=2\cdot(1-Y)+5Y=2+3Y$, so $E(X)=2+3\cdot(1/3)=3$.  
$\mathrm{Var}(X\mid Y)=E(X\mid Y)$ (Poisson), so $E[\mathrm{Var}(X\mid Y)]=3$.  
$\mathrm{Var}[E(X\mid Y)]=\mathrm{Var}(2+3Y)=9\cdot\mathrm{Var}(Y)=9\cdot(1/3)(2/3)=2$.  
$\mathrm{Var}(X)=3+2=5$.

## Practice / 练习

1. Urn 1 red, 1 blue; replace with extra same colour. $E(\text{second is red})$ by conditioning on first?

2. In Eve’s law, which term is zero if $X$ is a function of $Y$ only?

3. Why can $E[\mathrm{Var}(X\mid Y)]$ be smaller than $\mathrm{Var}(X)$?

<details>
<summary>Solution sketches / 解答提示</summary>

1. $1/2$ (as in Step 5).

2. $E[\mathrm{Var}(X\mid Y)]=0$ (no residual noise).

3. Because $\mathrm{Var}[E(X\mid Y)]\ge 0$ takes some of the total variance.

</details>
