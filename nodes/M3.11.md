# M3.11 Law of Total Expectation and Total Variance / 全期望与全方差（Adam & Eve）

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M3.1 Expectation](M3.1.md), [M1.3 Conditional Probability](M1.3.md)

**Source / 来源**: SDST2601A Chapter 8 Theorems 8.1–8.2 (Adam’s Law, Eve’s Law)

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Sometimes $X$ is hard to average directly, but **given** another variable $Y$, the conditional mean $E(X\mid Y)$ is simple. Total expectation rebuilds $E(X)$ from those conditional pieces. Total variance splits $\mathrm{Var}(X)$ into “average conditional variance” plus “variance of conditional means”.

有时 $X$ 不好直接求平均，但**给定** $Y$ 后条件均值 $E(X\mid Y)$ 很简单。全期望用这些条件块拼回 $E(X)$。全方差把 $\mathrm{Var}(X)$ 拆成「平均条件方差」+「条件均值的方差」。

---

## Step 1: Adam’s Law (Theorem 8.1) / 第一步：全期望

For a (suitable) function $u$,

$$E[u(X)] = E\bigl\{E[u(X)\mid Y]\bigr\}.$$

In particular,

$$E(X)=E\bigl[E(X\mid Y)\bigr].$$

Discrete picture: $E(X)=\sum_y E(X\mid Y=y)\Pr(Y=y)$. Continuous: integrate against $f_Y$.

---

## Step 2: Eve’s Law (Theorem 8.2) / 第二步：全方差

$$\mathrm{Var}(X)=E\bigl[\mathrm{Var}(X\mid Y)\bigr]+\mathrm{Var}\bigl[E(X\mid Y)\bigr].$$

- First term: average **noise** inside each slice $Y=y$.  
- Second term: how much the **slice means** jump as $Y$ varies.

第一项：每个 $Y=y$ 切片内部的平均噪声；第二项：切片均值随 $Y$ 跳动的幅度。

---

## Step 3: Hand numbers / 第三步：数字手算

$E(X\mid Y=1)=3$, $E(X\mid Y=2)=5$, $\Pr(Y=1)=0.4$, $\Pr(Y=2)=0.6$.  
Then $E(X)=3\cdot 0.4+5\cdot 0.6=4.2$.

If also $\mathrm{Var}(X\mid Y=1)=2$, $\mathrm{Var}(X\mid Y=2)=4$, then

$$E[\mathrm{Var}(X\mid Y)]=2\cdot 0.4+4\cdot 0.6=3.2,$$
$$\mathrm{Var}[E(X\mid Y)]=E[(E(X\mid Y))^{2}]-(E X)^{2}=3^{2}\cdot 0.4+5^{2}\cdot 0.6-4.2^{2}=0.96,$$
$$\mathrm{Var}(X)=3.2+0.96=4.16.$$

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

- Using only $E[\mathrm{Var}(X\mid Y)]$ and forgetting $\mathrm{Var}[E(X\mid Y)]$.  
- Thinking Adam/Eve need independence (they do not).  
- Mixing $E(X\mid Y=y)$ (number) with $E(X\mid Y)$ (random variable).

---

## Practice / 练习

Urn: 1 red, 1 blue. Draw one, replace it together with an extra ball of the same colour. Let $X=1$ if the second draw is red. Find $E(X)$ by conditioning on the first draw.

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

$E(X\mid\text{first red})=2/3$, $E(X\mid\text{first blue})=1/3$, each first colour has probability $1/2$, so $E(X)=1/2$.

</details>
