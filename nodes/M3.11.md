# M3.11 Conditional Expectation and the Law of Total Expectation / 条件期望与全期望公式

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M3.1 Expectation](M3.1.md), [M1.3 Conditional Probability](M1.3.md), [M1.8 Random Variables](M1.8.md)

---

## Content / 内容

The conditional expectation E(X|Y=y) is the ordinary expectation computed under the conditional distribution of X given Y=y. The law of total expectation asserts E(X) = E[E(X|Y)] = Σ_y E(X|Y=y) P(Y=y). The same identity underpins posterior predictive calculations: P(R_2|R_1) = E[P(R_2|X,R_1)|R_1].

条件期望 E(X|Y=y) 是在给定 Y=y 的条件分布下计算的普通期望。全期望公式断言 E(X) = E[E(X|Y)] = Σ_y E(X|Y=y) P(Y=y)。同一恒等式支撑后验预测计算：P(R_2|R_1) = E[P(R_2|X,R_1)|R_1]。

Conditional expectation is itself a random variable E(X|Y); taking its expectation recovers E(X). The law does not require independence and is used for Bayesian posterior prediction, weighted averages and conditional comparisons.

条件期望本身是随机变量 E(X|Y)；再取期望即得 E(X)。全期望公式不需要独立，用于贝叶斯后验预测、加权平均与条件比较。

## Example / 例子

E(X|Y=1) = 3, E(X|Y=2) = 5, P(Y=1)=0.4. Then E(X) = 3·0.4 + 5·0.6 = 4.2.

E(X|Y=1) = 3，E(X|Y=2) = 5，P(Y=1)=0.4。则 E(X) = 3·0.4 + 5·0.6 = 4.2。

## Practice Problem / 练习题

An urn initially contains 1 red and 1 blue ball. A ball is drawn and replaced together with an extra ball of the same colour. Let X be the colour of the second draw (1 = red). Find E(X).

瓮中最初 1 红 1 蓝。抽一球并连同同色额外一球放回。设 X 为第二次抽取的颜色（1 = 红）。求 E(X)。

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

Condition on the first draw. E(X|first red) = 2/3, E(X|first blue) = 1/3. Each first colour has probability 1/2, so E(X) = 1/2.

以第一次抽取为条件。E(X|第一次红) = 2/3，E(X|第一次蓝) = 1/3。第一次各色概率 1/2，故 E(X) = 1/2。

</details>
