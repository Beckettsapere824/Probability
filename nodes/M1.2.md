# M1.2 Complement, Union, Inclusion-Exclusion / 补事件与包含排除

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M1.1 Sample Space, Events, Probability Axioms](M1.1.md)

**Source / 来源**: SDST2601A Chapter 3 §3.1.3.1 (inclusion-exclusion)

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Many exam events are phrased as “at least one …” or “A or B”. Computing them by listing can explode; complement and inclusion-exclusion turn them into additions you already know.

很多题是「至少一个…」或「A 或 B」。枚举会爆炸；补事件与包含排除把它们变成你会的加法。

---

## Step 1: Complement / 第一步：补事件

$$\Pr(A^{c})=1-\Pr(A).$$

Standard move: $\Pr(\text{at least one})=1-\Pr(\text{none})$.

标准手法：$\Pr(\text{至少一个})=1-\Pr(\text{全无})$。

---

## Step 2: Two- and three-event inclusion-exclusion / 第二步：包含排除

$$\Pr(A\cup B)=\Pr(A)+\Pr(B)-\Pr(A\cap B).$$

$$\Pr(A\cup B\cup C)=\sum\Pr(A_{i})-\sum\Pr(A_{i}\cap A_{j})+\Pr(A\cap B\cap C).$$

Subtract intersections so double-counted outcomes are not paid twice.

减去交，避免重叠部分被加两次。

---

## Step 3: Hand numbers / 第三步：数字

$\Pr(A)=0.3$, $\Pr(B)=0.4$, $\Pr(A\cap B)=0.1$.  
$\Pr(A\cup B)=0.6$, $\Pr(A^{c}\cap B^{c})=1-0.6=0.4$.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

- Forgetting to subtract $\Pr(A\cap B)$.  
- Using complement on the wrong “none” event.  
- Applying $|A|/|\Omega|$ when the hard part was defining $A$ correctly.

---

## Practice / 练习

$\Pr(A)=0.5$, $\Pr(B)=0.4$, $\Pr(A\cup B)=0.7$. Find $\Pr(A\cap B)$ and $\Pr(A^{c}\cup B^{c})$.

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

$\Pr(A\cap B)=0.5+0.4-0.7=0.2$.  
$\Pr(A^{c}\cup B^{c})=1-\Pr(A\cap B)=0.8$.

</details>
