# M1.2 Complement, Union, Inclusion-Exclusion / 补事件与包含排除

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M1.1 Sample Space, Events, Probability Axioms](M1.1.md)

**Source anchors**: SDST2601A Ch3 inclusion-exclusion; “at least one” via complement.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Many exam events are “at least one …” or “A or B or C”. **Complement** and **inclusion-exclusion** turn them into additions and subtractions of probabilities you already know.

很多题是「至少一个…」或「A 或 B 或 C」。**补事件**与**包含排除**把它们变成已知概率的加减。

---

## Step 1: Complement / 第一步：补事件

$$\Pr(A^{c})=1-\Pr(A).$$

**Standard move:** $\Pr(\text{at least one success})=1-\Pr(\text{none})$.

---

## Step 2: Two-event inclusion-exclusion / 第二步：两事件

$$\Pr(A\cup B)=\Pr(A)+\Pr(B)-\Pr(A\cap B).$$

Subtract once to undo double-counting of $A\cap B$.

---

## Step 3: Three events / 第三步：三事件

$$\Pr(A\cup B\cup C)=\sum\Pr(A_i)-\sum\Pr(A_i\cap A_j)+\Pr(A\cap B\cap C).$$

Pattern: $+\;-\;+$ over intersection order.

---

## Step 4: De Morgan / 第四步：德摩根

$$(A\cup B)^{c}=A^{c}\cap B^{c},\qquad (A\cap B)^{c}=A^{c}\cup B^{c}.$$

---

## Step 5: Hand numbers / 第五步：数字

$\Pr(A)=0.3$, $\Pr(B)=0.4$, $\Pr(A\cap B)=0.1$ ⇒ $\Pr(A\cup B)=0.6$, neither $=0.4$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. “At least one” → complement of “none”.  
2. Inclusion-exclusion corrects double-counting.  
3. De Morgan swaps unions/intersections under complement.  
4. Independent “none” often multiplies.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Forgetting to subtract $\Pr(A\cap B)$ | Always IE for unions |
| Complement of the wrong event | Write “none” carefully |
| IE signs wrong for 3+ events | $+\;-\;+$ pattern |

## Practice / 练习（选择题）

**1.** $\Pr(A)=0.3$, $\Pr(B)=0.5$, independent. $\Pr(A\cup B)=$

A. $0.8$  
B. $0.65$  
C. $0.15$  
D. $0.5$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$0.3+0.5-0.15=0.65$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**2.** $\Pr(\text{at least one head in 3 fair coins})=$

A. $1/2$  
B. $7/8$  
C. $1/8$  
D. $3/4$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$1-(1/2)^{3}=7/8$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**3.** $\Pr(A\cup B)=\Pr(A)+\Pr(B)-\Pr(A\cap B)$ corrects

A. independence  
B. double-counting of $A\cap B$  
C. empty set  
D. complement only

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Intersection counted twice before subtraction.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**4.** $\Pr(A)=0.5$, $\Pr(B)=0.4$, $\Pr(A\cup B)=0.7$. $\Pr(A\cap B)=$

A. $0.9$  
B. $0.2$  
C. $0.3$  
D. $0.1$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$0.5+0.4-0.7=0.2$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**5.** $\Pr(A^{c}\cup B^{c})$ equals

A. $\Pr(A\cup B)$  
B. $1-\Pr(A\cap B)$  
C. $\Pr(A)\Pr(B)$  
D. $0$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

De Morgan + complement.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**6.** In three-event IE, the triple intersection term has sign

A. $-$  
B. $+$  
C. $0$  
D. depends on independence

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$+\;-\;+$ pattern.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**7.** $\Pr(A)=0.3$, $\Pr(B)=0.4$, $\Pr(A\cap B)=0.1$. $\Pr(\text{neither})=$

A. $0.6$  
B. $0.4$  
C. $0.7$  
D. $0.1$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$1-0.6=0.4$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**8.** “At least one success” is best computed as

A. sum of all success counts only  
B. $1-\Pr(\text{none})$  
C. $\Pr(A)+\Pr(B)$ always  
D. $\Pr(A\cap B)$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Complement is usually shorter.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>
