# M1.7 Mutual Exclusivity, Exhaustiveness and Compatibility / 互斥、穷尽、等可能及其相容性

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M1.2 Complement, Union, Inclusion-Exclusion](M1.2.md), [M1.6 Independence](M1.6.md)

---

## Content / 内容

Mutual exclusivity means P(A ∩ B) = 0. Exhaustiveness means A ∪ B = Ω (or a larger collection whose union is Ω). Equal likelihood means P(A) = P(B).

互斥：P(A ∩ B) = 0。穷尽：A ∪ B = Ω（或更大集合的并集为 Ω）。等可能：P(A) = P(B)。

Independence together with mutual exclusivity forces at least one of the probabilities to be zero. Mutual exclusivity together with exhaustiveness is the definition of a complementary pair (A and A^c). Independence together with exhaustiveness yields the equation P(A) + P(B) - P(A)P(B) = 1.

独立且互斥则至少一个概率为零。互斥且穷尽就是互补对（A 与 A^c）。独立且穷尽得到方程 P(A) + P(B) - P(A)P(B) = 1。

These compatibility checks prevent contradictory assumptions when a problem statement mixes several of the adjectives “independent”, “disjoint”, “exhaustive”, “equiprobable”.

这些相容性检查防止题目把“独立”“互斥”“穷尽”“等可能”混用时出现矛盾假设。

## Example / 例子

A and A^c are always mutually exclusive and exhaustive. They are independent only if one of them has probability 0 or 1.

A 与 A^c 永远互斥且穷尽。它们独立仅当其中一个概率为 0 或 1。

## Practice Problem / 练习题

Can two events with positive probability be both independent and mutually exclusive? Can three events be pairwise independent yet not mutually independent? Give brief reasons.

两个正概率事件能否既独立又互斥？三个事件能否两两独立却不相互独立？简要说明理由。

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

No: independence would require P(A ∩ B) = P(A)P(B) > 0 while mutual exclusivity requires the intersection probability to be zero. Yes: classic counter-examples exist (two independent fair bits and their XOR).

不能：独立要求交概率 = 乘积 > 0，互斥要求交概率 = 0。能：存在经典反例（两个独立公平比特及其异或）。

</details>
