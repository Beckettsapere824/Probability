# M1.7 Mutually Exclusive vs Exhaustive / 互斥与穷尽

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M1.1](M1.1.md)

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Partitions need disjoint (exclusive) pieces that cover $\Omega$ (exhaustive).

划分需要互斥且覆盖全集。

---

## Steps / 分步

- Mutually exclusive: pairwise empty intersections.  
- Exhaustive: union = $\Omega$.  
- Partition = both. Used in total probability.
