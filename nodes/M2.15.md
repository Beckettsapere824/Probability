# M2.15 Multinomial Coefficients / 多项式系数

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M2.6](M2.6.md)

**Source anchors**: $\binom{n}{n_1,\ldots,n_k}=n!/(n_1!\cdots n_k!)$; multinomial theorem.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Coefficient of $x_1^{n_1}\cdots x_k^{n_k}$ in $(x_1+\cdots+x_k)^n$, and ways to divide $n$ distinct objects into labeled groups of sizes $n_1,\ldots,n_k$.

---

## Step 1: Formula / 第一步：公式

$$\binom{n}{n_1,\ldots,n_k}=\frac{n!}{n_1!\cdots n_k!},\quad \sum n_i=n.$$

---

## Step 2: Multinomial theorem / 第二步：多项式定理

$$(x_1+\cdots+x_k)^n=\sum\binom{n}{n_1,\ldots,n_k}x_1^{n_1}\cdots x_k^{n_k}.$$

---

## Step 3: Hand check / 第三步：手算

$\binom{6}{2,2,2}=90$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Generalises $\binom{n}{k}$.  
2. Groups are labeled.  
3. Sequential product of binomials.  
4. Identical groups need extra division.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Forgetting $\sum n_i=n$ | Constraint |
| Using when groups identical without ÷ | Overcount |
| Missing factorials in denominator | Each $n_i!$ |

---
