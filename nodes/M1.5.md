# M1.5 Bayes’ Theorem / 贝叶斯公式

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M1.3 Conditional Probability](M1.3.md), [M1.4 Law of Total Probability](M1.4.md)

**Source / 来源**: SDST2601A Chapter 3 Theorems 3.4–3.5

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

You observe data $B$ and want the updated probability of a cause $A_i$. Bayes flips $\Pr(B\mid A_i)$ (often easy from a model) into $\Pr(A_i\mid B)$ using the prior and total probability.

你观测到数据 $B$，想更新某原因 $A_i$ 的概率。贝叶斯用先验与全概率，把模型里好算的 $\Pr(B\mid A_i)$ 翻成 $\Pr(A_i\mid B)$。

---

## Step 1: Formula / 第一步：公式

$$\Pr(A_i\mid B)=\frac{\Pr(B\mid A_i)\Pr(A_i)}{\sum_j \Pr(B\mid A_j)\Pr(A_j)}=\frac{\Pr(B\mid A_i)\Pr(A_i)}{\Pr(B)}.$$

Numerator: prior × likelihood. Denominator: marginal probability of the data.

分子：先验 × 似然。分母：数据的边际概率。

---

## Step 2: Base rates matter / 第二步：基础率很重要

Same likelihoods, tiny prior → modest posterior. Ignoring the prior is the **base-rate fallacy**.

同样似然、极小先验 → 后验仍可能不高。忽略先验就是**基础率谬误**。

Disease example: $\Pr(D\mid +)=(0.95\cdot 0.01)/0.1085\approx 0.0876$ — only about 9% of positive tests are true disease under these numbers.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

- Reporting $\Pr(B\mid A)$ when the question asked $\Pr(A\mid B)$.  
- Dropping the prior.  
- Denominator not equal to total probability of $B$.

---

## Practice / 练习

Prior $\Pr(D)=0.02$. $\Pr(+\mid D)=0.9$, $\Pr(+\mid D^{c})=0.05$. Find $\Pr(D\mid +)$.

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

$\Pr(+)=0.9\cdot 0.02+0.05\cdot 0.98=0.067$.  
$\Pr(D\mid +)=0.018/0.067\approx 0.269$.

</details>
