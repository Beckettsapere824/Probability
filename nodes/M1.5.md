# M1.5 Bayes’ Theorem / 贝叶斯公式

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M1.3 Conditional Probability](M1.3.md), [M1.4 Law of Total Probability](M1.4.md)

**Source anchors**: SDST2601A Ch3; medical diagnosis; base-rate fallacy; odds form.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

You observe data $B$ (positive test, submitted homework) and want the updated probability of a hidden cause $A_{i}$. The model usually gives $\Pr(B\mid A_{i})$ easily; Bayes turns that into $\Pr(A_{i}\mid B)$ using the prior and total probability.

观测到数据 $B$，想更新隐藏原因 $A_{i}$ 的概率。模型常容易给出 $\Pr(B\mid A_{i})$；贝叶斯用先验与全概率翻成 $\Pr(A_{i}\mid B)$。

---

## Step 1: Derive Bayes from definitions / 第一步：从定义推出

$$\Pr(A_{i}\mid B)=\frac{\Pr(B\mid A_{i})\Pr(A_{i})}{\sum_{j}\Pr(B\mid A_{j})\Pr(A_{j})}.$$

Numerator = prior × likelihood. Denominator = marginal of the data (total probability).

分子 = 先验 × 似然；分母 = 数据的边际概率。

---

## Step 2: Base rates are not optional / 第二步：基础率不可省略

**Classic disease numbers.** $\Pr(D)=0.01$, $\Pr(+\mid D)=0.95$, $\Pr(+\mid D^{c})=0.10$.

$$\Pr(+)=0.1085,\qquad \Pr(D\mid +)=\frac{0.95\cdot 0.01}{0.1085}\approx 0.0876.$$

Only about 9% of positive tests are true disease when the disease is rare. Ignoring the prior is the **base-rate fallacy**.

---

## Step 3: Odds form / 第三步：优势形式

$$\frac{\Pr(A\mid B)}{\Pr(A^{c}\mid B)}=\frac{\Pr(B\mid A)}{\Pr(B\mid A^{c})}\cdot\frac{\Pr(A)}{\Pr(A^{c})}.$$

Posterior odds = likelihood ratio × prior odds.

---

## Step 4: Sequential updates / 第四步：序贯更新

Independent observations given type multiply likelihood ratios; then use the posterior as the new prior for the next predictive probability.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Bayes is definition + total probability — not a new axiom.  
2. Prior (base rate) always enters; “accurate test” ≠ high $\Pr(D\mid +)$.  
3. Likelihood $\Pr(B\mid A)$ is not the posterior $\Pr(A\mid B)$.  
4. Sequential independent observations multiply likelihood ratios.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Answering $\Pr(B\mid A)$ when asked $\Pr(A\mid B)$ | Read the conditioning direction |
| Dropping the prior | Base-rate fallacy |
| Denominator not equal to total $\Pr(B)$ | Sum over the full partition |
| Treating dependent tests as independent | Check the model |

## Practice / 练习（选择题）

**1.** $\Pr(D)=0.01$, $\Pr(+\mid D)=0.95$, $\Pr(+\mid D^{c})=0.10$. $\Pr(D\mid +)$ is approximately

A. $0.95$  
B. $0.50$  
C. $0.09$  
D. $0.01$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$\Pr(+)=0.1085$, posterior $\approx 0.0876\approx 0.09$.  
Answer: **C**.
</details>

**2.** Bayes formula for $\Pr(A\mid B)$ has numerator

A. $\Pr(A)$  
B. $\Pr(B\mid A)\Pr(A)$  
C. $\Pr(B)$  
D. $\Pr(A\cup B)$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Prior × likelihood.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

Standard name.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>
