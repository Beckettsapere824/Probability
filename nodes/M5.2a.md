# M5.2a Odds Form of Bayes / 贝叶斯优势形式

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M5.2](M5.2.md)

**Source anchors**: posterior odds = prior odds × likelihood ratio; base-rate neglect.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Rewrite Bayes in **odds** form so that updating is a simple multiplication by the likelihood ratio.

用**优势**形式写贝叶斯：后验优势 = 先验优势 × 似然比。

---

## Step 1: Odds / 第一步：优势

Odds of $D$ vs $D^{c}$: $\dfrac{\Pr(D)}{\Pr(D^{c})}$.  
Posterior odds:
$$\frac{\Pr(D\mid +)}{\Pr(D^{c}\mid +)}=\frac{\Pr(+\mid D)}{\Pr(+\mid D^{c})}\cdot\frac{\Pr(D)}{\Pr(D^{c})}.$$

---

## Step 2: Likelihood ratio / 第二步：似然比

$\mathrm{LR}=\Pr(+\mid D)/\Pr(+\mid D^{c})$. Multiply prior odds by LR.

---

## Step 3: Base-rate neglect / 第三步：基础率忽视

Ignoring prior odds and reporting only the LR is the base-rate fallacy.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Odds form makes sequential updates multiply LRs.  
2. Prior odds must enter.  
3. LR > 1 supports $D$; magnitude is not the posterior probability.  
4. Convert odds $o$ back to probability $o/(1+o)$.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Treating LR as $\Pr(D\mid +)$ | Convert via odds |
| Dropping prior odds | Base-rate neglect |
| Mixing probability and odds scales | Label units |
