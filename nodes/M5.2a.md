# M5.2a Odds Form of Bayes and Base-Rate Fallacy / 诊断 Bayes 的优势形式与基础率谬误

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M5.2 Medical Diagnosis](M5.2.md)

---

## Content / 内容

The posterior odds equal the prior odds multiplied by the likelihood ratio:
P(D|+)/P(D^c|+) = [P(D)/P(D^c)] · [P(+|D)/P(+|D^c)].
Sensitivity is P(+|D), specificity is P(−|D^c), and the false-positive rate is 1−specificity. When the base rate (prior prevalence) is very small, even a highly accurate test can leave the posterior probability of disease modest. Solving for a threshold parameter is performed by writing P(D|+) as a fractional equation in that parameter. The odds form reduces repeated arithmetic.

后验优势 = 先验优势 × 似然比：
P(D|+)/P(D^c|+) = [P(D)/P(D^c)] · [P(+|D)/P(+|D^c)]。
敏感度为 P(+|D)，特异度为 P(−|D^c)，假阳性率为 1−特异度。基础率（先验患病率）很小时，即使测试高度准确，后验患病概率仍可能不高。反解阈值参数时把 P(D|+) 写成该参数的分式方程。优势形式可减少重复计算。

## Example / 例子

Prior odds 1:99, likelihood ratio 20. Posterior odds 20:99 ≈ 1:5, so posterior probability ≈ 1/6.

先验优势 1:99，似然比 20。后验优势 20:99 ≈ 1:5，后验概率 ≈ 1/6。

## Practice Problem / 练习题

Prior prevalence 0.001, sensitivity 0.99, false-positive rate 0.01. Compute the posterior odds and the posterior probability.

先验患病率 0.001，敏感度 0.99，假阳性率 0.01。求后验优势与后验概率。

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

Prior odds 1:999. Likelihood ratio 0.99/0.01=99. Posterior odds 99:999=1:10.1 ≈ 0.09.

先验优势 1:999。似然比 0.99/0.01=99。后验优势 99:999=1:10.1 ≈ 0.09。

</details>
