# M1.2a Monotonicity and Simple Bounds / 概率单调性与简单上界

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M1.2](M1.2.md)

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

If $A\subset B$ then $\Pr(A)\le\Pr(B)$. Useful crude bounds before exact count.

---

## Facts / 要点

$\Pr(A)\le 1$; Boolean inequality $\Pr(A\cup B)\le\Pr(A)+\Pr(B)$.

---

## Practice / 练习

If $\Pr(A)=0.7$ and $A\subset B$, can $\Pr(B)=0.5$?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>No.</details>
