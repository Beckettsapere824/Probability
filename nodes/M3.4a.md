# M3.4a Affine Transform Examples / 仿射变换例

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M3.4](M3.4.md)

**Source anchors**: drill $E(aX+b)$, $\mathrm{Var}(aX+b)$ on numbers.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Practice affine mean/variance rules on concrete values until automatic.

用具体数字熟练 $E(aX+b)$ 与 $\mathrm{Var}(aX+b)$。

---

## Step 1: Recall / 第一步：回顾

$E(aX+b)=aE(X)+b$, $\mathrm{Var}(aX+b)=a^{2}\mathrm{Var}(X)$.

---

## Step 2: Sign of $a$ / 第二步：$a$ 的符号

Variance uses $a^{2}$, so $\mathrm{Var}(-3X)=\mathrm{Var}(3X)$. SD uses $|a|$.

---

## Step 3: Hand checks / 第三步：手算

$\mathrm{Var}(X)=2$ ⇒ $\mathrm{Var}(-3X+5)=18$. $E(X)=1$ ⇒ $E(-3X+5)=2$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Shift never enters variance.  
2. Negative scale flips orientation but not variance.  
3. Chain of affines still affine.  
4. Units scale with $a$.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| $\mathrm{Var}(-3X)=-3\mathrm{Var}(X)$ | Square the coefficient |
| Adding $b$ into variance | Drop $b$ |
| Mixing SD and Var formulas | SD uses $|a|$ |

---
