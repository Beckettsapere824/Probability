# M4.4 Geometric Distribution and Waiting Times / 几何分布与等待时间

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M1.6 Independence](M1.6.md), [M4.1 Bernoulli](M4.1.md)

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

How many trials until the first success in i.i.d. Bernoulli($p$) experiments? That waiting time is geometric—and it is discrete-memoryless, the discrete cousin of the exponential.

独立 Bernoulli($p$) 试验中首次成功出现在第几次？该等待时间是几何分布——离散无记忆，指数分布的离散近亲。

---

## Step 1: PMF (support $1,2,\ldots$) / 第一步：PMF

$$\Pr(T=k)=(1-p)^{k-1}p, \quad k=1,2,\ldots.$$

(Some books support $0,1,2,\ldots$ for failures before first success—check the convention.)

---

## Step 2: Mean, variance, memoryless / 第二步：均值、方差、无记忆

$$E(T)=\frac{1}{p}, \qquad \mathrm{Var}(T)=\frac{1-p}{p^{2}}.$$

Memoryless: $\Pr(T>m+n\mid T>m)=\Pr(T>n)$.

---

## Step 3: Hand check / 第三步：核对

$p=1/2$: $E(T)=2$ (fair coin, expected flips until first head).

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

- Mixing “trial of first success” ($E=1/p$) with “number of failures before first success” ($E=(1-p)/p$).  
- Applying geometric formulas when trials are not independent or $p$ changes.

---

## Practice / 练习

$p=0.25$. Find $E(T)$ and $\Pr(T=3)$.

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

$E(T)=4$, $\Pr(T=3)=(0.75)^{2}(0.25)=0.140625$.

</details>
