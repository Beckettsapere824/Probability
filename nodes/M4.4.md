# M4.4 Geometric Distribution and Waiting Times / 几何分布与等待时间

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.5 Series](M0.5.md), [M1.6 Independence](M1.6.md)

---

## Content / 内容

The geometric distribution (supported on the positive integers) gives the trial number of the first success in a sequence of i.i.d. Bernoulli(p) trials:
P(T=k) = (1−p)^{k−1} p, k=1,2,… .
Its expectation is 1/p and its variance is (1−p)/p^2. The distribution is memoryless. Comparison of two independent geometrics (who succeeds first) is performed by summing P(T_A=k) P(T_B > k).

几何分布（支撑在正整数上）给出一列 i.i.d. Bernoulli(p) 试验中首次成功的试验编号：
P(T=k) = (1−p)^{k−1} p，k=1,2,… 。
期望为 1/p，方差为 (1−p)/p^2。分布具有无记忆性。两个独立几何变量的比较（谁先成功）通过求和 P(T_A=k) P(T_B > k) 完成。

Waiting time is counted starting from 1.

等待时间从 1 开始计数。

## Example / 例子

Fair coin. P(first head on the 3rd toss) = (1/2)^2 · (1/2) = 1/8. E(T)=2.

公平硬币。首次正面在第 3 次的概率 = (1/2)^2 · (1/2) = 1/8。E(T)=2。

## Practice Problem / 练习题

Success probability p=1/3. Find P(T=4) and E(T).

成功概率 p=1/3。求 P(T=4) 与 E(T)。

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

P(T=4)=(2/3)^3 · (1/3)=8/81. E(T)=3.

P(T=4)=(2/3)^3 · (1/3)=8/81。E(T)=3。

</details>
