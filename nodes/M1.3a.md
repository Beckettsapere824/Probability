# M1.3a Conditional Complement and Conditional Independence / 条件补事件与条件独立

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M1.3](M1.3.md), [M1.6](M1.6.md)

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Work inside the world “$C$ occurred”: complements and independence relative to $C$.

在「$C$ 已发生」的世界里做补事件与独立性。

---

## Facts / 要点

$\Pr(A^{c}\mid C)=1-\Pr(A\mid C)$.  
Conditional independence given $C$: $\Pr(A\cap B\mid C)=\Pr(A\mid C)\Pr(B\mid C)$.

---

## Practice / 练习

$\Pr(A\mid C)=0.4$. $\Pr(A^{c}\mid C)$?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>$0.6$.</details>
