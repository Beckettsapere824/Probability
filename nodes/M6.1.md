# M6.1 Two-State Markov Chain / 两状态马氏链

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.8](M0.8.md), [M1.3](M1.3.md)

**Source anchors**: transition matrix; memoryless of past given present; $\pi^{(t+1)}=\pi^{(t)}P$.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

A process whose future depends on the past **only through the current state**. Two states $\{0,1\}$ with transition matrix $P$ (row-stochastic) is the minimal nontrivial example.

未来只通过**当前状态**依赖过去。两状态转移矩阵是最小非平凡例子。

---

## Step 1: Transition matrix / 第一步：转移矩阵

$$P=\begin{pmatrix}1-\alpha&\alpha\\\beta&1-\beta\end{pmatrix},\quad \alpha,\beta\in[0,1].$$

Row $i$ sums to 1: from $i$, probabilities of next states. $\Pr(X_{t+1}=j\mid X_t=i)=P_{ij}$.

---

## Step 2: One-step update / 第二步：一步更新

Row vector $\pi^{(t)}=(\pi^{(t)}_0,\pi^{(t)}_1)$:

$$\pi^{(t+1)}=\pi^{(t)}P.$$

---

## Step 3: Hand check / 第三步：手算

$\alpha=0.3$, $\beta=0.2$, $\pi^{(0)}=(1,0)$ ⇒ $\pi^{(1)}=(0.7,0.3)$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Markov: only current state matters for the next step.  
2. Rows of $P$ are conditional distributions.  
3. Distribution evolves by right-multiplication by $P$.  
4. Two-state chain is fully determined by two parameters $\alpha,\beta$.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Columns sum to 1 instead of rows | Convention: row-stochastic |
| Treating independent trials as needing full history | i.i.d. is Markov with identical rows |
| Confusing $P_{ij}$ direction | From $i$ to $j$ |

---

**1.** Row-stochastic means each row sums to

A. $0$  
B. $1$  
C. $n$  
D. $\alpha$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Definition.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**2.** $\pi^{(t+1)}=$

A. $P\pi^{(t)}$ (column convention without care)  
B. $\pi^{(t)}P$ (row-vector convention)  
C. $\pi^{(t)}+\alpha$  
D. $P$ only

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Row-vector update.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**3.** $P_{01}=\alpha$ means

A. stay in 0  
B. probability of $0\to 1$  
C. stationary mass  
D. $\beta$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Off-diagonal transition.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**4.** $\pi^{(0)}=(1,0)$, $\alpha=0.3$. $\pi^{(1)}_1=$

A. $0$  
B. $0.3$  
C. $0.7$  
D. $1$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

From 0, move to 1 with prob $\alpha$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**5.** Markov property: given present, future is independent of

A. the transition matrix  
B. the past  
C. the next state  
D. $\alpha$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Definition of Markov.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**6.** If $\alpha=\beta=0$, the chain

A. always jumps  
B. stays forever in the starting state  
C. is undefined  
D. has $P=I$ only if both zero — yes stays

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$P=I$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**7.** Two-state $P$ is determined by how many free parameters in $[0,1]$?

A. $1$  
B. $2$  
C. $4$  
D. $0$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$\alpha$ and $\beta$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**8.** i.i.d. Bernoulli trials as a chain have

A. different rows  
B. identical rows (memoryless of state value in a trivial way)  
C. $\alpha=1$ only  
D. no $P$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Next trial independent of current — rows equal.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>
