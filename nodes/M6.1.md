# M6.1 Two-State Markov Chain / 两状态马尔可夫链

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.8 Matrices](M0.8.md), [M1.3 Conditional Probability](M1.3.md)

---

## Content / 内容

A two-state Markov chain on {W,L} is completely described by a 2×2 transition matrix whose rows each sum to 1. The next state depends on the past only through the current state. An initial distribution together with the transition matrix determines the distribution at every future time. Multi-step transitions are obtained by matrix powering: the (i,j) entry of P^n is the probability of being in j after n steps starting from i.

状态集 {W,L} 上的两状态马尔可夫链完全由行和为 1 的 2×2 转移矩阵描述。下一状态对过去的依赖只通过当前状态。初始分布与转移矩阵共同决定未来每一时刻的分布。多步转移由矩阵幂得到：P^n 的 (i,j) 元是从 i 出发 n 步后处于 j 的概率。

## Example / 例子

P = [[0.6,0.4],[0.3,0.7]]. Starting from W, the probability of W after one step is 0.6; after two steps it is the (1,1) entry of P^2.

P = [[0.6,0.4],[0.3,0.7]]。从 W 出发一步后仍在 W 的概率为 0.6；两步后为 P^2 的 (1,1) 元。

## Practice Problem / 练习题

For the matrix above compute the probability of being in L after one step starting from L.

对上面的矩阵，计算从 L 出发一步后处于 L 的概率。

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

The (2,2) entry of P is 0.7.

P 的 (2,2) 元为 0.7。

</details>
