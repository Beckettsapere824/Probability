# M2.9 Categorical Sampling without Replacement and Multivariate Hypergeometric / 分类无放回抽样与多元超几何

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.3 Factorials, Permutations, Combinations](M0.3.md), [M1.9 Classical Probability](M1.9.md)

---

## Content / 内容

When an urn contains several categories (white, black, orange, …) and balls are drawn without replacement, the number of favorable outcomes for a prescribed category counts is the product of the binomial coefficients for each category. The total number of outcomes is the single binomial coefficient C(N,n) where N is the total number of balls.

瓮中有多类球（白、黑、橙…）且无放回抽取时，指定各类抽取数的有利结果数是各类组合数的乘积。总结果数为单个组合数 C(N,n)，N 为总球数。

The resulting joint probability is the multivariate hypergeometric PMF. Different category combinations may map to the same value of a derived random variable; those probabilities must be merged before the PMF is written.

所得联合概率即多元超几何 PMF。不同类别组合可能映射到同一随机变量值，写 PMF 前必须合并。

## Example / 例子

Urn with 5 white, 4 black, 3 orange. Draw 2 balls. Probability both white: C(5,2)/C(12,2). Probability one white and one black: C(5,1)C(4,1)/C(12,2).

瓮中 5 白 4 黑 3 橙。抽 2 球。两白概率：C(5,2)/C(12,2)。一白一黑：C(5,1)C(4,1)/C(12,2)。

## Practice Problem / 练习题

An urn holds 6 red, 5 blue and 4 green balls. Three balls are drawn without replacement. Find the probability of one of each color.

瓮中 6 红 5 蓝 4 绿。无放回抽 3 球。求三色各一的概率。

<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

C(6,1)C(5,1)C(4,1)/C(15,3).

C(6,1)C(5,1)C(4,1)/C(15,3)。
