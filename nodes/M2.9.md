# M2.9 Sampling Without Replacement (categorical) / 无放回分类抽样

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M2.4](M2.4.md)

**Source anchors**: multivariate hypergeometric counts; product of binomials over types.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

From a finite population with several categories, draw a fixed size without replacement; count ways to obtain prescribed category totals.

有限总体多类、无放回固定样本量；计各类规定数量的取法。

---

## Step 1: Count / 第一步：计数

Population: $N_i$ of type $i$, $N=\sum N_i$. Draw $n=\sum n_i$. Ways: $\prod_i\binom{N_i}{n_i}$.

---

## Step 2: Probability / 第二步：概率

Divide by $\binom{N}{n}$ — multivariate hypergeometric. See [M4.7](M4.7.md).

---

## Step 3: Hand check / 第三步：手算

5 red, 4 blue; 2 red and 1 blue: $\binom{5}{2}\binom{4}{1}=40$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Without replacement couples the category counts.  
2. Product of binomials over types, one overall $\binom{N}{n}$.  
3. Ordered sequential assignment yields same probabilities after cancelling factorials.  
4. Two types ⇒ ordinary hypergeometric.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Using with-replacement $p_i^{n_i}$ | Wrong model |
| Forgetting $\sum n_i=n$ | Constraint |
| Mixing ordered/unordered denominators | Stay consistent |

---

## Practice / 练习（选择题）

**1.** Ways to choose $n_i$ from type $i$ (unordered) multiply

A. $\prod N_i$  
B. $\prod\binom{N_i}{n_i}$  
C. $\prod n_i!$  
D. $N^n$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Product of combinations.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**2.** Probability (unordered) divides by

A. $N^n$  
B. $\binom{N}{n}$  
C. $n!$  
D. $1$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Total ways to choose $n$ from $N$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**3.** 5 red, 4 blue; 2 red and 1 blue ways =

A. $5$  
B. $40$  
C. $9$  
D. $1$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$\binom{5}{2}\binom{4}{1}=40$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**4.** Two types reduces to

A. binomial only with replacement  
B. hypergeometric  
C. Poisson  
D. geometric

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Standard.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

</details>

Sample size.

</details>
