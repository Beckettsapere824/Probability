# M0.3 Factorials, Permutations, Combinations / 阶乘、排列、组合

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.2 Product and Sum Rules](M0.2.md)

**Source anchors**: $n!$, $P(n,k)$, $\binom{n}{k}$; ordered vs unordered selections.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

From $n$ distinct objects, how many ordered lists of length $k$ exist? How many unordered teams of size $k$? The answers are permutations and combinations; they differ only by whether order is regarded as different.

从 $n$ 个不同对象中取 $k$ 个：有序清单有多少？无序小组有多少？排列与组合的唯一差别在于是否把顺序算作不同。

---

## Step 1: Factorial / 第一步：阶乘

$$
n!=n(n-1)\cdots 1,\qquad 0!=1.
$$

$n!$ counts the number of ways to order $n$ distinct objects (permutations of the whole set).

---

## Step 2: Permutation $P(n,k)$ / 第二步：排列

$$
P(n,k)=\frac{n!}{(n-k)!}=n(n-1)\cdots(n-k+1)
$$

(order matters, no repetition). Product of $k$ decreasing terms.

---

## Step 3: Combination $\binom{n}{k}$ / 第三步：组合

$$
\binom{n}{k}=\frac{n!}{k!(n-k)!}=\frac{P(n,k)}{k!}.
$$

Order does **not** matter; we divide by the $k!$ internal orders of each team.

Key identities: $\binom{n}{k}=\binom{n}{n-k}$, $\binom{n}{0}=\binom{n}{n}=1$.

---

## Step 4: Hand checks / 第四步：手算

- $P(5,2)=5\times 4=20$  
- $\binom{5}{2}=20/2!=10$  
- Number of ways to seat 4 people in a row: $4!=24$  
- Choose 3 books out of 7 to take (order irrelevant): $\binom{7}{3}=35$

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Permutation = ordered selection; Combination = unordered selection.  
2. $\binom{n}{k}=P(n,k)/k!$ because each unordered set corresponds to $k!$ ordered lists.  
3. Always ask first: “Does order matter in the problem statement?”  
4. $0!=1$ is a convention that keeps the formulas clean.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Using $P$ when the problem treats order as irrelevant | Switch to $\binom{n}{k}$ |
| Using $\binom{n}{k}$ when seats / ranking / sequence matter | Use $P(n,k)$ |
| Writing $\binom{n}{k}=n!/k!$ (forgetting $(n-k)!$) | Keep the full formula |
| Believing $\binom{n}{k}$ is defined only for $k\le n$ without the zero convention | See M0.3a |

## Practice / 练习（选择题）

**1.** $P(5,2)$ equals

A. 10  
B. 20  
C. 25  
D. 120

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$P(5,2)=5\times 4=20$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**2.** $\binom{5}{2}$ equals

A. 10  
B. 20  
C. 25  
D. 60

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$\binom{5}{2}=P(5,2)/2!=20/2=10$.  
Answer: **A**.
</details>

**3.** Number of ways to arrange 4 distinct books on a shelf (order matters) is

A. $\binom{4}{4}$  
B. $4!$  
C. $P(4,2)$  
D. $4^{4}$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Full permutation of 4 objects: $4!=24$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**4.** A committee of 3 is chosen from 8 people; seats are identical. Number of committees?

A. $P(8,3)$  
B. $8^{3}$  
C. $\binom{8}{3}$  
D. $3!$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Order does not matter → combination $\binom{8}{3}=56$.  
Answer: **C**.
</details>

**5.** Which identity is always true?

A. $P(n,k)=\binom{n}{k}$  
B. $\binom{n}{k}=P(n,k)/k!$  
C. $\binom{n}{k}=P(n,k)\times k!$  
D. $P(n,k)=n!/k!$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

By definition $\binom{n}{k}=P(n,k)/k!$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**6.** How many 3-letter words (repetition not allowed, order matters) can be formed from the letters A,B,C,D,E?

A. $\binom{5}{3}$  
B. $P(5,3)$  
C. $5^{3}$  
D. $3^{5}$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Ordered, no repetition → $P(5,3)=5\times 4\times 3=60$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**7.** $\binom{n}{0}$ equals

A. 0  
B. 1  
C. $n$  
D. undefined

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

By definition $\binom{n}{0}=n!/(0!\,n!)=1$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**8.** Choosing 2 people out of 6 for a photo where left/right positions matter is counted by

A. $\binom{6}{2}$  
B. $P(6,2)$  
C. $6+2$  
D. $2^{6}$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Positions matter → permutation $P(6,2)=30$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>
