# M0.3 Factorials, Permutations, Combinations / 阶乘、排列、组合

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.2](M0.2.md)

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Ordered lists vs unordered teams from $n$ distinct people.

从 $n$ 个不同对象中选：有序还是无序？

---

## Step 1: Factorial / 阶乘

$n!=n(n-1)\cdots 1$, $0!=1$.

---

## Step 2: Permutation / 排列

$P(n,k)=n!/(n-k)!$ — order matters.

---

## Step 3: Combination / 组合

$\binom{n}{k}=n!/(k!(n-k)!)$ — order does not matter.

---

## Practice / 练习

$P(5,2)$ and $\binom{5}{2}$?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>$20$ and $10$.</details>
