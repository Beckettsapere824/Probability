# M0.3 Factorials, Permutations, Combinations / 阶乘、排列、组合

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.2 Two Fundamental Counting Principles](M0.2.md)

---

## Content / 内容

The factorial n! = n × (n-1) × … × 1 counts the number of ordered arrangements of n distinct objects (with 0! = 1 by convention). The number of permutations of k objects chosen from n is P(n,k) = n! / (n-k)! = n × (n-1) × … × (n-k+1). Order matters.

n 的阶乘 n! = n × (n-1) × … × 1 计算 n 个可区分对象的有序排列数（约定 0! = 1）。从 n 个中选 k 个的排列数为 P(n,k) = n! / (n-k)! = n × (n-1) × … × (n-k+1)。顺序有关。

The number of combinations (unordered selections) is C(n,k) = binom(n,k) = n! / [k! (n-k)!]. Order does not matter; each unordered set of k objects corresponds to k! ordered ones, so we divide. Useful identities: C(n,k) = C(n,n-k) and C(n,0) = 1.

组合数（无序选取）为 C(n,k) = binom(n,k) = n! / [k! (n-k)!]。顺序无关；每个 k 元无序集对应 k! 个有序，所以要除。常用恒等式：C(n,k) = C(n,n-k)，C(n,0) = 1。

When selecting from several categories, multiply the combination numbers of each category, provided the overall selection is unordered within categories. Always keep numerator and denominator consistent: both ordered or both unordered.

从多类对象选取时，把各类组合数相乘（类内无序）。始终保持分子分母口径一致：同为有序或同为无序。

## Example / 例子

From 10 people choose a president, vice-president and secretary: P(10,3) = 10 × 9 × 8. From 10 people choose a committee of 3: C(10,3) = 120. From 5 men and 4 women choose 2 men and 2 women: C(5,2) × C(4,2).

从 10 人中选主席、副主席、秘书：P(10,3) = 10 × 9 × 8。从 10 人中选 3 人委员会：C(10,3) = 120。从 5 男 4 女中选 2 男 2 女：C(5,2) × C(4,2)。

## Practice Problem / 练习题

A team of 5 is chosen from 8 students. How many teams contain a particular student A? How many do not contain A?

从 8 名学生中选 5 人队。包含特定学生 A 的队伍有多少？不包含 A 的有多少？

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

Contain A: choose 4 more from remaining 7 → C(7,4). Not contain A: C(7,5). Check: C(7,4) + C(7,5) = C(8,5).

包含 A：再从剩 7 人中选 4 → C(7,4)。不包含 A：C(7,5)。检查：C(7,4) + C(7,5) = C(8,5)。

</details>
