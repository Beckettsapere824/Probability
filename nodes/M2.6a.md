# M2.6a Harder Partition Examples / 分堆难题

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M2.6](M2.6.md)

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Indistinguishable boxes or objects change the divisor (divide by symmetries).

---

## Rule of thumb / 要点

Distinct objects into distinct boxes: multinomial. If boxes identical, divide by box permutations when sizes differ.

---

## Practice / 练习

3 distinct books on 2 identical shelves (nonempty)? Careful casework on sizes (2,1).

<details><summary>Hint</summary>Sizes (2,1): $\binom{3}{2}/1$ ways up to shelf swap already handled by choosing which two together.</details>
