# M2.6a “Exactly / At-least / Non-empty” Inclusion-Exclusion Conversions / “恰好/至少/非空”的容斥转换

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M2.6 Inclusion-Exclusion, Non-empty Boxes](M2.6.md)

---

## Content / 内容

“Exactly k boxes non-empty”, “at least one box non-empty” and “all boxes non-empty” are distinct events. All boxes non-empty is total minus at least one empty, restored by inclusion-exclusion on the empty-box indicators.

“恰好 k 个盒子非空”“至少一个盒子非空”“所有盒子非空”是不同事件。全非空 = 总数减去至少一个空盒，再通过空盒指示的容斥加回。

When balls of one color are indistinguishable, allocate that color by stars-and-bars first, then impose the non-emptiness conditions via inclusion-exclusion. Distinct colors remain independent and their generating functions (or counts) multiply.

同色球不可区分时，先用 stars-and-bars 分配该颜色，再对非空条件做容斥。不同颜色独立，生成函数（或计数）相乘。

“At least one person receives a ball” = total − all balls to the same person. Always begin by defining the bad events E_i = “box i is empty”.

“至少一人有球” = 总数 − 全给同一人。写容斥时先定义坏事件 E_i = 第 i 个盒子为空。

## Example / 例子

3 indistinguishable red balls into 2 distinguishable boxes, both non-empty: unrestricted non-negative solutions of x+y=3 is 4; subtract the two cases (3,0) and (0,3); answer 2.

3 个不可区分红球入 2 个可区分盒，两盒皆非空：无约束非负解 4 个；减去 (3,0) 与 (0,3)；答案 2。

## Practice Problem / 练习题

Distribute 4 indistinguishable balls into 3 distinguishable boxes so that no box is empty. How many ways?

把 4 个不可区分球放入 3 个可区分盒子且无空盒，有多少种方式？

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

Positive solutions of x+y+z=4: C(3,2)=3. (The compositions are the permutations of (2,1,1).)

正整数解 x+y+z=4：C(3,2)=3。（即 (2,1,1) 的排列。）

</details>
