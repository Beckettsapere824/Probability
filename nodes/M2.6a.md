# M2.6a Harder Partition Examples / 分堆难题

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M2.6](M2.6.md)

**Source anchors**: identical vs distinct boxes; divide by symmetries when sizes equal.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

When boxes (or groups) are **identical**, partitions that differ only by permuting box labels are the same. Adjust multinomial counts by dividing by symmetries.

盒子（组）**相同**时，仅交换盒标签的划分算同一个；按对称性除以多重计数。

---

## Step 1: Distinct boxes / 第一步：相异盒

Distinct objects into distinct boxes with sizes $n_1,\ldots,n_k$: $\binom{n}{n_1,\ldots,n_k}$.

---

## Step 2: Identical boxes / 第二步：相同盒

Casework on size patterns; divide by multiplicities of equal sizes.

---

## Step 3: Hand idea / 第三步：思路

3 distinct books, 2 identical nonempty shelves: pattern (2,1) → $\binom{3}{2}=3$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Distinct labels ⇒ multinomial.  
2. Identical labels ⇒ divide by automorphisms of the size tuple.  
3. Empty boxes allowed or not changes the model.  
4. Casework on integer partitions of $n$ into $k$ parts often required.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Always dividing by $k!$ | Only when all sizes equal (or account multiplicities) |
| Treating identical boxes as distinct | Overcount |
| Forgetting empty restriction | State nonempty if required |

---
