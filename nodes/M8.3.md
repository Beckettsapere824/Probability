# M8.3 Uniform Distribution U(a,b) / 均匀分布

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M8.1 Continuous RV and pdf](M8.1.md)

**Source anchors**: SDST2601A Ch5 Def 5.3, Example 5.3 (rod angle); length-ratio probability; linear transforms.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Sometimes every value in an interval is equally plausible: a spinner on a circle, a random time of day, an angle with no preferred direction. We need the simplest continuous law that encodes “no preference on $(a,b)$”.

有时区间里每个取值同样可信：圆盘指针、一天中的随机时刻、无偏好方向的角度。需要最简单的连续律表达「在 $(a,b)$ 上无偏好」。

That law is the **uniform** distribution. Its pdf is a flat rectangle; probability is length of the sub-interval divided by total length.

这就是**均匀分布**。pdf 是平的矩形；概率 = 子区间长度 / 总长度。

---

## Step 1: Constant density / 第一步：常值密度

$$f(x)=\begin{cases} \dfrac{1}{b-a} & a<x<b, \\ 0 & \text{otherwise.} \end{cases}$$

Write $X\sim U(a,b)$. Height $1/(b-a)$ makes the rectangle area equal to 1.

记 $X\sim U(a,b)$。高度 $1/(b-a)$ 使矩形面积为 1。

---

## Step 2: CDF, mean, variance / 第二步：CDF、均值、方差

$$F(x)=\begin{cases} 0 & x\le a, \\ \dfrac{x-a}{b-a} & a<x<b, \\ 1 & x\ge b. \end{cases}$$

$$E(X)=\frac{a+b}{2}, \qquad \mathrm{Var}(X)=\frac{(b-a)^{2}}{12}.$$

Mean is the midpoint. Variance grows with the **square** of the interval length — doubling $(b-a)$ multiplies variance by 4.

均值是中点；方差随区间长度的**平方**增大。

---

## Step 3: Probability as length ratio / 第三步：概率即长度比

For any sub-interval $(c,d)\subset(a,b)$,

$$\Pr(c<X<d)=\frac{d-c}{b-a}.$$

No integration required beyond recognising area of a rectangle. This is the continuous analogue of “equally likely outcomes”.

子区间概率 = 长度比，是「等可能」的连续对应。

---

## Step 4: Hand example (rod angle) / 第四步：手算（杆的角度）

Rod falls; angle to North $X\sim U(0,2\pi)$.

$$E(X)=\pi, \quad \mathrm{Var}(X)=\frac{(2\pi)^{2}}{12}=\frac{\pi^{2}}{3}, \quad F(x)=\frac{x}{2\pi}\ (0\le x\le 2\pi).$$

Probability of pointing between two bearings = corresponding arc length over $2\pi$.

落在两方位之间的概率 = 圆心角 / $2\pi$。

---

## Step 5: Linear image / 第五步：线性像

If $X\sim U(0,1)$ and $Y=a+(b-a)X$ with $b>a$, then $Y\sim U(a,b)$.  
Many simulation algorithms start from $U(0,1)$ and transform.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Uniform = continuous “equally likely on an interval”.  
2. Probability = length ratio; density height is not a probability.  
3. Mean = midpoint; variance = $(b-a)^{2}/12$.  
4. $U(0,1)$ is the seed for many other continuous laws via transforms.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Treating density height $1/(b-a)$ as a probability | Probability is area |
| Denominator $b$ instead of $b-a$ | Length of support |
| Variance $(b-a)/12$ | Must be $(b-a)^{2}/12$ |
| Including endpoints inconsistently | Continuous: endpoints free |

---

## Worked exam-style example / 考试风格例题

**Problem.** $X\sim U(2,8)$. Find $E(X)$, $\mathrm{Var}(X)$, $\Pr(3<X<5)$.

**Solution.** $E=5$, $\mathrm{Var}=36/12=3$, $\Pr=(5-3)/(8-2)=1/3$.

## Practice / 练习

1. $X\sim U(0,1)$. $E(X^{2})$?

2. $X\sim U(-1,1)$. $\mathrm{Var}(X)$?

3. Why does doubling the length of the interval multiply variance by 4?

<details>
<summary>Solution sketches / 解答提示</summary>

1. $\int_0^1 x^{2}\,dx=1/3$.

2. $(2)^{2}/12=1/3$.

3. Variance formula has $(b-a)^{2}$.

</details>
