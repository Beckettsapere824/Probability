# M8.3 Uniform Distribution U(a,b) / 均匀分布

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M8.1 Continuous RV and pdf](M8.1.md)

**Source / 来源**: SDST2601A Chapter 5 §5.2.1 Definition 5.3, Example 5.3

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Sometimes every value in an interval is equally plausible: a spinner on a circle, a random time of day, an angle with no preferred direction. We need the simplest continuous law that encodes “no preference on $(a,b)$”.

有时区间里每个取值同样可信：圆盘上的指针、一天中的随机时刻、没有偏好方向的角度。我们需要最简单的连续律来表达「在 $(a,b)$ 上无偏好」。

That law is the **uniform** distribution. Its pdf is a flat rectangle; probability is just length of the interval divided by total length.

这就是**均匀分布**。pdf 是平的矩形；概率就是「子区间长度 / 总长度」。

---

## Step 1: Constant density (Def 5.3) / 第一步：常值密度

Draw a point “at random” from $(a,b)$ with **constant** density:

$$
f(x) = \begin{cases} \dfrac{1}{b-a} & a < x < b, \\ 0 & \text{otherwise.} \end{cases}
$$

Write $X\sim U(a,b)$. Height $1/(b-a)$ is chosen so area of the rectangle is 1.

记 $X\sim U(a,b)$。高度取 $1/(b-a)$，使矩形面积为 1。

---

## Step 2: CDF, mean, variance / 第二步：cdf、期望、方差

$$
F(x) = \begin{cases}
0 & x\le a, \\
\dfrac{x-a}{b-a} & a<x<b, \\
1 & x\ge b.
\end{cases}
$$

$$
E(X)=\frac{a+b}{2}, \qquad \mathrm{Var}(X)=\frac{(b-a)^{2}}{12}.
$$

Mean is the midpoint. Variance grows with the square of the length of the interval.

均值是中点；方差随区间长度平方增大。

---

## Step 3: Hand example (rod angle) / 第三步：手算例（杆的角度）

Example 5.3: a rod falls; angle to North satisfies $X\sim U(0,2\pi)$.

$$
E(X)=\pi, \quad \mathrm{Var}(X)=\frac{(2\pi)^{2}}{12}=\frac{\pi^{2}}{3}, \quad
F(x)=\frac{x}{2\pi}\ (0\le x<2\pi).
$$

Probability of pointing between two bearings = corresponding arc length over $2\pi$.

落在两方位之间的概率 = 对应圆心角 / $2\pi$。

---

## Summary / 小结

- Uniform = continuous “equally likely on an interval”.  
- Probability = length ratio.  
- Always check whether the support is $(a,b)$ or something shifted.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

- Treating density height as a probability.  
- Using $b$ instead of $b-a$ in the denominator.  
- Forgetting variance is $(b-a)^{2}/12$, not $(b-a)/12$.

---

## Practice / 练习

$X\sim U(2,8)$. Find $E(X)$, $\mathrm{Var}(X)$, and $\Pr(3<X<5)$.

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

$E=5$, $\mathrm{Var}=36/12=3$.  
$\Pr(3<X<5)=(5-3)/(8-2)=1/3$.

</details>
