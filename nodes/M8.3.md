# M8.3 Uniform Distribution U(a,b) / 均匀分布

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M8.1 Continuous RV and pdf](M8.1.md)

**Source / 来源**: SDST2601A Chapter 5 §5.2.1 Definition 5.3

---

## Content / 内容

### Definition 5.3

\(X\) is drawn “at random” from interval \((a,b)\) with **constant** density:

\[
f(x) = \begin{cases} \dfrac{1}{b-a} & a < x < b, \\ 0 & \text{otherwise.} \end{cases}
\]

Write \(X \sim U(a,b)\).

### CDF

\[
F(x) = \begin{cases}
0 & x \le a, \\
\dfrac{x-a}{b-a} & a < x < b, \\
1 & x \ge b.
\end{cases}
\]

### Mean and variance

\[
E(X) = \frac{a+b}{2}, \qquad \mathrm{Var}(X) = \frac{(b-a)^2}{12}.
\]

Interpretation: no preference for any sub-region of \((a,b)\).

---

## Example / 例子

**Example 5.3.** Rod falls on a plane; angle to North satisfies \(X \sim U(0,2\pi)\).

\[
E(X)=\pi, \quad \mathrm{Var}(X)=\frac{(2\pi)^2}{12}=\frac{\pi^2}{3}, \quad F(x)=\frac{x}{2\pi}\ (0\le x<2\pi).
\]

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

- Density height \(1/(b-a)\) is **not** a probability.
- Open vs closed endpoints do not change continuous probabilities.
- Length of interval in the denominator: \(b-a\), not \(b\) alone.

---

## Practice Problem / 练习题

\(X \sim U(2,8)\). Find \(E(X)\), \(\mathrm{Var}(X)\), and \(\Pr(3 < X < 5)\).

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

\(E=5\), \(\mathrm{Var}=36/12=3\).  
\(\Pr(3<X<5)=(5-3)/(8-2)=1/3\).

</details>
