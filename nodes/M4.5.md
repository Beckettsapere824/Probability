# M4.5 Negative Binomial / Trial of the r-th Success / 负二项分布/第 r 次成功位置

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M4.2 Binomial](M4.2.md), [M4.4 Geometric](M4.4.md)

---

## Content / 内容

The negative-binomial distribution (trial-number version) gives the probability that the r-th success occurs exactly on the n-th trial:
P(T=n) = C(n−1,r−1) p^r (1−p)^{n−r}, n=r,r+1,… .
This is equivalent to having exactly r−1 successes in the first n−1 trials and a success on the n-th trial. A concrete illustration is “the second buzz occurs on the 10th board”.

负二项分布（试验编号版本）给出第 r 次成功恰好发生在第 n 次试验的概率：
P(T=n) = C(n−1,r−1) p^r (1−p)^{n−r}，n=r,r+1,… 。
这等价于前 n−1 次中恰有 r−1 次成功且第 n 次成功。具体例子是“第 10 块板出现第二次 buzz”。

There is an alternative definition that counts the number of failures before the r-th success; the two versions have different supports and different means. Always clarify which version is intended before inserting numbers into a formula. For the trial-number version the mean is r/p; for the failure-count version the mean is r(1−p)/p.

另有一种定义计数第 r 次成功前的失败次数；两种版本支撑与均值不同。套公式前务必明确采用哪一种。试验编号版本均值为 r/p；失败次数版本均值为 r(1−p)/p。

## Example / 例子

r=2, p=1/2, n=4. P(T=4)=C(3,1)(1/2)^4=3/16.

r=2，p=1/2，n=4。P(T=4)=C(3,1)(1/2)^4=3/16。

## Practice Problem / 练习题

Probability that the 3rd head occurs on the 5th toss of a fair coin.

公平硬币第 3 次正面恰好出现在第 5 次抛掷的概率。

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

C(4,2)(1/2)^5 = 6/32 = 3/16.

C(4,2)(1/2)^5 = 6/32 = 3/16。

</details>
