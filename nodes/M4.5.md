# M4.5 Negative Binomial — Trial of the r-th Success / 负二项：第 r 次成功的试验编号

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M4.2 Binomial](M4.2.md), [M4.4 Geometric](M4.4.md)

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Geometric waits for the **first** success. Negative binomial waits for the **$r$-th** success: on which trial does the $r$-th head appear?

几何等的是**第一次**成功。负二项等的是**第 $r$ 次**成功：第 $r$ 次正面出现在第几次试验？

---

## Step 1: Trial-number version (this node’s default) / 第一步：试验编号版

$$\Pr(T=n)=\binom{n-1}{r-1}p^{r}(1-p)^{n-r}, \quad n=r,r+1,\ldots.$$

Story: exactly $r-1$ successes in the first $n-1$ trials, and a success on trial $n$.

叙述：前 $n-1$ 次中恰有 $r-1$ 次成功，第 $n$ 次成功。

Mean: $E(T)=r/p$.

---

## Step 2: Failure-count version (other books) / 第二步：失败次数版

Some texts let $X=$ number of **failures before** the $r$-th success. Then support starts at $0$ and $E(X)=r(1-p)/p$.

**Always check which version a formula assumes before plugging in numbers.**

套公式前必须分清版本。

---

## Step 3: Special case / 第三步：特例

$r=1$ recovers the geometric (trial-number) law of [M4.4](M4.4.md).

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

- Using $\binom{n}{r}$ instead of $\binom{n-1}{r-1}$.  
- Mixing trial-number mean $r/p$ with failure-count mean $r(1-p)/p$.

---

## Practice / 练习

$p=1/2$, $r=2$. Probability that the second head occurs on trial 4?

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

$\Pr(T=4)=\binom{3}{1}(1/2)^{2}(1/2)^{2}=3/16$.

</details>
