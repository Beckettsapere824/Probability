# M4.7 Hypergeometric — Sampling Without Replacement / 超几何：不放回抽样

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.3 Combinations](M0.3.md), [M4.2 Binomial](M4.2.md)

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

An urn has $K$ “success” items and $N-K$ others; you draw $n$ **without** replacement. How many successes land in the sample? Dependence between draws rules out a plain binomial model.

瓮中有 $K$ 个「成功」物品与 $N-K$ 个其他；不放回抽 $n$ 个。样本中成功数多少？抽取相依，不能直接用二项。

---

## Step 1: PMF / 第一步：PMF

$$\Pr(X=k)=\frac{\binom{K}{k}\binom{N-K}{n-k}}{\binom{N}{n}},$$

for integers $k$ where the binomial coefficients are defined (and $0$ otherwise).

Normalisation is automatic: the denominator counts all $n$-subsets.

---

## Step 2: Contrast with binomial / 第二步：与二项对比

| | Hypergeometric | Binomial |
|--|----------------|----------|
| Sampling | without replacement | with replacement / infinite population |
| Dependence | draws dependent | independent trials |
| Parameter feel | finite $N,K$ | fixed $p$ each trial |

When $N$ is huge and $n\ll N$, hypergeometric ≈ binomial with $p=K/N$.

---

## Step 3: Hand example / 第三步：手算

5 red, 7 blue, draw 3. $\Pr(\text{exactly 2 red})=\binom{5}{2}\binom{7}{1}/\binom{12}{3}$.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

- Using binomial $p=K/N$ when $n$ is not negligible relative to $N$.  
- Allowing $k$ outside the feasible range (more reds than available).

---

## Practice / 练习

Urn: 4 white, 6 black. Draw 3 without replacement. $\Pr(\text{exactly 1 white})$?

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

$\dfrac{\binom{4}{1}\binom{6}{2}}{\binom{10}{3}}=\dfrac{4\cdot 15}{120}=0.5$.

</details>
