# M4.7 Hypergeometric and Multivariate Hypergeometric / 超几何/多元超几何

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M2.9 Categorical Sampling without Replacement](M2.9.md)

---

## Content / 内容

Sampling without replacement from a finite population that is partitioned into categories yields the (multivariate) hypergeometric distribution. The joint probability of prescribed category counts is the product of the corresponding binomial coefficients divided by the single binomial coefficient that counts the total ways to choose the sample. The draws are dependent, which distinguishes the model from the binomial. The sample size cannot exceed the size of any category that is being depleted.

从被划分为若干类的有限总体中无放回抽样，得到（多元）超几何分布。指定各类计数的联合概率是相应组合数的乘积除以总抽样组合数。抽取之间相依，这与二项模型不同。抽样数不能超过正在被耗尽的任何一类的总数。

The PMF is written entirely in terms of binomial coefficients; normalisation is automatic.

PMF 完全用组合数写出；归一化自动成立。

## Example / 例子

Urn with 5 red and 7 blue, draw 3. P(exactly 2 red)=C(5,2)C(7,1)/C(12,3).

瓮中 5 红 7 蓝，抽 3。恰 2 红概率 = C(5,2)C(7,1)/C(12,3)。

## Practice Problem / 练习题

A committee of 4 is chosen from 6 men and 5 women. Probability the committee contains exactly 2 women?

从 6 男 5 女中选 4 人委员会。恰含 2 女的概率？
