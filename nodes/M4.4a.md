# M4.4a Geometric Memorylessness and Comparison of Two Geometrics / 几何分布无记忆性与两个几何变量比较

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M4.4 Geometric](M4.4.md), [M1.10 Independence of RVs](M1.10.md)

---

## Content / 内容

For T ~ Geometric(p) one has the memoryless property P(T > m+n | T > m) = P(T > n). The survival function is P(T > k) = (1−p)^k. When T_A and T_B are independent geometrics with respective success probabilities p_A and p_B,
P(T_A < T_B) = Σ_{k≥1} P(T_A=k) P(T_B > k).
The inner survival probability is (1−p_B)^k; the resulting series is a finite or infinite geometric sum.

对 T ~ Geometric(p) 有无记忆性 P(T > m+n | T > m) = P(T > n)。生存函数 P(T > k) = (1−p)^k。当 T_A、T_B 为独立几何且成功概率分别为 p_A、p_B 时，
P(T_A < T_B) = Σ_{k≥1} P(T_A=k) P(T_B > k)。
内层生存概率为 (1−p_B)^k；所得级数是有限或无限几何和。

## Example / 例子

p_A = p_B = 1/2. By symmetry P(T_A < T_B) = 1/2 − (1/2)P(tie) = 1/3 after accounting for the probability of equality.

p_A = p_B = 1/2。由对称性并计入相等概率后 P(T_A < T_B) = 1/3。

## Practice Problem / 练习题

T_A ~ Geo(1/2), T_B ~ Geo(1/3), independent. Write the series for P(T_A < T_B) and evaluate it.

T_A ~ Geo(1/2)，T_B ~ Geo(1/3)，独立。写出 P(T_A < T_B) 的级数并求值。

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

Σ_{k=1}^∞ (1/2)^k · (2/3)^k = Σ (1/3)^k = (1/3)/(1−1/3) = 1/2.

Σ_{k=1}^∞ (1/2)^k · (2/3)^k = Σ (1/3)^k = (1/3)/(1−1/3) = 1/2。

</details>
