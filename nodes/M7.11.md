# M7.11 Probability Upper Bounds and Parameter Optimisation / 事件概率上界与参数优化

## Content / 内容

The inequality P(A∪B∪C)≤1 together with the non-negativity of all intersection probabilities produces a system of linear inequalities on the unknown parameters α,β. Pairwise intersection probabilities satisfy P(A∩B)≤min(P(A),P(B)). Expanding the union by inclusion-exclusion and imposing the upper bound of 1 yields the feasible region. The maximum of a linear objective (for example the largest admissible β) is attained at a vertex of that region. Non-negativity constraints must be checked at every candidate vertex.

不等式 P(A∪B∪C)≤1 连同所有交概率的非负，产生未知参数 α、β 的线性不等式组。两两交概率满足 P(A∩B)≤min(P(A),P(B))。用包含排除展开并施加上界 1，得到可行域。线性目标（例如最大容许 β）的最大值在该域的顶点取得。每个候选顶点都必须检查非负约束。

## Example / 例子

If all single-event probabilities equal α, all pairwise intersections equal β and the triple intersection is zero, the constraint 3α−3β≤1 together with 0≤β≤α defines the feasible set; the maximal β occurs at a corner of this polygon.

若所有单事件概率等于 α，所有两两交等于 β 且三重交为零，则约束 3α−3β≤1 连同 0≤β≤α 定义可行集；最大 β 出现在该多边形的角点。

## Practice Problem / 练习题

P(A)=P(B)=α, P(A∩B)=β, P(A∪B)≤1. What is the largest possible β in terms of α?

P(A)=P(B)=α，P(A∩B)=β，P(A∪B)≤1。用 α 表示最大可能的 β。

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

2α−β≤1 and β≤α, β≥0. The upper bound on β is min(α,2α−1) (the latter only when α>1/2).

2α−β≤1 且 β≤α，β≥0。β 的上界为 min(α,2α−1)（后者仅当 α>1/2 时有效）。

</details>
