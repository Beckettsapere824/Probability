# M7.11 Optimising a Probability Bound / 概率上界优化

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M3.6](M3.6.md)

**Source anchors**: Markov / Chebyshev parameter tuning; minimise a valid upper bound.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

A family of valid bounds (e.g. Markov on $e^{tX}$) depends on a free parameter. Choose the parameter to **minimise** the upper bound (or maximise a lower bound).

一族合法界依赖自由参数；选参数使上界尽量小。

---

## Step 1: Markov template / 第一步：马尔可夫型

For $X\ge 0$, $\Pr(X\ge a)\le E(X)/a$. For $e^{tX}$ with $t>0$: $\Pr(X\ge a)\le e^{-ta}M(t)$ where $M(t)=E(e^{tX})$.

---

## Step 2: Optimise in $t$ / 第二步：对 $t$ 优化

Minimise $e^{-ta}M(t)$ over $t>0$ (or take log and differentiate). The optimal $t$ often solves $M'(t)/M(t)=a$.

---

## Step 3: Hand idea / 第三步：思路

Chebyshev: $\Pr(|X-\mu|\ge\varepsilon)\le\mathrm{Var}(X)/\varepsilon^{2}$ — no free $t$, but Markov-on-squares is the same bound. Chernoff-style bounds optimise $t$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Every $t$ gives a valid bound; some $t$ are tighter.  
2. Optimising $t$ is calculus on the mgf.  
3. The bound remains valid even if $t$ is chosen with knowledge of $a$.  
4. Loose bounds can still be useful for rates.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Claiming the optimised bound is sharp equality | Still an inequality |
| Using $t<0$ for upper tail of unbounded $X$ | Match tail direction |
| Forgetting domain of $M(t)$ | mgf must exist |
