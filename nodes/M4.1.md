# M4.1 Bernoulli Distribution / Bernoulli 分布

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M3.1 Expectation](M3.1.md)

**Source / 来源**: SDST2601A discrete families; building block for binomial

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

The simplest non-trivial random variable is a yes/no indicator: disease detected or not, trial success or failure. We need its mean and variance in closed form, because every binomial is a sum of such indicators.

最简单的非平凡随机变量是是/否指示：检出与否、试验成功与否。需要闭式均值方差，因为二项是这些指示的和。

---

## Step 1: Definition / 第一步：定义

$X$ takes values in $\{0,1\}$ with $\Pr(X=1)=p$, $\Pr(X=0)=1-p$ ($0\le p\le 1$). Write $X\sim\mathrm{Bernoulli}(p)$.

Any event indicator $I_{A}$ is Bernoulli with $p=\Pr(A)$.

---

## Step 2: Mean and variance / 第二步：均值与方差

$$E(X)=p, \qquad \mathrm{Var}(X)=p(1-p).$$

Variance is largest at $p=1/2$ and zero at the extremes $p\in\{0,1\}$.

---

## Step 3: Hand check / 第三步：核对

Fair coin: $p=1/2$, $E=1/2$, $\mathrm{Var}=1/4$.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

- Using $p(1-p)$ as a probability (it is a variance).  
- Forgetting that Bernoulli is the $n=1$ case of binomial.

---

## Practice / 练习

Detection probability $0.9$ when disease is present. Model the detection indicator; give mean and variance.

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

$\mathrm{Bernoulli}(0.9)$, mean $0.9$, variance $0.09$.

</details>
