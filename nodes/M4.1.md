# M4.1 Bernoulli Distribution / Bernoulli 分布

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M3.1 Expectation](M3.1.md), [M1.8 Discrete RV](M1.8.md)

**Source anchors**: SDST2601A discrete families; indicator of any event; building block for binomial and geometric.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

The simplest non-trivial random variable is a yes/no indicator: success or failure on one trial, disease detected or not, item defective or not. We need its mean and variance in closed form, because every binomial is a sum of such indicators, and every geometric is built from repeated independent Bernoullis.

最简单的非平凡随机变量是是/否指示：一次试验成功与否、检出与否、次品与否。需要闭式均值方差，因为二项是这些指示的和，几何由重复独立 Bernoulli 构成。

---

## Step 1: Definition / 第一步：定义

$X$ takes values in $\{0,1\}$ with

$$\Pr(X=1)=p, \qquad \Pr(X=0)=1-p, \qquad 0\le p\le 1.$$

Write $X\sim\mathrm{Bernoulli}(p)$.  
Any event indicator $I_A(\omega)=1$ if $\omega\in A$ and $0$ otherwise is Bernoulli with parameter $\Pr(A)$.

PMF: $p_X(x)=p^{x}(1-p)^{1-x}$ for $x\in\{0,1\}$ (compact form used in likelihood writing).

任意事件指示 $I_A$ 都是参数为 $\Pr(A)$ 的 Bernoulli。

---

## Step 2: Mean and variance / 第二步：均值与方差

$$E(X)=0\cdot(1-p)+1\cdot p=p.$$

$$E(X^{2})=E(X)=p$$

(because $X^{2}=X$ on $\{0,1\}$), so

$$\mathrm{Var}(X)=E(X^{2})-[E(X)]^{2}=p-p^{2}=p(1-p).$$

Variance is largest at $p=1/2$ ($\mathrm{Var}=1/4$) and zero at the extremes $p\in\{0,1\}$ (deterministic).

$X^{2}=X$ 是 Bernoulli 的代数特征；方差在 $p=1/2$ 最大。

---

## Step 3: Link to binomial and geometric / 第三步：与二项、几何的联系

- $n$ i.i.d. Bernoulli($p$) sum to $\mathrm{Binomial}(n,p)$ ([M4.2](M4.2.md)).  
- Independent Bernoulli trials until first success yield Geometric($p$) ([M4.4](M4.4.md)).  
- Linearity of indicators: expected number of successes among $n$ (possibly dependent) events is $\sum\Pr(A_j)$, even without independence.

---

## Step 4: Hand check / 第四步：核对

Fair coin: $p=1/2$, $E=1/2$, $\mathrm{Var}=1/4$.  
Detection probability $0.9$ when disease present: indicator $\sim\mathrm{Bernoulli}(0.9)$, mean $0.9$, variance $0.09$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Bernoulli = single yes/no trial; indicator of any event.  
2. $E=p$, $\mathrm{Var}=p(1-p)$; max variance at fair coin.  
3. Binomial = sum of i.i.d. Bernoullis; geometric = waiting among i.i.d. Bernoullis.  
4. Indicator linearity gives expectations without full joint laws.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Using $p(1-p)$ as a probability | It is a variance |
| Forgetting Bernoulli is $\mathrm{Bin}(1,p)$ | Same PMF |
| Writing $\mathrm{Var}=p$ | Only if you confuse with Poisson |
| Applying Bernoulli formulas when outcomes are not $\{0,1\}$ | Recode or use a different family |

---

## Worked exam-style example / 考试风格例题

**Problem.** Let $I$ be the indicator that a random item is defective, with $\Pr(\text{defective})=0.03$. Find $E(I)$ and $\mathrm{Var}(I)$.

**Solution.** $E(I)=0.03$, $\mathrm{Var}(I)=0.03\cdot 0.97=0.0291$.

## Practice / 练习

1. Detection probability $0.9$. Mean and variance of the detection indicator?

2. Show $X^{2}=X$ a.s. for Bernoulli $X$, and deduce $\mathrm{Var}(X)=p-p^{2}$.

3. Why does $\mathrm{Var}(X)$ vanish at $p=0$ and $p=1$?

<details>
<summary>Solution sketches / 解答提示</summary>

1. Mean $0.9$, variance $0.09$.

2. On $\{0,1\}$, $0^{2}=0$ and $1^{2}=1$; then $E(X^{2})=p$, $\mathrm{Var}=p-p^{2}$.

3. $X$ is almost surely constant (always 0 or always 1).

</details>
