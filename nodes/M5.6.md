# M5.6 Posterior Prediction and Conditional Expectation / 后验预测与条件期望

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M5.1 Conditional and Posterior Distributions](M5.1.md), [M4.2 Binomial](M4.2.md), [M3.11 Conditional Expectation](M3.11.md)

---

## Content / 内容

After the first draw yields a red ball, the posterior distribution of the number X of red balls remaining in the urn is obtained by Bayes. The probability that the next draw is also red is the posterior expectation E[X/3 | R_1] (or the appropriate remaining total). This is precisely a posterior predictive probability and equals a conditional expectation. The computational pattern is: obtain the posterior, then take the expectation of the predictive probability under that posterior. The law of total expectation can be used as a consistency check.

第一次抽到红球后，瓮中剩余红球数 X 的后验分布由贝叶斯得到。下一次仍抽到红球的概率是后验期望 E[X/3 | R_1]（或相应的剩余总数）。这正是后验预测概率，等于一个条件期望。计算模式为：先得后验，再在该后验下对预测概率取期望。全期望公式可用作一致性检查。

## Example / 例子

Three balls, X ~ B(3,p). After drawing a red, the posterior predictive probability of another red is a rational function of p that can be computed explicitly.

三球，X ~ B(3,p)。抽到一红后，再抽到红的后验预测概率是 p 的有理函数，可显式计算。

## Practice Problem / 练习题

Urn starts with 1 red and 1 white. A ball is drawn and replaced with an extra ball of the same colour. Given that the first draw was red, what is the probability that the second draw is red?

瓮中最初 1 红 1 白。抽一球并放回同色额外一球。已知第一次为红，第二次为红的概率？

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

After a red is drawn and replaced with an extra red the urn has 2 red and 1 white. Predictive probability = 2/3.

抽到红并放入额外红后，瓮中 2 红 1 白。预测概率 = 2/3。

</details>
