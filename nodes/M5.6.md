# M5.6 Posterior Prediction / 后验预测

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M3.11](M3.11.md), [M5.1](M5.1.md), [M1.5](M1.5.md)

**Source anchors**: average the conditional predictive over the posterior; urn and parameter examples.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

After data update the belief about a latent state or parameter, predict a **future** observation by averaging its conditional probability under the **posterior**.

数据更新对潜在状态/参数的信念后，用**后验**平均条件预测概率，得到下一次观测的预测。

---

## Step 1: Law of total probability under posterior / 第一步：后验下的全概率

$$\Pr(\text{next}\mid\text{data})=\sum_\theta\Pr(\text{next}\mid\theta,\text{data})\Pr(\theta\mid\text{data}).$$

---

## Step 2: Examples / 第二步：例子

- Urn: posterior on number of red; predictive next red mixes $r/N$ under posterior.  
- Binary tests: posterior on disease; predictive next result mixes accuracy under posterior disease probability.

---

## Step 3: Distinction / 第三步：区分

Posterior is about the latent; predictive is about the next observable.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Predictive = posterior mixture of conditionals.  
2. Plug-in at the posterior mean is an approximation, not always exact.  
3. Without replacement, conditionals depend on remaining composition.  
4. Same structure as [M7.6](M7.6.md) Bayesian urn.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Using prior instead of posterior | Update first |
| Reporting $\theta$ posterior as the predictive | Different objects |
| Forgetting dependence of likelihood on remaining counts | Model carefully |
