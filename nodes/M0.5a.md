# M0.5a Finite Geometric Series / 有限几何级数

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.5](M0.5.md)

**Source anchors**: closed form of $\sum_{k=0}^{m}r^{k}$; complement probabilities of the form $1-(1-p)^{m}$.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Summing $1+r+r^{2}+\cdots+r^{m}$ term by term is slow. A closed form lets us solve for the smallest $m$ such that a probability exceeds a threshold, and distinguishes finite sums from the infinite geometric series (which needs $|r|<1$).

逐项加 $1+r+\cdots+r^{m}$ 慢。闭式可解「最小 $m$ 使概率超过阈值」，并分清有限和与需要 $|r|<1$ 的无限几何级数。

---

## Step 1: Derivation / 第一步：推导

Let $S=1+r+r^{2}+\cdots+r^{m}$. Then

$$
rS=r+r^{2}+\cdots+r^{m}+r^{m+1}.
$$

Subtract:

$$
S-rS=1-r^{m+1}\implies S=\frac{1-r^{m+1}}{1-r}\quad(r\neq 1).
$$

If $r=1$, then $S=m+1$.

$r=1$ 时每一项都是 1，共 $m+1$ 项。

---

## Step 2: Variants / 第二步：常用变体

$$
\sum_{k=0}^{m}r^{k}=\frac{1-r^{m+1}}{1-r},\qquad
\sum_{k=1}^{m}r^{k-1}=\frac{1-r^{m}}{1-r}.
$$

Complement form used in independent trials: probability of at least one success in $m$ trials with success probability $p$ is

$$
1-(1-p)^{m}.
$$

这是「$m$ 次独立试验至少一次成功」的补事件写法。

---

## Step 3: Finite vs infinite / 第三步：有限与无限

| Sum | Condition | Value |
|-----|-----------|-------|
| $\sum_{k=0}^{m}r^{k}$ | any $r\neq 1$ | $(1-r^{m+1})/(1-r)$ |
| $\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}r^{k}$ | $\|r\|<1$ | $1/(1-r)$ |

Never apply the infinite formula to a finite problem without taking the limit $m\to\infty$ under $\|r\|<1$.

---

## Step 4: Hand checks / 第四步：手算

$1+2+4+\cdots+2^{5}=2^{6}-1=63$ (formula $(2^{6}-1)/(2-1)$).

$1+1/2+\cdots+(1/2)^{3}=(1-(1/2)^{4})/(1-1/2)=15/8$.

Smallest $m$ with $1-(0.9)^{m}\ge 0.5$: solve $(0.9)^{m}\le 0.5$, $m\ln 0.9\le\ln 0.5$, $m\ge\ln 0.5/\ln 0.9\approx 6.58$, so $m=7$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Finite geometric sum has a closed form with no convergence restriction (except $r\neq 1$).  
2. Infinite sum is the limit and needs $|r|<1$.  
3. $1-(1-p)^{m}$ is the workhorse for “at least one” in $m$ independent trials.  
4. Solving for $m$ uses logs; watch the direction of the inequality when $\ln r<0$.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Using $1/(1-r)$ for a finite sum | Use $(1-r^{m+1})/(1-r)$ |
| Forgetting $r=1$ case | Handle separately: sum $=m+1$ |
| Inequality direction when taking $\ln$ of both sides with base $<1$ | $\ln$ is increasing but $\ln r$ is negative, so inequality flips when dividing |
| Off-by-one in the exponent | Count: $k=0$ to $m$ is $m+1$ terms |

## Practice / 练习（选择题）

**1.** $\sum_{k=0}^{5}2^{k}$ equals

A. 31  
B. 32  
C. 63  
D. 64

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$(2^{6}-1)/(2-1)=63$.  
Answer: **C**.
</details>

**2.** For $r\neq 1$, $\sum_{k=0}^{m}r^{k}=$

A. $(1-r^{m})/(1-r)$  
B. $(1-r^{m+1})/(1-r)$  
C. $1/(1-r)$  
D. $r^{m+1}-1$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Standard closed form: numerator $1-r^{m+1}$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**3.** Probability of at least one success in 3 independent trials with $p=0.2$ is

A. $0.2^{3}$  
B. $1-0.8^{3}$  
C. $3\cdot 0.2$  
D. $0.8^{3}$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Complement of zero successes: $1-(1-p)^{3}=1-0.8^{3}$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**4.** If $r=1$, $\sum_{k=0}^{4}r^{k}=$

A. 0  
B. 1  
C. 4  
D. 5

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Five terms of 1: $5$.  
Answer: **D**.
</details>

**5.** $\sum_{k=0}^{3}(1/2)^{k}=$

A. $15/8$  
B. $15/16$  
C. $7/8$  
D. $2$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$(1-(1/2)^{4})/(1-1/2)= (1-1/16)/(1/2)=15/8$.  
Answer: **A**.
</details>

**6.** The infinite sum $\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}r^{k}$ converges only when

A. $r\neq 1$  
B. $|r|<1$  
C. $r>0$  
D. always

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Geometric series radius: $|r|<1$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**7.** Smallest integer $m$ such that $1-(0.5)^{m}\ge 0.9$ is

A. 2  
B. 3  
C. 4  
D. 5

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$(0.5)^{m}\le 0.1\Rightarrow m\ge \log_{2}10\approx 3.32$, so $m=4$. Check: $1-1/16=15/16=0.9375\ge 0.9$; $m=3$ gives $0.875<0.9$.  
Answer: **C**.
</details>

**8.** $\sum_{k=1}^{m}r^{k-1}$ for $r\neq 1$ equals

A. $(1-r^{m})/(1-r)$  
B. $(1-r^{m+1})/(1-r)$  
C. $r(1-r^{m})/(1-r)$  
D. $m$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

This is $1+r+\cdots+r^{m-1}=(1-r^{m})/(1-r)$.  
Answer: **A**.
</details>
