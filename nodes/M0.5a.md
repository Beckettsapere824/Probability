# M0.5a Finite Geometric Series / 有限几何级数

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.5](M0.5.md)

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Sum $1+r+\cdots+r^{n}$ without listing terms.

---

## Formula / 公式

$S=(1-r^{n+1})/(1-r)$ for $r\neq 1$; $S=n+1$ if $r=1$.

---

## Practice / 练习

Sum of $1+2+4+\cdots+2^{5}$?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>$(2^{6}-1)/(2-1)=63$.</details>
