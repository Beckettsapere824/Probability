# M0.5a Finite Geometric Series / 有限几何级数

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.5 Sums, Series, Logarithmic Expansion](M0.5.md)

---

## Content / 内容

The finite geometric sum is Σ_{k=0}^m r^k = (1 - r^{m+1}) / (1-r) for r ≠ 1. Equivalently Σ_{k=1}^m r^{k-1} = (1 - r^m) / (1-r). No absolute-value restriction is required; the formula is algebraic.

有限几何和：Σ_{k=0}^m r^k = (1 - r^{m+1}) / (1-r)（r ≠ 1）。等价地 Σ_{k=1}^m r^{k-1} = (1 - r^m) / (1-r)。不需要 |r| < 1；公式是代数恒等式。

The complementary-event form 1 - (1-p)^m appears constantly for the probability of at least one success in m independent trials. Solving inequalities such as 1 - (1-p)^n > α for the smallest integer n uses logarithms and careful handling of inequality direction.

补事件形式 1 - (1-p)^m 在 m 次独立试验“至少一次成功”的概率中反复出现。解不等式 1 - (1-p)^n > α 求最小整数 n 时要用对数并注意不等号方向。

Finite sums are used for “smallest n such that probability exceeds a threshold”, geometric waiting-time comparisons with a finite horizon, and exact tail probabilities of binomial or geometric distributions truncated at a fixed length.

有限和用于“最小 n 使概率超过阈值”、有限视野下的几何等待比较、以及截断到固定长度的二项或几何尾概率。

## Example / 例子

Σ_{k=0}^3 (1/2)^k = 1 + 1/2 + 1/4 + 1/8 = 15/8 = (1 - (1/2)^4)/(1-1/2). Probability of at least one head in 5 fair coin tosses: 1 - (1/2)^5 = 31/32.

Σ_{k=0}^3 (1/2)^k = 1 + 1/2 + 1/4 + 1/8 = 15/8 = (1 - (1/2)^4)/(1-1/2)。5 次公平抛币至少一次正面：1 - (1/2)^5 = 31/32。

## Practice Problem / 练习题

Find the smallest integer n such that the probability of at least one success in n independent Bernoulli(0.1) trials exceeds 0.5.

求最小整数 n，使 n 次独立 Bernoulli(0.1) 试验中至少一次成功的概率超过 0.5。

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

1 - (0.9)^n > 0.5 ⇒ (0.9)^n < 0.5 ⇒ n ln 0.9 < ln 0.5. Since ln 0.9 < 0, inequality reverses: n > ln 0.5 / ln 0.9 ≈ 6.58. Thus n = 7.

1 - (0.9)^n > 0.5 ⇒ (0.9)^n < 0.5 ⇒ n ln 0.9 < ln 0.5。因 ln 0.9 < 0，不等号反向：n > ln 0.5 / ln 0.9 ≈ 6.58。故 n = 7。
