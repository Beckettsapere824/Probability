# M2.11 Derangements / 错排

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M1.2](M1.2.md), [M0.3](M0.3.md)

**Source anchors**: $!n=n!\sum_{k=0}^{n}(-1)^k/k!$; $\Pr\to 1/e$.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Number of permutations of $n$ items with **no fixed points** (derangements), and the probability a random permutation is a derangement.

$n$ 个元素无不动点的排列数，以及随机排列为错排的概率。

---

## Step 1: IE formula / 第一步：容斥

$$!n=n!\sum_{k=0}^{n}\frac{(-1)^k}{k!}.$$

---

## Step 2: Probability / 第二步：概率

$!n/n!\to 1/e$ as $n\to\infty$.

---

## Step 3: Hand check / 第三步：手算

$!0=1$, $!1=0$, $!2=1$, $!3=2$, $!4=9$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Inclusion-exclusion on the properties “$i$ is fixed”.  
2. Probability of no fixed points stabilises near $37\%$.  
3. Related to hat-check / matching problems ([M7.5](M7.5.md)).  
4. Expected number of fixed points is 1 for every $n\ge1$.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Using $n!-1$ | Far too large |
| Stopping IE at $k=1$ | Full alternating sum |
| Confusing $!n$ with $n!$ | Subfactorial notation |
