# M2.3a Injection / Partial Permutation Notes / 单射与部分排列

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M2.3](M2.3.md)

**Source anchors**: $P(n,k)=n!/(n-k)!$; injections from a $k$-set into an $n$-set.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

$P(n,k)$ counts **ordered** selections of $k$ distinct items from $n$, equivalently the number of injective functions from a labeled $k$-set into an $n$-set.

$P(n,k)$ 计数从 $n$ 中有序选 $k$ 个互异元素，等价于 $k$ 元集到 $n$ 元集的单射个数。

---

## Step 1: Formula / 第一步：公式

$$P(n,k)=n(n-1)\cdots(n-k+1)=\frac{n!}{(n-k)!}\quad(0\le k\le n).$$

---

## Step 2: Special cases / 第二步：特例

$P(n,n)=n!$, $P(n,1)=n$, $P(n,0)=1$.

---

## Step 3: vs combinations / 第三步：与组合对比

$\binom{n}{k}=P(n,k)/k!$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Injection = no repeated images.  
2. Order of assignment matters for $P$; not for $\binom{n}{k}$.  
3. $P(n,k)=0$ for $k>n$.  
4. Same count as sequential distinct draws without replacement (ordered).

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Using $\binom{n}{k}$ when order matters | Use $P$ |
| Writing $n^k$ for distinct positions | $n^k$ allows repeats |
| $k>n$ still positive | Zero |

---
