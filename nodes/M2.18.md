# M2.18 Gap Method / 间隔法

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M2.4](M2.4.md)

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Place people with gaps so restricted positions (e.g. no two adjacent) become ordinary combinations on gaps.

---

## Practice / 练习

Seat 3 people in 10 seats no two adjacent: place 7 “blocks” creating gaps, choose 3 gaps: standard gap count.
