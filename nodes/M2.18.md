# M2.18 Gap Method / 间隔法

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M2.4](M2.4.md)

**Source anchors**: place restricted objects into gaps; no-two-adjacent seating.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Seat $k$ people in $n$ seats so that **no two are adjacent** by first placing unrestricted units to create gaps, then choosing gaps.

用间隔法处理“不相邻”：先放无限制对象形成空隙，再往空隙里放受限对象。

---

## Step 1: Create gaps / 第一步：造空隙

Place $n-k$ empties → $n-k+1$ gaps. Choose $k$ gaps.

---

## Step 2: Count / 第二步：计数

$\binom{n-k+1}{k}$ (identical people); × $k!$ if distinct.

---

## Step 3: Hand idea / 第三步：思路

3 people in 10 seats, distinct, no adjacent: $P(8,3)$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Gaps turn a hard constraint into ordinary selection.  
2. Line vs circle changes gap count.  
3. Distinct vs identical people: $P$ vs $\binom{\cdot}{\cdot}$.  
4. Stronger separation = more forced empties first.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Using $\binom{n}{k}$ without gaps | Allows adjacency |
| Wrong gap count on a circle | Often $n-k$ gaps |
| Forgetting to permute distinct people | × $k!$ or use $P$ |

---
