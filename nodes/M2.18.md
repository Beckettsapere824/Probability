# M2.18 Gap Method, Forbidden Positions, Seat Counting / 间隔法、禁选组合、座位计数

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.3 Factorials, Permutations, Combinations](M0.3.md), [M2.3 Stars and Bars](M2.3.md)

---

## Content / 内容

The number of ways to choose k positions from 1…N with no two adjacent is C(N-k+1,k). More generally, to enforce a minimum gap of d empty seats between selected seats one substitutes y_i = x_i - (d-1)(i-1). The same technique counts the ways 12 people can sit in 40 seats with no two adjacent, or with at least two empty seats between each pair.

从 1…N 中选 k 个位置且无两者相邻的方式数为 C(N-k+1,k)。更一般地，要保证所选座位之间至少隔 d 个空位，令 y_i = x_i - (d-1)(i-1)。同一技巧计数 12 人坐 40 座无相邻、或每对之间至少两空座的方式。

One may first seat the people and then place the required empty seats into the gaps, or first choose the seats and then verify the gap condition. Conditional probabilities become ratios of the corresponding combinatorial counts. Note that “at least two empty seats” and “at least one empty seat” correspond to different values of d.

可先排人再在间隙放空座，或先选座位再检查间隔。条件概率成为相应组合计数的比值。注意“至少两个空座”与“至少一个空座”对应不同的 d。

## Example / 例子

Choose 3 seats from 10 with no two adjacent: C(10-3+1,3) = C(8,3) = 56.

从 10 座中选 3 座无相邻：C(10-3+1,3) = C(8,3) = 56。

## Practice Problem / 练习题

How many ways can 4 people sit in 12 seats in a row so that no two are adjacent?

4 人坐在一排 12 座中且无两人相邻，有多少种方式？（座位可区分，人可区分）

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

First choose 4 non-adjacent seats: C(12-4+1,4)=C(9,4). Then assign 4 people to those seats: 4!. Total C(9,4)·24.

先选 4 个不相邻座位：C(12-4+1,4)=C(9,4)。再把 4 人排到这些座位：4!。总数 C(9,4)·24。

</details>
