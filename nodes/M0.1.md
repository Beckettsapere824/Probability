# M0.1 Sets and Event Language / 集合与事件语言

**Prerequisites / 前置**: none

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Probability is built on sets. Before numbers, we need language: element, subset, union, intersection, complement, and Venn pictures.

概率建立在集合上。先要有语言：元素、子集、并、交、补与 Venn 图。

---

## Step 1: Basic operations / 第一步：基本运算

$A\cup B$, $A\cap B$, $A^{c}$, $A\setminus B$. Disjoint means $A\cap B=\emptyset$.

---

## Step 2: De Morgan / 第二步：德摩根

$(A\cup B)^{c}=A^{c}\cap B^{c}$, $(A\cap B)^{c}=A^{c}\cup B^{c}$.

---

## Step 3: Events / 第三步：事件

In probability, outcomes live in $\Omega$; events are subsets we assign probability to.

---

## Practice / 练习

If $A\subset B$, simplify $A\cup B$ and $A\cap B$.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>$A\cup B=B$, $A\cap B=A$.</details>
