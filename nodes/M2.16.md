# M2.16 Hockey-Stick Identity / 曲棍球恒等式

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.4](M0.4.md)

**Source anchors**: $\sum_{i=r}^{n}\binom{i}{r}=\binom{n+1}{r+1}$.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Sum binomial coefficients along a diagonal of Pascal’s triangle: the hockey-stick identity.

沿帕斯卡三角对角线的二项系数求和。

---

## Step 1: Identity / 第一步：恒等式

$$\sum_{i=r}^{n}\binom{i}{r}=\binom{n+1}{r+1}.$$

---

## Step 2: Combinatorial proof idea / 第二步：组合证明思路

Largest element $m$ in an $(r+1)$-subset of $\{1,\ldots,n+1\}$; rest from $\{1,\ldots,m-1\}$.

---

## Step 3: Hand check / 第三步：手算

$\sum_{i=2}^{4}\binom{i}{2}=10=\binom{5}{3}$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Visual “hockey stick” in Pascal’s triangle.  
2. Full row sum $\sum_i\binom{n}{i}=2^n$ is different.  
3. Useful for cumulative diagonal sums.  
4. Induction is an easy alternative proof.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Off-by-one in limits | $i$ from $r$ to $n$; right side $n+1$ choose $r+1$ |
| Summing $\binom{n}{i}$ over $i$ expecting same form | Different sum |
| Starting $i$ at 0 when $r>0$ | $\binom{i}{r}=0$ for $i<r$ |

---
