# M2.16 Hockey-stick Identity / 组合恒等式 hockey-stick

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.4 Binomial Coefficient Identities](M0.4.md)

---

## Content / 内容

The hockey-stick identity asserts Σ_{k=r}^m C(k,r) = C(m+1,r+1). It converts a sum of binomial coefficients with fixed lower index into a single binomial coefficient. Combined with the binomial theorem it is a standard tool for coefficient extraction. Empty sums are handled by the boundary convention C(n,k)=0 outside the legal range.

Hockey-stick 恒等式：Σ_{k=r}^m C(k,r) = C(m+1,r+1)。它把固定下标的组合数之和变成单个组合数。与二项式定理联用是提取系数的标准工具。空和由边界约定 C(n,k)=0 处理。

Pay careful attention to the lower and upper limits of summation.

注意求和下限与上限。

## Example / 例子

Σ_{k=4}^{10} C(k,4) = C(11,5) = 462.

Σ_{k=4}^{10} C(k,4) = C(11,5) = 462。

## Practice Problem / 练习题

计算 Σ_{k=2}^{6} C(k,2)。
