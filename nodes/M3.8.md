# M3.8 Order-Statistic Expectation, Variance and Combinatorial Sums / 次序统计量期望/方差与组合求和

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M3.7 Maximum PMF](M3.7.md), [M0.4 Binomial Coefficient Identities](M0.4.md)

---

## Content / 内容

With the PMF of the maximum Y one writes E(Y) = Σ y C(y−1,n−1)/C(N,n). The sum is evaluated by means of combinatorial identities (often hockey-stick or the identity relating k C(k,r) to C(k+1,r+1)). Variance follows from E(Y^2) − [E(Y)]^2; sometimes E[Y(Y−1)] is easier to compute first.

有了最大值 Y 的 PMF 后写 E(Y) = Σ y C(y−1,n−1)/C(N,n)。求和借助组合恒等式（常为 hockey-stick 或把 k C(k,r) 变成 C(k+1,r+1) 的恒等式）。方差由 E(Y^2) − [E(Y)]^2 得到；有时先算 E[Y(Y−1)] 更方便。

Pay careful attention to the summation limits. The same technique works for any order statistic whose PMF is expressed by binomial coefficients.

注意求和上下限。同一技巧适用于任何 PMF 由组合数表达的次序统计量。

## Example / 例子

For sampling 2 out of N the expectation of the maximum is (2(N+1))/3.

从 N 中抽 2 个时最大值的期望为 (2(N+1))/3。

## Practice Problem / 练习题

Using the hockey-stick identity or direct summation, verify that Σ_{y=n}^N C(y−1,n−1) = C(N,n).

用 hockey-stick 或直接求和验证 Σ_{y=n}^N C(y−1,n−1) = C(N,n)。

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

Let k = y−1. Sum becomes Σ_{k=n−1}^{N−1} C(k,n−1) = C(N,n) by hockey-stick.

令 k = y−1。和变为 Σ_{k=n−1}^{N−1} C(k,n−1) = C(N,n)（hockey-stick）。

</details>
