# M3.8 Correlation Interpretation / 相关系数解读

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M3.12](M3.12.md)

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

$\rho=\pm 1$ means perfect linear relation; $\rho=0$ means uncorrelated, not necessarily independent.

---

## Practice / 练习

Can $\rho=0$ with nonlinear dependence? Yes.
