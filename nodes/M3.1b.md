# M3.1b Existence of Expectation / 期望存在性

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M3.1](M3.1.md)

**Source anchors**: absolute integrability $\sum|x|p(x)<\infty$ or $\int|x|f(x)\,dx<\infty$.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Before writing $E(X)$, ensure the defining sum/integral **converges absolutely**.

写 $E(X)$ 前需绝对收敛；否则期望可能不存在或为无穷。

---

## Step 1: Absolute convergence / 第一步：绝对收敛

Discrete: $\sum_x |x|p(x)<\infty$. Continuous: $\int|x|f<\infty$.

---

## Step 2: Improper cancellation / 第二步：不当抵消

Positive and negative parts both infinite ⇒ not well-defined as a real.

---

## Step 3: Heavy tails / 第三步：重尾

Some proper distributions have $E|X|=\infty$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Finite $E|X|$ ⇒ $E(X)$ exists and is finite.  
2. Finite mean does not imply finite variance.  
3. Nonnegative $X$: $E(X)\in[0,\infty]$ well-defined.  
4. Check tails before applying mean formulas.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Rearranging conditionally convergent series | Require absolute convergence |
| Assuming every distribution has finite mean | Counterexamples exist |
| Using CLT without finite variance | Stronger moment needs |
