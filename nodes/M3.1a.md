# M3.1a Indicator Expectation / 指示变量期望

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M3.1](M3.1.md), [M4.1](M4.1.md)

**Source anchors**: $E(I_A)=\Pr(A)$; linearity for counts without independence.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Indicator $I_A=1$ on $A$, $0$ else. $E(I_A)=\Pr(A)$. Sum of indicators gives expected counts even when events are dependent.

示性变量：$E(I_A)=\Pr(A)$；示性之和的期望 = 期望计数，不需要独立。

---

## Step 1: Definition / 第一步：定义

$E(I_A)=\Pr(A)$.

---

## Step 2: Linearity / 第二步：线性

$X=\sum I_i$ ⇒ $E(X)=\sum\Pr(A_i)$ regardless of dependence.

---

## Step 3: Hand check / 第三步：手算

Fixed points in random permutation: $E=1$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Indicators turn probability into expectation.  
2. Linearity does not need independence.  
3. Variance of a sum of indicators needs covariances.  
4. Standard tool for matching, occupancy, threshold problems.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Requiring independence for $E\sum I_i$ | Linearity free |
| Using $E(X)=\sum\Pr$ for nonlinear $X$ | Only for sum of indicators |
| Computing Var as if independent | Need $E(I_i I_j)$ |
