# M3.2 LOTUS — Expectation of a Function of X / 随机变量函数的期望

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M3.1 Expectation](M3.1.md)

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

You need $E(X^{2})$, $E(e^{X})$, or $E(g(X))$ for some nonlinear $g$. Finding the full distribution of $Y=g(X)$ first is often harder than necessary. LOTUS says: stay on the original PMF and average $g(x)$.

你需要 $E(X^{2})$、$E(e^{X})$ 或一般 $E(g(X))$。先求 $Y=g(X)$ 的全部分布往往更难。LOTUS：留在原 PMF 上对 $g(x)$ 取平均。

---

## Step 1: Formula / 第一步：公式

For discrete $X$ with PMF $p$,

$$E\bigl(g(X)\bigr)=\sum_{x} g(x)\,p(x)$$

(when the sum converges absolutely). Continuous analogue: $\int g(x)f(x)\,dx$.

---

## Step 2: When not to use LOTUS / 第二步：何时不必用

Linear $g(x)=ax+b$: use linearity $E(aX+b)=aE(X)+b$ directly—no need to expand via LOTUS.

线性函数直接用线性性即可。

---

## Step 3: Hand example / 第三步：手算

$X\in\{-1,0,1\}$ each with probability $1/3$.  
$E(X^{2})=1\cdot\frac13+0+1\cdot\frac13=\frac23$. $E(X)=0$ by symmetry.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

- Computing the law of $g(X)$ when only $E(g(X))$ was asked.  
- Applying $\sum g(x)$ without multiplying by $p(x)$.

---

## Practice / 练习

$p(-2)=1/4$, $p(0)=1/2$, $p(2)=1/4$. Find $E(X^{2})$ and $E(3X^{2}-1)$.

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

$E(X^{2})=4\cdot\frac14+0+4\cdot\frac14=2$.  
$E(3X^{2}-1)=3\cdot 2-1=5$.

</details>
