# M0.5 Series Basics for Probability / 概率用到的级数

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.2 Product and Sum Rules](M0.2.md)

**Source anchors**: geometric series (finite & infinite), exponential series; appear in geometric/Poisson distributions and moment-generating functions.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Geometric and Poisson probabilities, as well as many moment-generating functions, rest on closed forms of power series. We need the geometric sum and the exponential series ready to hand.

几何分布、泊松分布与许多 MGF 都依赖幂级数的闭式。几何级数与指数级数必须熟练。

---

## Step 1: Infinite geometric series / 第一步：无穷几何级数

For $|r|<1$,

$$
\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}r^{k}=\frac{1}{1-r}.
$$

Differentiating (or other standard manipulations) yields

$$
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}kr^{k-1}=\frac{1}{(1-r)^{2}},\qquad\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}kr^{k}=\frac{r}{(1-r)^{2}}.
$$

---

## Step 2: Finite geometric series / 第二步：有限几何级数

$$
\sum_{k=0}^{m}r^{k}=\frac{1-r^{m+1}}{1-r}\quad(r\neq 1).
$$

No convergence restriction is required for a finite sum.

---

## Step 3: Exponential series / 第三步：指数级数

$$
e^{x}=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{x^{k}}{k!}\qquad\text{for all real }x.
$$

This is the Taylor expansion of the exponential; it is the generating function behind the Poisson distribution.

---

## Step 4: Hand checks / 第四步：手算

- $\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}(1/2)^{k}=1/(1-1/2)=2$  
- $\sum_{k=0}^{3}(1/3)^{k}=(1-(1/3)^{4})/(1-1/3)=(1-1/81)/(2/3)=40/27$  
- $e^{0}=1$, coefficient of $x^{2}/2!$ in $e^{x}$ is 1

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Infinite geometric sum needs $|r|<1$; finite sum does not.  
2. Differentiating the geometric series produces the formulas needed for expectation of a geometric random variable.  
3. The exponential series converges everywhere and normalises the Poisson probabilities.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Using $1/(1-r)$ when $|r|\ge 1$ | Check the radius of convergence |
| Confusing the starting index ($k=0$ vs $k=1$) | Write the first term explicitly |
| Forgetting $0!=1$ in the exponential series | Recall the constant term is 1 |

## Practice / 练习（选择题）

**1.** $\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}(1/2)^{k}$ equals

A. 1  
B. 2  
C. $\infty$  
D. $1/2$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$|1/2|<1$, so sum $=1/(1-1/2)=2$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**2.** The finite sum $\sum_{k=0}^{2}r^{k}$ equals

A. $1/(1-r)$  
B. $(1-r^{3})/(1-r)$  
C. $1+r+r^{2}$ only when $|r|<1$  
D. $r^{3}$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Formula for finite geometric sum: $(1-r^{3})/(1-r)$ (valid for $r\neq 1$).  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**3.** The series $\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}x^{k}/k!$ equals

A. $1/(1-x)$  
B. $e^{x}$  
C. $\ln(1-x)$  
D. $1+x$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Definition of the exponential series.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**4.** For which $r$ does $\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}r^{k}$ converge?

A. all real $r$  
B. $|r|<1$  
C. $|r|>1$  
D. $r>0$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Classic radius of convergence of the geometric series.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**5.** $\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}k(1/3)^{k}$ equals

A. $1/2$  
B. $3/4$  
C. $1/3$  
D. $3/2$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Formula $\sum kr^{k}=r/(1-r)^{2}$ with $r=1/3$: $(1/3)/(2/3)^{2}=(1/3)/(4/9)=3/4$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**6.** The constant term in the expansion of $e^{x}$ is

A. 0  
B. 1  
C. $x$  
D. $1/e$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$k=0$ term: $x^{0}/0!=1$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**7.** Finite geometric sum $\sum_{k=0}^{n}1$ (i.e. $r=1$) equals

A. undefined  
B. $n+1$  
C. $1/(1-1)$  
D. 0

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

When $r=1$ the original formula is invalid; direct count gives $n+1$ terms each equal to 1.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>
