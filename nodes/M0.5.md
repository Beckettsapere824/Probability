**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.2](M0.2.md)

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Geometric and exponential series appear in geometric/Poisson distributions and MGFs.

几何/泊松分布与 MGF 会用到几何级数与指数级数。

---

## Step 1: Geometric series / 几何级数

$\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} r^{k}=1/(1-r)$ for $|r|<1$. Finite: $\sum_{k=0}^{n} r^{k}=(1-r^{n+1})/(1-r)$.

---

## Step 2: Exponential / 指数

$e^{x}=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} x^{k}/k!$.

---
