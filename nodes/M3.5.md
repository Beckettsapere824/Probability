# M3.5 Standardization / 标准化

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M3.4](M3.4.md)

**Source anchors**: $Z=(X-\mu)/\sigma$; mean 0, variance 1.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Centre and scale so the result has mean 0 and variance 1 (when $\sigma>0$). Enables comparison across units and standard tables.

中心化并缩放，使均值为 0、方差为 1。

---

## Step 1: Definition / 第一步：定义

$$Z=\frac{X-\mu}{\sigma},\quad \mu=E(X),\ \sigma=\sqrt{\mathrm{Var}(X)}>0.$$

---

## Step 2: Moments / 第二步：矩

$E(Z)=0$, $\mathrm{Var}(Z)=1$.

---

## Step 3: Inversion / 第三步：反解

$X=\mu+\sigma Z$. $\{X\le x\}=\{Z\le(x-\mu)/\sigma\}$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Standardization is affine with $a=1/\sigma$, $b=-\mu/\sigma$.  
2. Does not make $Z$ normal unless $X$ is normal.  
3. Essential for normal table look-ups.  
4. Same shape, different location/scale.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Claiming $Z\sim N(0,1)$ always | Only if $X$ normal |
| Using sample $s$ without noting estimation | Population $\sigma$ here |
| $\sigma=0$ | Cannot divide |
