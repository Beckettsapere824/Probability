# M3.7 Covariance Computational Form / 协方差计算式

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M3.12](M3.12.md)

**Source anchors**: $\mathrm{Cov}(X,Y)=E(XY)-\mu_X\mu_Y$.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Compute covariance via $E(XY)-\mu_X\mu_Y$ instead of expanding $(X-\mu_X)(Y-\mu_Y)$ term by term.

用 $E(XY)-\mu_X\mu_Y$ 算协方差。

---

## Step 1: Identity / 第一步：恒等式

$$\mathrm{Cov}(X,Y)=E(XY)-\mu_X\mu_Y.$$

---

## Step 2: From a table / 第二步：从表

$E(X)$, $E(Y)$, $E(XY)=\sum\sum xy\,p(x,y)$, then subtract.

---

## Step 3: Special cases / 第三步：特例

$\mathrm{Cov}(X,X)=\mathrm{Var}(X)$. Independent ⇒ Cov $=0$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Computational form is algebraically identical.  
2. Uncorrelated does not imply independent.  
3. Bilinearity: $\mathrm{Cov}(aX+b,cY+d)=ac\,\mathrm{Cov}(X,Y)$.  
4. Sign tracks co-movement direction.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Forgetting to subtract $\mu_X\mu_Y$ | Full formula |
| Claiming Cov $=0$ ⇒ independent | Only one direction |
| Mixing $E(XY)$ with $E(X)E(Y)$ always | Needs uncorrelated/indep |

---
