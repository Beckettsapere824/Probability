# M7.2 Ball-Drawing Prize PMF and CDF / 抽球奖金 PMF/CDF

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M2.9 Multivariate Hypergeometric](M2.9.md), [M1.8 Random Variables](M1.8.md), [M1.8a Merging Equal Values](M1.8a.md), [M4.7 Hypergeometric](M4.7.md)

---

## Content / 内容

Two balls are drawn without replacement from an urn containing white, black and orange balls. A prize random variable X is defined by the colour combination obtained. The probability of each combination is the corresponding hypergeometric probability (product of binomial coefficients over C(N,2)). Different combinations that yield the same numerical prize must have their probabilities merged before the PMF is written. The CDF is the cumulative sum of the ordered PMF values and is expressed as a step function. Always verify that the PMF sums to 1.

从含白、黑、橙球的瓮中无放回抽两球。奖金随机变量 X 由所得颜色组合定义。每种组合的概率是相应的超几何概率（组合数乘积除以 C(N,2)）。产生相同数值奖金的不同组合必须在写 PMF 前合并概率。CDF 是有序 PMF 值的累加和，表示为阶梯函数。始终检查 PMF 和为 1。

## Example / 例子

12 balls, 5 white, 4 black, 3 orange. P(two white)=C(5,2)/C(12,2). If two white and one white-one black both map to the same prize, add their probabilities.

12 球，5 白 4 黑 3 橙。P(两白)=C(5,2)/C(12,2)。若两白与一白一黑映射到同一奖金，则把它们的概率相加。

## Practice Problem / 练习题

Urn: 3 red, 3 blue. Draw 2 without replacement. Let X = number of red balls. Write the PMF of X.

瓮：3 红 3 蓝。无放回抽 2。设 X = 红球数。写出 X 的 PMF。
