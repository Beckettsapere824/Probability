# M6.2 Stationary Distribution / 平稳分布

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M6.1](M6.1.md)

**Source anchors**: $\pi P=\pi$, $\sum\pi_i=1$; long-run frequency; two-state closed form.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Find a distribution $\pi$ that does not change under one step: the **stationary** (invariant) distribution. Under mild conditions it is also the long-run proportion of time in each state.

找一步不变的分布 $\pi$（平稳分布）；温和条件下也是长期时间比例。

---

## Step 1: Equations / 第一步：方程

$$\pi P=\pi,\qquad \sum_i\pi_i=1,\qquad \pi_i\ge 0.$$

For two-state $P$ with $\alpha+\beta>0$:

$$\pi_0=\frac{\beta}{\alpha+\beta},\qquad \pi_1=\frac{\alpha}{\alpha+\beta}.$$

---

## Step 2: Interpretation / 第二步：解释

Balance: flow $0\to 1$ equals flow $1\to 0$ in stationarity: $\pi_0\alpha=\pi_1\beta$.

---

## Step 3: Hand check / 第三步：手算

$\alpha=0.3$, $\beta=0.2$ ⇒ $\pi=(0.4,0.6)$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Stationarity = eigenvector of $P^\top$ for eigenvalue 1 (row form: left eigenvector).  
2. Normalise to a probability vector.  
3. Two-state: mass proportional to the *exit rate of the other* state.  
4. Existence uniqueness needs irreducibility / aperiodicity for limit theorems.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Forgetting $\sum\pi=1$ | Always normalise |
| Swapping $\alpha$ and $\beta$ in the formula | $\pi_0\propto\beta$ |
| Claiming every chain has a unique stationary law | Need conditions |

## Practice / 练习（选择题）

**1.** Stationary $\pi$ satisfies

A. $\pi=P$  
B. $\pi P=\pi$ and $\sum\pi_i=1$  
C. $\pi=0$  
D. $\alpha=\beta$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Definition.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**2.** Two-state, $\alpha=0.3$, $\beta=0.2$. $\pi_0=$

A. $0.3$  
B. $0.4$  
C. $0.6$  
D. $0.5$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$\beta/(\alpha+\beta)=0.2/0.5=0.4$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**3.** Balance equation $\pi_0\alpha=\pi_1\beta$ means

A. no transitions  
B. probability flux $0\to1$ equals $1\to0$  
C. $\alpha=\beta$  
D. absorbing

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Global balance for two states.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**4.** If $\alpha=\beta=0.5$, $\pi=$

A. $(1,0)$  
B. $(0.5,0.5)$  
C. $(0,1)$  
D. undefined

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Symmetric.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**5.** $\pi_1=\alpha/(\alpha+\beta)$ is

A. always $1$  
B. the stationary mass on state 1  
C. the transition $P_{01}$  
D. $\beta$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Closed form.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**6.** Without normalising $\sum\pi=1$, solutions of $\pi P=\pi$ are

A. unique probability vectors  
B. positive multiples of the stationary law (if unique direction)  
C. always zero  
D. rows of $P$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Linear eigenspace.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**7.** Long-run frequency interpretation requires roughly

A. only $\alpha=0$  
B. irreducibility (and often aperiodicity for convergence)  
C. $\pi=(1,0)$  
D. finite $t$ only

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Ergodic conditions.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**8.** If $\beta=0$ and $\alpha>0$, stationary mass sits on

A. state 0 only  
B. state 1  
C. both equally  
D. nowhere

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

All mass drains to 1.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>
