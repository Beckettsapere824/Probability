## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Many measurements cluster around a centre with roughly symmetric spread—washer thickness, exam scores, noise. We need a continuous law with two knobs (location, scale), a known mgf, and **tables** for interval probabilities.

许多测量值围着中心、大致对称散开——垫片厚度、分数、噪声。需要一个有位置与尺度两个旋钮、mgf 已知、且能用**表**算区间概率的连续律。

That law is the **normal**. Exam work almost always reduces to the **standard** normal $Z\sim N(0,1)$ via standardization.

这就是**正态**。考试计算几乎总是通过标准化回到 **标准正态** $Z\sim N(0,1)$。

---

## Step 1: Definition 5.8 / 第一步：定义

$$
f(x)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\sigma^{2}}}\exp\Bigl(-\frac{(x-\mu)^{2}}{2\sigma^{2}}\Bigr), \quad x\in\mathbb{R},
$$

with $\sigma>0$. Write $X\sim N(\mu,\sigma^{2})$.

$$
E(X)=\mu, \quad \mathrm{Var}(X)=\sigma^{2}, \quad
M_{X}(t)=\exp\bigl(\mu t+\tfrac12\sigma^{2}t^{2}\bigr).
$$

Bell-shaped, symmetric about $\mu$. Rough area rules from the notes: about 68% in $\mu\pm\sigma$, 95% in $\mu\pm 2\sigma$, 99.7% in $\mu\pm 3\sigma$.

---

## Step 2: Standardization / 第二步：标准化

$$
Z=\frac{X-\mu}{\sigma}\sim N(0,1).
$$

CDF of $Z$ is $\Phi(z)=\Pr(Z\le z)$. Course table lists $\Phi(z)$ for $z\ge 0$; use

$$
\Phi(-z)=1-\Phi(z).
$$

Any interval probability becomes a difference of $\Phi$ values:

$$
\Pr(a\le X\le b)=\Phi\Bigl(\frac{b-\mu}{\sigma}\Bigr)-\Phi\Bigl(\frac{a-\mu}{\sigma}\Bigr).
$$

---

## Step 3: Symmetry / 第三步：对称性

$$
\Pr(X\le \mu-x)=\Pr(X\ge \mu+x).
$$

Steepest slope of the density is at $\mu\pm\sigma$.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

- Writing $N(\mu,\sigma)$ when the book’s second parameter is **variance** $\sigma^{2}$.  
- Using the table without standardizing.  
- Right-tail mistakes: forget $1-\Phi$.

---

## Practice / 练习

$X\sim N(10,4)$ (variance 4). Express $\Pr(8<X<14)$ in terms of $\Phi$.

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

$\sigma=2$.  
$\Pr(-1<Z<2)=\Phi(2)-\Phi(-1)=\Phi(2)-(1-\Phi(1))=\Phi(2)+\Phi(1)-1$.

</details>
