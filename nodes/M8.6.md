# M8.6 Normal Distribution / 正态分布

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M8.1 Continuous RV and pdf](M8.1.md)

**Source anchors**: SDST2601A Ch5 Def 5.8; standard normal table; symmetry; 68–95–99.7; linear transforms stay normal.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Many measurements cluster around a centre with roughly symmetric spread — thickness, exam scores, noise, measurement error. We need a continuous two-parameter family with known mean/variance, a usable mgf, and **tables** for interval probabilities. That family is the **normal**. Exam calculation almost always reduces to the **standard** normal $Z\sim N(0,1)$ via standardization.

许多测量围着中心、大致对称散开。需要有均值/方差、可用 mgf、且能查**表**的两参数连续族。这就是**正态**。考试计算几乎总是通过标准化回到 **标准正态** $Z\sim N(0,1)$。

---

## Step 1: Density and parameters / 第一步：密度与参数

$$f(x)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}\,\sigma}\exp\Bigl(-\frac{(x-\mu)^{2}}{2\sigma^{2}}\Bigr), \qquad x\in\mathbb{R},\quad \sigma>0.$$

Write $X\sim N(\mu,\sigma^{2})$.  
**Notation trap:** the second parameter in this course is **variance** $\sigma^{2}$, not the standard deviation. $N(0,4)$ has $\sigma=2$.

$$E(X)=\mu, \qquad \mathrm{Var}(X)=\sigma^{2}, \qquad M_X(t)=\exp\bigl(\mu t+\tfrac12\sigma^{2}t^{2}\bigr).$$

Bell-shaped, symmetric about $\mu$. Rough empirical rules: about 68% of mass in $\mu\pm\sigma$, 95% in $\mu\pm 2\sigma$, 99.7% in $\mu\pm 3\sigma$.

第二参数是**方差** $\sigma^{2}$。约 68%/95%/99.7% 落在 $\mu\pm\sigma/2\sigma/3\sigma$。

---

## Step 2: Standardization / 第二步：标准化

$$Z=\frac{X-\mu}{\sigma}\sim N(0,1).$$

CDF of $Z$ is $\Phi(z)=\Pr(Z\le z)$. Course tables list $\Phi(z)$ for $z\ge 0$; use symmetry

$$\Phi(-z)=1-\Phi(z).$$

Any interval becomes a difference of $\Phi$ values:

$$\Pr(a\le X\le b)=\Phi\Bigl(\frac{b-\mu}{\sigma}\Bigr)-\Phi\Bigl(\frac{a-\mu}{\sigma}\Bigr).$$

**Exam rhythm:** (1) standardize both endpoints, (2) apply $\Phi(-z)=1-\Phi(z)$ if needed, (3) subtract.

考试节奏：两端标准化 → 负号用 $1-\Phi$ → 相减。

---

## Step 3: Symmetry and table tricks / 第三步：对称与查表技巧

- $\Pr(X\le \mu-x)=\Pr(X\ge \mu+x)$.  
- $\Pr(|Z|\le z)=2\Phi(z)-1$.  
- $\Pr(Z>z)=1-\Phi(z)$ (right tail).

Steepest slope of the density is at $\mu\pm\sigma$.

---

## Step 4: Linear transforms stay normal / 第四步：线性变换仍正态

If $X\sim N(\mu,\sigma^{2})$ then $aX+b\sim N(a\mu+b,\,a^{2}\sigma^{2})$ for $a\neq 0$.  
Sum of independent normals is normal (means add, variances add).  
These facts are used constantly in sampling distributions and later inference.

$X$ 正态 ⇒ $aX+b$ 正态；独立正态之和仍正态（均值、方差分别相加）。

---

## Step 5: Hand example / 第五步：手算

$X\sim N(10,4)$ (variance 4, so $\sigma=2$).  
$\Pr(8<X<14)=\Pr\bigl(\frac{8-10}{2}<Z<\frac{14-10}{2}\bigr)=\Pr(-1<Z<2)$  
$=\Phi(2)-\Phi(-1)=\Phi(2)-(1-\Phi(1))=\Phi(2)+\Phi(1)-1$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Normal = location $\mu$ + scale $\sigma$; second parameter is variance.  
2. Every normal probability reduces to $\Phi$ after standardization.  
3. Symmetry $\Phi(-z)=1-\Phi(z)$ is half the table work.  
4. Linear images and independent sums of normals remain normal.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Writing $N(\mu,\sigma)$ when the book uses variance | $N(\mu,\sigma^{2})$; $\sigma$ is SD |
| Forgetting to standardize before using the table | Always $(x-\mu)/\sigma$ |
| Right-tail: using $\Phi(z)$ instead of $1-\Phi(z)$ | Tail = $1-\Phi$ |
| $\Phi(-z)=\Phi(z)$ | No: $\Phi(-z)=1-\Phi(z)$ |

---

## Worked exam-style example / 考试风格例题

**Problem.** $X\sim N(10,4)$. Express $\Pr(8<X<14)$ in terms of $\Phi$.

**Solution.** $\sigma=2$. $\Phi(2)+\Phi(1)-1$ (as above).

**Another.** $Z\sim N(0,1)$. $\Pr(|Z|>1.96)=2(1-\Phi(1.96))$ (two-sided critical value pattern).

## Practice / 练习

1. $X\sim N(10,4)$. $\Pr(8<X<14)$ in terms of $\Phi$.

2. Why is $\Phi(-z)=1-\Phi(z)$?

3. $X\sim N(\mu,\sigma^{2})$. Law of $(X-\mu)/\sigma$?

<details>
<summary>Solution sketches / 解答提示</summary>

1. $\Phi(2)+\Phi(1)-1$.

2. Symmetry of $N(0,1)$ density about 0: left tail mass equals right tail mass.

3. $N(0,1)$.

</details>
