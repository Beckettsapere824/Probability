# M8.9 Survival Function and Hazard Rate / 生存函数与风险率

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M8.1 Continuous RV and pdf](M8.1.md), [M8.4 Exponential](M8.4.md)

**Source / 来源**: SDST2601A Chapter 6 §6.1 Definition 6.1, Examples 6.1–6.2

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

For a lifetime $X>0$, cdf $F(t)=\Pr(X\le t)$ answers “failed by time $t$”. Engineers and actuaries often want the complementary question: “still alive at $t$?”, and the instantaneous risk of failing right after having survived to $t$.

寿命 $X>0$ 时，cdf 回答「在 $t$ 前已失效」。工程与精算更常问：「$t$ 时刻仍存活？」以及「活到 $t$ 之后立刻失效的瞬时强度」。

Those are the **survival function** $S$ and the **hazard rate** $\lambda$.

这就是**生存函数** $S$ 与**风险率** $\lambda$。

---

## Step 1: Definition 6.1 / 第一步：定义

$$
S(t)=\Pr(X>t)=1-F(t).
$$

$$
\lambda(t)=\frac{f(t)}{S(t)}\quad (S(t)>0).
$$

Interpretation: $\lambda(t)\,dt \approx \Pr(t<X<t+dt\mid X>t)$ — conditional failure intensity for a $t$-unit-old item.

解释：$\lambda(t)\,dt$ 近似「已活到 $t$ 的个体在接下来 $dt$ 内失效」的条件概率强度。

---

## Step 2: Hazard determines the law / 第二步：风险率决定分布

$$
\lambda(t)=-\frac{d}{dt}\ln S(t)
\implies
F(x)=1-\exp\Bigl(-\int_{0}^{x}\lambda(t)\,dt\Bigr)\quad (x>0).
$$

So specifying a hazard path is another way to specify $F$.

指定风险率路径等价于指定 $F$。

Four functions linked: $F$, $S=1-F$, $f=F'$, $\lambda=f/S$.

---

## Step 3: Exponential = constant hazard / 第三步：指数 = 常数风险

For $X\sim\mathrm{Exp}(\lambda)$ (rate), $S(t)=e^{-\lambda t}$ and $\lambda(t)\equiv\lambda$ (constant). That is why residual life forgets age—hazard does not grow with $t$.

指数的风险率为常数，所以残寿与年龄无关。

Example 6.2 in the notes uses increasing hazard $\lambda(t)=a+bt$ (older items more fragile); $a=0$ recovers a Rayleigh law.

课件例 6.2 用递增风险 $\lambda(t)=a+bt$；$a=0$ 时为 Rayleigh。

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

- Confusing $S(t)=\Pr(X>t)$ with $F(t)$.  
- Calling $f(t)$ itself the “failure rate” (the failure rate is $\lambda=f/S$).  
- Assuming every lifetime is memoryless (only constant hazard is).

---

## Practice / 练习

$X\sim\mathrm{Exp}(\lambda=0.5)$ (rate). Write $S(t)$ and $\lambda(t)$. Compute $\Pr(X>4\mid X>2)$.

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

$S(t)=e^{-0.5 t}$, $\lambda(t)=0.5$ constant.  
Memoryless: $\Pr(X>4\mid X>2)=\Pr(X>2)=e^{-1}$.

</details>
