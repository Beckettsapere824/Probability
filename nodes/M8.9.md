# M8.9 Survival Function and Hazard Rate / 生存函数与风险率

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M8.1 Continuous RV and pdf](M8.1.md), [M8.4 Exponential](M8.4.md)

**Source anchors**: SDST2601A Ch6 §6.1 Def 6.1, Examples 6.1–6.2; constant hazard ⇔ exponential; increasing hazard models.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

For a lifetime $X>0$, the cdf $F(t)=\Pr(X\le t)$ answers “failed by time $t$”. Engineers and actuaries often want the complementary question: “still alive at $t$?”, and the instantaneous risk of failing right after having survived to $t$.

寿命 $X>0$ 时，cdf 回答「在 $t$ 前已失效」。工程与精算更常问：「$t$ 时刻仍存活？」以及「活到 $t$ 之后立刻失效的瞬时强度」。

Those are the **survival function** $S$ and the **hazard rate** $\lambda$.

这就是**生存函数** $S$ 与**风险率** $\lambda$。

---

## Step 1: Definitions / 第一步：定义

$$S(t)=\Pr(X>t)=1-F(t).$$

$$\lambda(t)=\frac{f(t)}{S(t)}\quad\text{when }S(t)>0.$$

Interpretation: $\lambda(t)\,dt\approx\Pr(t<X<t+dt\mid X>t)$ — the conditional failure intensity for an item that has already survived to age $t$.

$\lambda(t)\,dt$ 近似「已活到 $t$ 的个体在接下来 $dt$ 内失效」的条件概率强度。

---

## Step 2: Hazard determines the law / 第二步：风险率决定分布

From $\lambda(t)=-\dfrac{d}{dt}\ln S(t)$ one recovers

$$S(x)=\exp\Bigl(-\int_0^{x}\lambda(t)\,dt\Bigr), \qquad F(x)=1-S(x)\quad(x>0).$$

So specifying a hazard path is another complete way to specify the lifetime distribution. Four linked objects: $F$, $S=1-F$, $f=F'$, $\lambda=f/S$.

指定风险率路径等价于指定寿命分布。$F,S,f,\lambda$ 四者互通。

---

## Step 3: Exponential = constant hazard / 第三步：指数 = 常数风险

For $X\sim\mathrm{Exp}(\lambda)$ (rate parameter),

$$S(t)=e^{-\lambda t}, \qquad \lambda(t)\equiv\lambda\quad\text{(constant)}.$$

Constant hazard is exactly why residual life forgets age — the instantaneous risk does not grow with $t$. Among continuous lifetimes on $(0,\infty)$, **only** the exponential has constant hazard (equivalently, only exponential is memoryless).

常数风险 ⇔ 无记忆。连续寿命中**只有**指数具有常数风险。

---

## Step 4: Non-constant hazards (exam flavour) / 第四步：非常数风险

Increasing hazard $\lambda(t)=a+bt$ ($b>0$): older items more fragile. Integrating gives

$$S(x)=\exp\bigl(-(ax+b x^{2}/2)\bigr).$$

When $a=0$, $\lambda(t)=bt$ recovers a Rayleigh-type law. Decreasing hazards model “infant mortality” (high early risk that declines).

递增风险：越老越脆；递减风险：早期高风险后下降（婴儿死亡型）。

---

## Step 5: Hand example / 第五步：手算

$X\sim\mathrm{Exp}(\lambda=0.5)$ (rate).  
$S(t)=e^{-0.5 t}$, $\lambda(t)=0.5$ constant.  
$\Pr(X>4\mid X>2)=\Pr(X>2)=e^{-1}$ by memorylessness (or direct: $S(4)/S(2)=e^{-2}/e^{-1}=e^{-1}$).

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. $S(t)=\Pr(\text{still alive at }t)$; $\lambda(t)=$ instantaneous risk given survival to $t$.  
2. Hazard path determines $F$ completely via the exponential integral formula.  
3. Constant hazard ⇔ exponential ⇔ memoryless.  
4. Increasing / decreasing hazard = aging / infant-mortality shapes.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Confusing $S(t)$ with $F(t)$ | $S=1-F$ |
| Calling $f(t)$ the “failure rate” | Failure rate / hazard is $\lambda=f/S$ |
| Assuming every lifetime is memoryless | Only constant hazard |
| Integrating $\lambda$ from $1$ instead of $0$ | Survival from birth uses $\int_0^{x}$ |

---

## Worked exam-style example / 考试风格例题

**Problem.** $X\sim\mathrm{Exp}(0.5)$ (rate). Write $S(t)$ and $\lambda(t)$. Compute $\Pr(X>4\mid X>2)$.

**Solution.** $S(t)=e^{-0.5t}$, $\lambda(t)=0.5$. $\Pr=e^{-1}$.

---

## Practice / 练习

1. If $\lambda(t)=2$ for all $t>0$, what is the law of $X$?

2. Express $f$ in terms of $S$ and $\lambda$.

3. Why does increasing hazard destroy memorylessness?

<details>
<summary>Solution sketches / 解答提示</summary>

1. $\mathrm{Exp}(\text{rate }2)$.

2. $f=\lambda\cdot S$.

3. Residual risk depends on current age; the future is not a fresh copy of $X$.

</details>
