# M1.6 Independence / 独立性

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M1.3 Conditional Probability and Multiplication Rule](M1.3.md)

---

## Content / 内容

Two events are independent when P(A ∩ B) = P(A) P(B). Equivalently P(A|B) = P(A) whenever P(B) > 0. A finite collection is mutually independent when every subcollection satisfies the product rule for its intersection.

两事件独立当且仅当 P(A ∩ B) = P(A) P(B)。等价地，当 P(B) > 0 时 P(A|B) = P(A)。有限个事件相互独立，当且仅当任意子族的交概率等于各自概率的乘积。

Independence and mutual exclusivity are almost incompatible for events of positive probability: if A and B are both non-empty of positive probability then they cannot be both independent and disjoint. For independent events the union formula simplifies to P(A ∪ B) = 1 - (1-P(A))(1-P(B)).

独立与互斥对正概率事件几乎不相容：若 A、B 都有正概率，则它们不可能既独立又互斥。独立事件的并公式简化为 P(A ∪ B) = 1 - (1-P(A))(1-P(B))。

Joint probabilities of independent repeated trials are obtained by multiplying the individual probabilities.

独立重复试验的联合概率由各项概率相乘得到。

## Example / 例子

Two independent fair coin tosses. P(both heads) = (1/2)·(1/2) = 1/4. P(at least one head) = 1 - (1/2)·(1/2) = 3/4.

两次独立公平抛币。P(两次正面) = (1/2)·(1/2) = 1/4。P(至少一次正面) = 1 - (1/2)·(1/2) = 3/4。

## Practice Problem / 练习题

Events A and B are independent with P(A) = 0.3, P(B) = 0.4. Compute P(A ∪ B) and P(A^c ∩ B).

事件 A、B 独立，P(A) = 0.3，P(B) = 0.4。求 P(A ∪ B) 和 P(A^c ∩ B)。

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

P(A ∪ B) = 0.3 + 0.4 - 0.3·0.4 = 0.58. P(A^c ∩ B) = (1-0.3)·0.4 = 0.28.

P(A ∪ B) = 0.3 + 0.4 - 0.3·0.4 = 0.58。P(A^c ∩ B) = (1-0.3)·0.4 = 0.28。

</details>
