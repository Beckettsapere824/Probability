# M1.6 Independence of Events / 事件独立性

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M1.3 Conditional Probability](M1.3.md)

**Source / 来源**: SDST2601A Chapter 3 (independence after conditional probability)

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Sometimes learning that $B$ occurred does **not** change the odds of $A$. That is a modelling assumption you must state—and it unlocks product formulas for joint events and repeated trials.

有时知道 $B$ 发生了**并不会**改变 $A$ 的机会。这是需要写明的建模假设，并由此得到交事件与重复试验的乘积公式。

---

## Step 1: Definition / 第一步：定义

Events $A,B$ are **independent** when

$$\Pr(A\cap B)=\Pr(A)\Pr(B).$$

If $\Pr(B)>0$, this is equivalent to $\Pr(A\mid B)=\Pr(A)$ (and likewise with roles reversed).

若 $\Pr(B)>0$，等价于 $\Pr(A\mid B)=\Pr(A)$。

---

## Step 2: Mutual independence / 第二步：相互独立

A finite family is **mutually independent** when every subcollection’s intersection factors into the product of the individual probabilities—not only pairs.

有限族**相互独立**要求任意子族的交都等于各自概率之积，而不只是两两。

Pairwise independence does **not** imply mutual independence (standard counter-examples exist).

两两独立**不蕴含**相互独立。

---

## Step 3: Independence ≠ disjointness / 第三步：独立 ≠ 互斥

If $\Pr(A)>0$, $\Pr(B)>0$ and $A\cap B=\emptyset$, then $\Pr(A\cap B)=0\neq\Pr(A)\Pr(B)$. Positive-probability events cannot be both independent and disjoint.

正概率事件不能既独立又互斥。

For independent $A,B$:

$$\Pr(A\cup B)=1-(1-\Pr(A))(1-\Pr(B)).$$

---

## Step 4: Repeated trials / 第四步：重复试验

Independent trials: multiply trial probabilities to get the probability of a specified sequence (e.g. HHTH for fair coins: $(1/2)^4$).

独立试验：指定序列的概率 = 各次概率相乘。

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

- Confusing independence with mutual exclusivity.  
- Checking only pairs when a problem needs mutual independence.  
- Assuming independence without justification in the model.

---

## Practice / 练习

Fair die. $A=$“even”, $B=$“$\ge 4$”. Are $A$ and $B$ independent?

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

$\Pr(A)=1/2$, $\Pr(B)=1/2$, $\Pr(A\cap B)=\Pr(\{4,6\})=1/3\neq 1/4$. Not independent.

</details>
