# M7.12 Couples Non-adjacent via Inclusion-Exclusion / 夫妻不相邻容斥

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M2.10 Pairings](M2.10.md), [M1.2 Inclusion-Exclusion](M1.2.md), [M0.3 Combinations](M0.3.md)

---

## Content / 内容

Four married couples are seated in a row. Let E_i be the event that couple i sits side by side. The probability that no couple sits together is
P(∩ E_i^c)=1−Σ P(E_i)+Σ P(E_i∩E_j)−…
by inclusion-exclusion. Treating a couple that sits together as a single super person reduces the number of entities to be permuted and multiplies by 2 for the internal order of that couple. The total number of unrestricted seatings is 8!. The signs of the inclusion-exclusion terms alternate.

四对夫妻排成一行。设 E_i 为第 i 对坐在一起的事件。无夫妻相邻的概率由容斥给出：
P(∩ E_i^c)=1−Σ P(E_i)+Σ P(E_i∩E_j)−…
把坐在一起的夫妻视为单个超级人，减少待排列实体数，并乘以 2 计入该夫妻的内部顺序。无限制座位总数为 8!。容斥各项符号交替。

## Example / 例子

P(E_1)= (2·7!)/8! = 2/8 = 1/4 (treat the couple as a block). Higher-order terms follow the same block-counting pattern.

P(E_1)= (2·7!)/8! = 1/4（把该对视为块）。高阶项遵循同一分块计数模式。

## Practice Problem / 练习题

Two couples (4 people) sit in a row. Find the probability that neither couple sits together.

两对夫妻（4 人）坐成一行。求无夫妻相邻的概率。

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

Total 4!=24. P(E_1)=2·3!/24=1/2, similarly P(E_2)=1/2, P(E_1∩E_2)=2·2·2!/24=1/3. Inclusion-exclusion: 1−1/2−1/2+1/3=1/3.

总数 4!=24。P(E_1)=2·3!/24=1/2，同理 P(E_2)=1/2，P(E_1∩E_2)=2·2·2!/24=1/3。容斥：1−1/2−1/2+1/3=1/3。

</details>
