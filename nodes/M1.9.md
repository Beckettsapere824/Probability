# M1.9 Classical Probability and Equiprobable Outcomes / 古典概型与等可能结果

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.3 Factorials, Permutations, Combinations](M0.3.md), [M1.1 Sample Space, Events, Probability Axioms](M1.1.md)

---

## Content / 内容

Under the classical model every outcome in a finite sample space is equally likely, so P(A) = |A| / |Ω|. The sample space itself may be ordered or unordered; the numerator and the denominator must be counted with the same convention.

古典概型下有限样本空间中每个结果等可能，故 P(A) = |A| / |Ω|。样本空间本身可以有序或无序；分子分母必须用同一口径计数。

Complex events are decomposed into simpler ones that are easier to count. The equiprobability assumption must be stated explicitly; a loaded die cannot be treated by simply counting faces.

复杂事件先分解成更易计数的简单事件。等可能假设必须明确写出；非均匀骰子不能直接数面数。

## Example / 例子

Two fair dice. Probability that the sum is 7: there are 6 ordered pairs out of 36, so 6/36 = 1/6. Probability that the two faces are different: 30/36 = 5/6.

两枚公平骰子。和为 7 的概率：36 个有序对中有 6 个，故 6/36 = 1/6。两面不同的概率：30/36 = 5/6。

## Practice Problem / 练习题

A fair coin is tossed three times. Using an ordered sample space of size 8, find the probability of exactly two heads.

公平硬币抛三次。用大小为 8 的有序样本空间，求恰好两次正面的概率。

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

Favorable outcomes: HHT, HTH, THH (3 of them). Probability 3/8.

有利结果：HHT, HTH, THH（3 个）。概率 3/8。

</details>
