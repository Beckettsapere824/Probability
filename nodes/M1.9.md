# M1.9 Classical Equiprobable Model / 古典概型

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M1.1](M1.1.md), [M0.3](M0.3.md)

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

When every outcome is equally likely, probability reduces to counting.

等可能时，概率化为计数。

---

## Formula / 公式

$\Pr(A)=|A|/|\Omega|$ on finite $\Omega$ with uniform outcomes.

**Critical:** choose $\Omega$ so symmetry is real (ordered vs unordered).

---

## Practice / 练习

Two fair coins. $\Pr(\text{two heads})$ with $\Omega=\{HH,HT,TH,TT\}$?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>$1/4$.</details>
