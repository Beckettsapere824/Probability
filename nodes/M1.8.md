# M1.8 Discrete Random Variables, PMF, CDF / 离散随机变量、PMF、CDF

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M1.1 Sample Space, Events, Axioms](M1.1.md)

**Source anchors**: SDST2601A Ch4 discrete RV; bridge to continuous [M8.1](M8.1.md); endpoint care in $F(b)-F(a)$.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Events are subsets of $\Omega$. We often care about a **number** attached to each outcome — sum of two dice, number of defectives. A **random variable** packages that number so we can talk about its distribution once.

事件是 $\Omega$ 的子集。我们常常关心每个结果上的**数字**。**随机变量**把该数字打包，从而一次说清它的分布。

---

## Step 1: Discrete random variable / 第一步：离散随机变量

A discrete random variable $X$ is a function $X:\Omega\to\mathbb{R}$ whose range is **countable**. Events become $\{X=x\}$, $\{X\le x\}$, $\{X\in A\}$.

离散随机变量：值域可数的 $X:\Omega\to\mathbb{R}$。

---

## Step 2: Probability mass function (PMF) / 第二步：概率质量函数

$$p_X(x)=\Pr(X=x).$$

Requirements: $p_X(x)\ge 0$, $\sum_x p_X(x)=1$.  
$\Pr(X\in A)=\sum_{x\in A}p_X(x)$.

---

## Step 3: Cumulative distribution function (CDF) / 第三步：分布函数

$$F_X(x)=\Pr(X\le x)=\sum_{u\le x}p_X(u).$$

Properties: non-decreasing, right-continuous, $F(-\infty)=0$, $F(+\infty)=1$, jump at $x$ equals $p_X(x)$.

**Interval rule:** $\Pr(a<X\le b)=F(b)-F(a)$. Watch endpoints when masses sit on $a$ or $b$.

---

## Step 4: Hand example / 第四步：手算

$p(0)=1/4$, $p(1)=1/2$, $p(2)=1/4$.

| Range | $F(x)$ |
|-------|--------|
| $x<0$ | $0$ |
| $0\le x<1$ | $1/4$ |
| $1\le x<2$ | $3/4$ |
| $x\ge 2$ | $1$ |

$\Pr(0<X\le 1)=F(1)-F(0)=1/2$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. RV = number attached to each outcome; discrete = countable range.  
2. PMF puts mass on points; must sum to 1.  
3. CDF accumulates from the left; jumps recover the PMF.  
4. Endpoint care: $F(b)-F(a)$ is $\Pr(a<X\le b)$.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Writing $p(x)$ for continuous $X$ | Use density $f$ |
| $\Pr(X<b)$ vs $\Pr(X\le b)$ at a mass point | Differ by $p(b)$ |
| PMF not summing to 1 | Always check normalisation |
| Treating $F(x)=\Pr(X<x)$ | Standard is $\le$ |

## Practice / 练习（选择题）

**1.** $p(1)=0.2$, $p(2)=0.5$, $p(3)=0.3$. $F(2.5)=$

A. $0.2$  
B. $0.5$  
C. $0.7$  
D. $1$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$F(2.5)=p(1)+p(2)=0.7$.  
Answer: **C**.
</details>

**2.** Same PMF. $\Pr(X\ge 2)=$

A. $0.2$  
B. $0.5$  
C. $0.8$  
D. $1$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$p(2)+p(3)=0.8$.  
Answer: **C**.
</details>

**3.** A discrete PMF must satisfy

A. $\sum p(x)=0$  
B. $\sum p(x)=1$ and $p\ge 0$  
C. $p(x)=1$ for all $x$  
D. $F(x)=p(x)$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Non-negativity and normalisation.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**4.** Jump of $F$ at a mass point $x$ equals

A. $F(x)$  
B. $p(x)$  
C. $1-p(x)$  
D. $0$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$p(x)=F(x)-F(x-)$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**5.** $\Pr(a<X\le b)$ for discrete $X$ equals

A. $F(a)-F(b)$  
B. $F(b)-F(a)$  
C. $p(a)+p(b)$  
D. $F(b)+F(a)$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Standard identity.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**6.** Fair die $X\in\{1,\ldots,6\}$. $F(3.5)=$

A. $1/6$  
B. $1/2$  
C. $3/6$  
D. $1$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$F(3.5)=\Pr(X\le 3)=3/6=1/2$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**7.** Discrete CDF is conventionally

A. left-continuous  
B. right-continuous  
C. continuous  
D. strictly increasing

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

So $F(x)=\Pr(X\le x)$ includes the mass at $x$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**8.** $p(0)=1/4$, $p(1)=1/2$, $p(2)=1/4$. $\Pr(0<X\le 1)=$

A. $1/4$  
B. $1/2$  
C. $3/4$  
D. $1$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$F(1)-F(0)=3/4-1/4=1/2$ (equals $p(1)$).  
Answer: **B**.
</details>
