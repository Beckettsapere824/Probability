# M1.8 Discrete Random Variables, PMF, CDF / 离散随机变量、PMF、CDF

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M1.1 Sample Space, Events, Probability Axioms](M1.1.md)

**Source / 来源**: SDST2601A Chapter 4 (discrete RV); bridge to continuous in [M8.1](M8.1.md)

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Events are subsets of $\Omega$. We often care about a **number** attached to each outcome—sum of two dice, number of defectives, indicator of a disease. A **random variable** packages that number so we can talk about its distribution once, without renaming events every time.

事件是 $\Omega$ 的子集。我们常常关心每个结果上的**数字**——两骰之和、次品数、是否患病。**随机变量**把该数字打包，从而一次说清它的分布。

---

## Step 1: Discrete random variable / 第一步：离散随机变量

A discrete random variable $X$ is a function $X:\Omega\to\mathbb{R}$ whose range is countable (finite or listable). The interesting events become $\{X=x\}$, $\{X\le x\}$, etc.

离散随机变量 $X$ 是值域可数的函数 $X:\Omega\to\mathbb{R}$。关心的事件变成 $\{X=x\}$、$\{X\le x\}$ 等。

---

## Step 2: PMF / 第二步：概率质量函数

$$p(x)=\Pr(X=x).$$

Must satisfy $p(x)\ge 0$ and $\sum_x p(x)=1$. Probabilities of sets of values: $\Pr(X\in A)=\sum_{x\in A}p(x)$.

---

## Step 3: CDF / 第三步：分布函数

$$F(x)=\Pr(X\le x)=\sum_{u\le x}p(u).$$

$F$ is non-decreasing and right-continuous; $F(-\infty)=0$, $F(+\infty)=1$. Jumps of $F$ recover the PMF: the jump at $x$ is $p(x)$.

$F$ 的跳跃高度就是 $p(x)$。

Interval rule (careful with endpoints for discrete):

$$\Pr(a<X\le b)=F(b)-F(a).$$

---

## Step 4: Hand example / 第四步：手算

$X\in\{0,1,2\}$ with probabilities $1/4,\,1/2,\,1/4$.

- $F(x)=0$ for $x<0$;  
- $1/4$ for $0\le x<1$;  
- $3/4$ for $1\le x<2$;  
- $1$ for $x\ge 2$.

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

- Writing $p(x)$ for a continuous variable (use $f$ and intervals).  
- Off-by-one errors: $\Pr(X<b)$ vs $\Pr(X\le b)$ when $b$ is a mass point.  
- PMF rows that do not sum to 1.

---

## Practice / 练习

$p(1)=0.2$, $p(2)=0.5$, $p(3)=0.3$. Find $F(2.5)$ and $\Pr(X\ge 2)$.

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

$F(2.5)=p(1)+p(2)=0.7$.  
$\Pr(X\ge 2)=p(2)+p(3)=0.8$.

</details>
