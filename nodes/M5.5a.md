# M5.5a States and Boundary Conditions for Absorbing Probability Equations / 吸收概率方程的状态与边界条件

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M5.5 Pattern Competition](M5.5.md), [M0.8 Matrices](M0.8.md)

---

## Content / 内容

States are labelled by the longest current prefix of either target pattern. For the competition between HHH and HTH the states may be ∅, H, HH, HTH (absorbing), HHH (absorbing). Let h_s be the probability that HHH appears first when the current state is s. The first-step equation reads
h_s = p h_{sH} + (1−p) h_{sT},
where sH (respectively sT) is the state reached after appending H (respectively T). Boundary conditions are h_HHH=1 and h_HTH=0. The resulting linear system is solved by ordinary algebra. A naïve comparison of first-occurrence probabilities fails precisely because of overlaps.

状态用当前任一目标模式的最长前缀标记。对 HHH 与 HTH 的竞争，状态可为 ∅、H、HH、HTH（吸收）、HHH（吸收）。设 h_s 为当前状态为 s 时 HHH 先出现的概率。首步方程为
h_s = p h_{sH} + (1−p) h_{sT}，
其中 sH（sT）为追加 H（T）后到达的状态。边界条件 h_HHH=1，h_HTH=0。所得线性方程组用普通代数求解。仅比较首次出现概率之所以失败，正是因为重叠。

## Example / 例子

From state HH: H leads to HHH (probability 1), T leads to the state whose prefix is the leading T of HTH (or to ∅ if T starts no pattern).

从状态 HH：H 通向 HHH（概率 1），T 通向前缀为 HTH 首字母 T 的状态（若 T 不启动任何模式则到 ∅）。

## Practice Problem / 练习题

Write the first-step equation for the empty state ∅ when the two patterns are HHH and HTH and the coin is fair.

当两模式为 HHH 与 HTH 且硬币公平时，写出空状态 ∅ 的首步方程。

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

h_∅ = (1/2) h_H + (1/2) h_∅ (T returns to ∅). Rearrangement yields h_∅ = h_H.

h_∅ = (1/2) h_H + (1/2) h_∅（T 回到 ∅）。整理得 h_∅ = h_H。

</details>
