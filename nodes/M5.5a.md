# M5.5a Boundary Conditions for Absorption / 吸收边界条件

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M5.5](M5.5.md)

**Source anchors**: $h=1$ on goal, $h=0$ on failure; well-posed linear system.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Absorption probabilities are determined only after **boundary conditions** are fixed. Wrong boundaries produce a wrong solution even if interior equations are correct.

吸收概率由**边界条件**决定；边界写错则整组解错。

---

## Step 1: Goal and failure / 第一步：目标与失败

- Goal absorbing states: $h=1$.  
- Failure absorbing states: $h=0$.  
- If several goals, can define separate $h$ for each target or a combined success set.

---

## Step 2: No free constants / 第二步：无自由常数

With all absorbing states assigned and the chain able to reach them, the transient equations have a unique solution under standard conditions.

---

## Step 3: Hand check / 第三步：手算

Two absorbing states $0$ and $N$, goal $=N$: $h_0=0$, $h_N=1$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Boundaries are part of the model definition (what counts as success).  
2. Interior equations alone are underdetermined without boundaries.  
3. Multiple goal sets → multiple $h$ functions or indicators.  
4. Same boundaries appear in expected-time problems ($e=0$ on absorbing states).

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Leaving absorbing $h$ unknown | Assign 0/1 first |
| Setting both ends to 1 | Defines a different problem |
| Using $\pi$ as boundary | Stationarity ≠ absorption |
