# M4.6a Poisson as the Limit of Rare-Event Counts / 泊松分布作为稀有事件计数极限

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M4.6 Poisson](M4.6.md)

---

## Content / 内容

The Poisson PMF is P(X=k)=e^{−λ} λ^k / k!. The exponential series Σ λ^k / k! = e^λ guarantees that the probabilities sum to one. The single parameter λ is simultaneously the mean and the variance. The classical binomial limit states that if n→∞, p→0 and np→λ then Binomial(n,p) converges in distribution to Poisson(λ). More generally, the sum of a large number of rare, weakly dependent indicators also tends to a Poisson limit; a strict binomial structure is not required. In the secretary problem the number of fixed points has mean 1 for every n and converges to Poisson(1), consistent with the mean of the limit.

泊松 PMF 为 P(X=k)=e^{−λ} λ^k / k!。指数级数 Σ λ^k / k! = e^λ 保证概率和为 1。单参数 λ 同时是均值与方差。经典二项极限断言：若 n→∞、p→0 且 np→λ，则 Binomial(n,p) 依分布收敛到 Poisson(λ)。更一般地，大量稀有、弱相依指示之和也趋于泊松极限；不需要严格的二项结构。秘书问题中不动点数对每个 n 均值都为 1，并收敛到 Poisson(1)，与极限均值一致。

## Example / 例子

n=100, p=0.03 → λ=3. Binomial(100,0.03) is already close to Poisson(3).

n=100，p=0.03 → λ=3。Binomial(100,0.03) 已接近 Poisson(3)。

## Practice Problem / 练习题

Why does the fact that E(X)=1 for the number of fixed points already suggest that any limiting distribution, if Poisson, must be Poisson(1)?

为什么不动点数 E(X)=1 已经暗示：若极限分布为泊松，则必须是 Poisson(1)？

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

Means converge under the usual conditions that justify the Poisson limit; therefore the limiting mean equals the common finite-n mean 1, forcing λ=1.

在证明泊松极限的通常条件下均值收敛；因此极限均值等于有限 n 的公共均值 1，迫使 λ=1。

</details>
