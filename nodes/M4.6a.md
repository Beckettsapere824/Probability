# M4.6a Poisson Approximation Checklist / 泊松近似检查

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M4.6](M4.6.md)

**Source anchors**: $n$ large, $p$ small, $\lambda=np$ moderate; rare events.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

When is $\mathrm{Bin}(n,p)\approx\mathrm{Poisson}(\lambda=np)$ good enough for hand calculation?

$n$ 大、$p$ 小、$np$ 适中时，用泊松近似二项。

---

## Step 1: Regime / 第一步：制度

Rule of thumb: $n$ large, $p$ small, $\lambda=np$ moderate. Limit: $n\to\infty$, $p\to 0$, $np\to\lambda$.

---

## Step 2: What to compute / 第二步：算什么

$\Pr(X=k)\approx e^{-\lambda}\lambda^{k}/k!$.

---

## Step 3: Hand check / 第三步：手算

$n=100$, $p=0.03$ ⇒ $\lambda=3$. $\Pr(X=0)\approx e^{-3}$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Rare independent events ⇒ Poisson counts.  
2. Match the mean $\lambda=np$.  
3. Approximation quality degrades if $p$ is not small.  
4. Continuity correction is for normal approx, not Poisson.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Using $\lambda=p$ | $\lambda=np$ |
| Applying when $p=0.5$, $n=10$ | Regime fails |
| Confusing with normal approximation | Different limit |

---
