## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Exam language is often “at least $r$” or “at most $r$”. Complement and clear boundaries cut the work.

「至少 / 至多」语言；补事件与边界减负。

---

## Step 1: Boundaries / 第一步：边界

| Phrase | Event |
|--------|--------|
| at least $r$ | $X\ge r$ |
| at most $r$ | $X\le r$ |
| more than $r$ | $X>r$ |
| fewer than $r$ | $X<r$ |
| exactly $r$ | $X=r$ |

---

## Step 2: Complement / 第二步：补事件

$$\Pr(X\ge r)=1-\Pr(X\le r-1).$$

---

## Step 3: Hand example / 第三步：手算

$X\sim B(5,1/2)$. $\Pr(X\ge 4)=3/16$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Read inequality direction carefully.  
2. Complement when the lower tail is shorter.  
3. Off-by-one is the main trap.  
4. Term ratios speed successive PMF values.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| $\Pr(X\ge r)=1-\Pr(X\le r)$ | Use $1-\Pr(X\le r-1)$ |
| “At least two” as $1-\Pr(X=0)$ | Also remove $X=1$ |
| Mixing continuous tail language | Discrete endpoints matter |

**3.** $X\sim B(5,1/2)$. $\Pr(X\ge 4)=$

A. $1/2$  
B. $3/16$  
C. $1/32$  
D. $5/16$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$5/32+1/32=3/16$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**4.** Fair coin, 6 tosses, $\Pr(\text{at least 2 heads})=$

D. $6/64$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$1-1/64-6/64=57/64$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>
