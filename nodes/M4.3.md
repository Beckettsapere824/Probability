# M4.3 Binomial Tail Probabilities and Complements / 二项尾概率与补事件

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M4.2 Binomial](M4.2.md), [M1.2 Complement and Inclusion-Exclusion](M1.2.md)

## Content / 内容

The upper tail is most often evaluated by the complement: P(X ≥ r) = 1 − P(X ≤ r−1). The phrase “at least two successes in ten trials” therefore becomes 1 − P(0) − P(1). Direct term-by-term summation is feasible for small n; the successive-term ratio reduces the arithmetic. Always distinguish the boundaries of “at least”, “at most” and “exactly”.

上尾最常通过补事件计算：P(X ≥ r) = 1 − P(X ≤ r−1)。因此“十次试验中至少两次成功”变成 1 − P(0) − P(1)。n 较小时可逐项累加；相邻项比值可减少计算量。始终区分“至少”“至多”“恰好”的边界。

## Example / 例子

X ~ B(5,1/2). P(X ≥ 4) = P(4)+P(5) = 5/32 + 1/32 = 6/32 = 3/16.

X ~ B(5,1/2)。P(X ≥ 4) = P(4)+P(5) = 5/32 + 1/32 = 6/32 = 3/16。

## Practice Problem / 练习题

A fair coin is tossed 6 times. Find the probability of at least two heads.

公平硬币抛 6 次。求至少两次正面的概率。

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

1 − P(0) − P(1) = 1 − 1/64 − 6/64 = 57/64.

1 − P(0) − P(1) = 1 − 1/64 − 6/64 = 57/64。

</details>
