# M4.3 Binomial Tail Probabilities / 二项尾概率

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M4.2 Binomial](M4.2.md), [M1.2 Complement](M1.2.md)

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Exam language is often “at least $r$ successes” or “at most $r$”. Summing many PMF terms is tedious; complement and clear boundary reading cut the work.

题目常写「至少 $r$ 次成功」或「至多 $r$ 次」。多项相加麻烦；补事件与分清边界能减负。

---

## Step 1: Boundaries / 第一步：边界

| Phrase | Event |
|--------|--------|
| at least $r$ | $X\ge r$ |
| at most $r$ | $X\le r$ |
| more than $r$ | $X>r$ |
| fewer than $r$ | $X<r$ |
| exactly $r$ | $X=r$ |

---

## Step 2: Complement / 第二步：补事件

$$\Pr(X\ge r)=1-\Pr(X\le r-1)=1-\sum_{k=0}^{r-1}\Pr(X=k).$$

For small $n$, sum directly; successive-term ratios from [M4.2](M4.2.md) help.

---

## Step 3: Hand example / 第三步：手算

$X\sim B(5,1/2)$. $\Pr(X\ge 4)=\Pr(4)+\Pr(5)=5/32+1/32=3/16$.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

- Off-by-one: $\Pr(X\ge r)$ vs $1-\Pr(X\le r)$.  
- Mixing “at least two” with $1-\Pr(X=0)$ (must also remove $X=1$).

---

## Practice / 练习

Fair coin, 6 tosses. Probability of at least two heads?

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

$X\sim B(6,1/2)$. $\Pr(X\ge 2)=1-\Pr(0)-\Pr(1)=1-1/64-6/64=57/64$.

</details>
