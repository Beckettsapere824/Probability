# M2.13 Grid Paths and Turning Segments / 网格路径与转向分段

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.3 Factorials, Permutations, Combinations](M0.3.md)

---

## Content / 内容

The number of shortest paths from (0,0) to (m,n) using only unit East and North steps is C(m+n,n). A path with exactly three turns can be decomposed into four straight segments. One enumerates by the direction of the first turn, the columns/rows where the turns occur, and the lengths of the successive segments.

从 (0,0) 到 (m,n) 仅用单位东、北步的最短路径数为 C(m+n,n)。恰三次转向的路径可分成四段直线。按第一次转向方向、转向所在列/行以及各段长度枚举。

The starting point and the ending point are never counted as turns. Care must be taken not to count the same geometric path under different turn-point labelings. A reliable method is to fix the sequence of turn directions first and then choose the positive lengths of each segment.

起点和终点不算转向。避免把同一几何路径按不同转向点重复计数。可靠做法是先固定转向方向序列，再选每段的正长度。

## Example / 例子

Paths from (0,0) to (2,2) with exactly one turn: the turn must be at (2,0) or at (0,2), giving two paths (EEN N and NN EE).

从 (0,0) 到 (2,2) 恰一次转向的路径：转向必在 (2,0) 或 (0,2)，共两条（EEN N 与 NN EE）。

## Practice Problem / 练习题

How many shortest paths from (0,0) to (3,2) have exactly two turns?

从 (0,0) 到 (3,2) 的最短路径中恰有两次转向的有多少条？

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

Possible direction patterns: ENE N, NEN E, etc. Enumerate segment lengths that sum to the required totals; the answer is 6.

可能方向模式：ENE N、NEN E 等。枚举段长使其和为所需总量；答案为 6。

</details>
