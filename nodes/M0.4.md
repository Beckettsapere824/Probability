# M0.4 Binomial Coefficient Identities / 组合恒等式

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.3 Factorials, Permutations, Combinations](M0.3.md), [M0.3a](M0.3a.md)

**Source anchors**: symmetry, Pascal’s identity, hockey-stick; used to simplify combinatorial sums.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Many counting problems produce awkward binomial coefficients or long sums. A short list of identities lets us rewrite them into simpler expressions that are easier to evaluate or to recognise as probabilities.

许多计数问题会出现难看的组合数或长求和。几条恒等式可把它们改写成更简单的表达式。

---

## Step 1: Symmetry / 第一步：对称

$$
\binom{n}{k}=\binom{n}{n-k}.
$$

Choosing $k$ elements to include is the same as choosing $n-k$ elements to exclude.

---

## Step 2: Pascal’s identity / 第二步：帕斯卡恒等式

$$
\binom{n}{k}=\binom{n-1}{k}+\binom{n-1}{k-1}.
$$

Combinatorial proof: fix one distinguished element. Either it is in the $k$-set (then choose $k-1$ from the remaining $n-1$) or it is not (then choose all $k$ from the remaining $n-1$).

---

## Step 3: Hockey-stick identity (optional but useful) / 第三步：曲棍球棍

$$
\sum_{i=r}^{m}\binom{i}{r}=\binom{m+1}{r+1}.
$$

(Useful for summing consecutive binomial coefficients.)

---

## Step 4: Hand checks / 第四步：手算

- $\binom{6}{4}=\binom{6}{2}=15$  
- $\binom{5}{2}=\binom{4}{2}+\binom{4}{1}=6+4=10$  
- $\sum_{i=2}^{4}\binom{i}{2}=\binom{2}{2}+\binom{3}{2}+\binom{4}{2}=1+3+6=10=\binom{5}{3}$

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Symmetry halves the number of distinct values one must remember.  
2. Pascal’s identity is the recurrence that builds Pascal’s triangle.  
3. These identities are algebraic consequences of the factorial definition and also have direct combinatorial proofs.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Using symmetry with non-integer $n$ | Stick to non-negative integers |
| Off-by-one in the hockey-stick upper index | Write $\sum_{i=r}^{m}\binom{i}{r}=\binom{m+1}{r+1}$ carefully |
| Applying Pascal when $k=0$ without the zero convention | $\binom{n-1}{-1}=0$ |

## Practice / 练习（选择题）

**1.** By symmetry, $\binom{8}{3}$ equals

A. $\binom{8}{4}$  
B. $\binom{8}{5}$  
C. $\binom{8}{6}$  
D. $\binom{7}{3}$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$\binom{8}{3}=\binom{8}{8-3}=\binom{8}{5}$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**2.** Pascal’s identity states

A. $\binom{n}{k}=\binom{n}{k-1}$  
B. $\binom{n}{k}=\binom{n-1}{k}+\binom{n-1}{k-1}$  
C. $\binom{n}{k}=\binom{n-1}{k}\times\binom{n-1}{k-1}$  
D. $\binom{n}{k}=n\binom{n-1}{k}$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Standard Pascal recurrence.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**3.** Compute $\binom{5}{2}$ via Pascal.

A. $\binom{4}{2}+\binom{4}{1}=6+4=10$  
B. $\binom{4}{2}+\binom{4}{0}=6+1=7$  
C. $\binom{5}{1}+\binom{5}{0}=5+1=6$  
D. $5\times 2=10$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$\binom{5}{2}=\binom{4}{2}+\binom{4}{1}=6+4=10$.  
Answer: **A**.
</details>

**4.** $\sum_{i=3}^{5}\binom{i}{3}$ equals (hockey-stick)

A. $\binom{6}{4}$  
B. $\binom{5}{4}$  
C. $\binom{6}{3}$  
D. $\binom{5}{3}$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$\sum_{i=3}^{5}\binom{i}{3}=\binom{6}{4}=15$.  
Answer: **A**.
</details>

**5.** Which is a correct combinatorial reading of Pascal’s identity?

A. Every $k$-subset of $[n]$ either contains $n$ or does not  
B. Order always matters  
C. Symmetry of rows  
D. Generating functions only

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Fix the element $n$: it is either chosen (then choose $k-1$ from the first $n-1$) or not (then choose $k$ from the first $n-1$).  
Answer: **A**.
</details>

**6.** $\binom{7}{0}+\binom{7}{1}+\cdots+\binom{7}{7}$ equals

A. $7!$  
B. $2^{7}$  
C. $7^{2}$  
D. $\binom{8}{4}$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Binomial theorem: $(1+1)^{7}=2^{7}=128$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**7.** True or false: $\binom{n}{k}=\binom{n}{n-k}$ holds for all integers $0\le k\le n$.

A. True  
B. False  
C. Only when $n$ is even  
D. Only when $k=0$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

This is the symmetry identity.  
Answer: **A**.
</details>
