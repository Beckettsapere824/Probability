# M0.4 Binomial Coefficient Identities / 组合恒等式

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.3](M0.3.md)

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Speed up counting by rewriting $\binom{n}{k}$ using symmetry and Pascal.

用对称与 Pascal 恒等式加速计数。

---

## Step 1: Symmetry / 对称

$\binom{n}{k}=\binom{n}{n-k}$.

---

## Step 2: Pascal / 帕斯卡

$\binom{n}{k}=\binom{n-1}{k}+\binom{n-1}{k-1}$.

---

## Practice / 练习

Write $\binom{6}{4}$ via symmetry.

<details><summary>Answer</summary>$\binom{6}{2}=15$.</details>
