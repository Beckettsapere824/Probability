# M5.3 Conditional Counting Probabilities (Seating Problems) / 条件计数概率（座位问题）

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M2.18 Gap Method](M2.18.md), [M1.3 Conditional Probability](M1.3.md)

---

## Content / 内容

A conditional probability that is purely combinatorial equals the number of configurations satisfying both the conditioning event and the target event, divided by the number of configurations satisfying the conditioning event alone. If the instructor condition already implies the TA condition, the conditional probability collapses to N_I / N_TA.

纯组合的条件概率等于同时满足条件事件与目标事件的配置数，除以仅满足条件事件的配置数。若 instructor 条件已蕴含 TA 条件，则条件概率简化为 N_I / N_TA。

First count the conditioning event, then count the intersection. The gap method supplies the necessary enumerations. Distinguish carefully between “at least two empty seats” and “no two adjacent”. The denominator must be positive.

先数条件事件，再数交事件。间隔法提供所需枚举。仔细区分“至少两个空座”与“无相邻”。分母必须为正。

## Example / 例子

12 seats, 3 people, no two adjacent. Probability that seats 1 and 12 are both occupied, given that no two are adjacent, is the ratio of the corresponding combinatorial counts.

12 座、3 人、无两人相邻。在无相邻条件下座位 1 与 12 都被占用的概率，是相应组合计数的比值。

## Practice Problem / 练习题

From 10 seats choose 3 with no two adjacent. Given that seat 1 is occupied, what is the probability that seat 10 is also occupied?

从 10 座中选 3 座且无相邻。已知座位 1 被占，座位 10 也被占的概率是多少？

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

Conditioning: seat 1 fixed, choose 2 more from the remaining 8 seats with gaps. Intersection: seats 1 and 10 fixed, choose 1 more from the middle 6 with gaps. Ratio of the two counts.

条件：座位 1 固定，从剩余 8 座中再选 2 且保持间隔。交事件：座位 1 与 10 固定，从中间 6 座中再选 1 且保持间隔。两计数之比。

</details>
