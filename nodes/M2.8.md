# M2.8 Birthday Problem / Occupancy Model / 生日问题/占位模型

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M1.9 Classical Probability](M1.9.md), [M2.1 Ordered/Unordered With/Without Replacement](M2.1.md)

---

## Content / 内容

The classical occupancy model places n distinguishable balls into m distinguishable boxes; the total number of outcomes is m^n. The probability of no collision (all boxes distinct) is P(m,n)/m^n when n ≤ m. The probability of at least one collision is therefore 1 - P(m,n)/m^n.

经典占位模型把 n 个可区分球放入 m 个可区分盒子；总结果数 m^n。无碰撞（全不同盒）概率在 n ≤ m 时为 P(m,n)/m^n。至少一碰撞的概率为 1 - P(m,n)/m^n。

The model assumes distinguishable balls and distinguishable boxes. If either assumption fails a different counting scheme is required. In the birthday problem a “collision” means at least two people share a birthday (at least one box contains two or more balls).

模型假定球可区分、盒可区分。若任一假设不成立则需换计数方案。生日问题中“碰撞”= 至少两人同生日（至少一盒含两个及以上球）。

## Example / 例子

Probability that 3 people have distinct birthdays (365 days): P(365,3)/365^3. Probability of at least one shared birthday among 23 people is already greater than 1/2.

3 人生日全不同（365 天）的概率：P(365,3)/365^3。23 人中至少两人同生日的概率已大于 1/2。

## Practice Problem / 练习题

What is the probability that, among 5 people, at least two share a birthday month (12 months, assume uniform and independent)?

5 人中至少两人同一出生月份（12 月，假定均匀独立）的概率是多少？

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

1 - P(12,5)/12^5.

1 - P(12,5)/12^5。

</details>
