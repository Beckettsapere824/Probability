# M2.8 Birthday-Type Collisions / 生日类碰撞

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M2.2](M2.2.md), [M1.2](M1.2.md)

**Source anchors**: complement of all distinct; $1-P(d,k)/d^{k}$.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Probability that $k$ independent uniform draws from $d$ days produce **at least one collision**.

$k$ 次独立均匀取自 $d$ 个格，至少一次碰撞的概率。

---

## Step 1: Complement / 第一步：补事件

$$\Pr(\text{at least one shared})=\ 1-\frac{P(d,k)}{d^{k}}.$$

---

## Step 2: Why complement / 第二步：为何用补

Direct “at least one pair matches” needs inclusion-exclusion; complement is a single falling factorial.

---

## Step 3: Classic numbers / 第三步：经典数字

$d=365$, $k\approx23$ already gives $\Pr>1/2$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Collisions appear sooner than intuition suggests.  
2. Complement of injections into the day set.  
3. Approximation $1-\exp(-k(k-1)/(2d))$ for moderate $k/\sqrt{d}$.  
4. Same structure as hash collisions.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Using $k/d$ as the probability | Wrong scale |
| Expanding all pair unions without IE | Use complement |
| Assuming independence of pair indicators naively | Dependent |
