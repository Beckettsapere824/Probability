# M2.8 Birthday-Type Collisions / 生日类碰撞

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M2.2](M2.2.md), [M1.2](M1.2.md)

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

$\Pr(\text{at least one shared birthday})$ among $k$ people ≈ $1-P(365,k)/365^{k}$.

---

## Practice / 练习

Why complement of “all distinct” is the right path?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>Direct “at least one match” unions are messy; complement is a single product.</details>
