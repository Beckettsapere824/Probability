# M0.8 Matrices (minimal) / 矩阵基础

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.2](M0.2.md)

**Source anchors**: row-stochastic matrices; $\pi P=\pi$; two-state chain multiplication.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Markov chain transitions are written as matrix multiplication. We need the minimal vocabulary: matrix product, row-stochastic matrices, and the linear system for a stationary distribution.

马氏链转移用矩阵乘法表示。需要最小词汇：矩阵乘、行随机矩阵、平稳分布线性方程组。

---

## Step 1: Matrix product / 第一步：矩阵乘法

For $A$ of size $m\times n$ and $B$ of size $n\times p$,

$$
(AB)_{ij}=\sum_{k=1}^{n}A_{ik}B_{kj}.
$$

Row vector times matrix: if $\mu$ is $1\times n$ and $P$ is $n\times n$, then $\mu P$ is the distribution after one step.

---

## Step 2: Stochastic matrix / 第二步：随机矩阵

A row-stochastic matrix has nonnegative entries and each row sums to 1:

$$
\sum_{j}P_{ij}=1\quad\text{for every }i.
$$

Then if $\mu$ is a probability row vector, so is $\mu P$.

---

## Step 3: Stationary distribution / 第三步：平稳分布

$\pi$ is stationary if $\pi P=\pi$ and $\sum_{i}\pi_{i}=1$, $\pi_{i}\ge 0$.

This is a linear system: $\pi(P-I)=0$ together with the normalisation constraint.

---

## Step 4: Hand calculation / 第四步：手算

$$
\begin{pmatrix}0.5&0.5\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}0.9&0.1\\0.2&0.8\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}0.55&0.45\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Check: $0.5\cdot 0.9+0.5\cdot 0.2=0.55$, $0.5\cdot 0.1+0.5\cdot 0.8=0.45$.

For two-state $P=\begin{pmatrix}1-a&a\\b&1-b\end{pmatrix}$, stationary $\pi=(b/(a+b),\,a/(a+b))$ when $a+b>0$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. One step of a chain = multiply the current distribution row vector by $P$.  
2. Rows of $P$ are conditional distributions of the next state.  
3. Stationarity is an eigenvector equation with eigenvalue 1, plus normalisation.  
4. Nonnegativity and row sums are what keep probability vectors inside the simplex.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Multiplying $P\mu$ as if $\mu$ were a column without transposing | Agree on row-vector convention and stick to it |
| Forgetting normalisation when solving $\pi P=\pi$ | Always add $\sum\pi_{i}=1$ |
| Allowing negative entries in $P$ | Transition probabilities must be $\ge 0$ |
| Confusing row-stochastic with column-stochastic | Rows sum to 1 in the usual DTMC convention |

## Practice / 练习（选择题）

**1.** $(AB)_{ij}$ equals

A. $A_{ij}B_{ij}$  
B. $\sum_{k}A_{ik}B_{kj}$  
C. $\sum_{k}A_{ki}B_{jk}$  
D. $A_{i\cdot}B_{\cdot j}$ only when square

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Definition of matrix product.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**2.** A row-stochastic matrix has

A. columns summing to 1  
B. rows summing to 1 and entries $\ge 0$  
C. determinant 1  
D. trace 1

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Definition used for DTMC transition matrices.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**3.** $\begin{pmatrix}0.5&0.5\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}0.9&0.1\\0.2&0.8\end{pmatrix}=$

A. $(0.9,0.1)$  
B. $(0.55,0.45)$  
C. $(0.7,0.3)$  
D. $(1,0)$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$0.5\cdot 0.9+0.5\cdot 0.2=0.55$, $0.5\cdot 0.1+0.5\cdot 0.8=0.45$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**4.** Stationary $\pi$ satisfies

A. $P\pi=\pi$ (column convention) or $\pi P=\pi$ (row convention), and $\sum\pi_{i}=1$  
B. $\pi=0$  
C. $P=I$  
D. $\pi_{i}=1/n$ always

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Eigenvector for eigenvalue 1 plus normalisation.  
Answer: **A**.
</details>

**5.** If $P$ is row-stochastic and $\mu$ is a probability vector, then $\mu P$ is

A. not necessarily a probability vector  
B. again a probability vector  
C. a zero vector  
D. an integer vector

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Nonnegativity preserved; total mass $\mu P\mathbf{1}=\mu\mathbf{1}=1$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**6.** For $P=\begin{pmatrix}0.5&0.5\\0.5&0.5\end{pmatrix}$, a stationary distribution is

A. $(1,0)$  
B. $(0.5,0.5)$  
C. $(0,1)$  
D. $(2,-1)$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Any convex combination works; the unique one with normalisation is $(1/2,1/2)$. $(2,-1)$ is invalid.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**7.** Identity matrix $I$ satisfies

A. $PI=P$ and $IP=P$  
B. $PI=0$  
C. rows sum to 0  
D. only for $2\times 2$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Definition of identity.  
Answer: **A**.
</details>

**8.** Two-state chain with $P=\begin{pmatrix}1-a&a\\b&1-b\end{pmatrix}$, $a,b>0$, has stationary

A. $(a,b)$  
B. $(b/(a+b),\,a/(a+b))$  
C. $(1/2,1/2)$ always  
D. $(a+b,a-b)$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Solve $\pi P=\pi$ with $\pi_{1}+\pi_{2}=1$: $\pi_{1}=b/(a+b)$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>
