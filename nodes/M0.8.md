**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.2](M0.2.md)

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Transition matrices for Markov chains need row/column multiplication.

马氏链转移矩阵需要矩阵乘法。

---

## Facts / 要点

$(AB)_{ij}=\sum_k A_{ik}B_{kj}$. Identity $I$. Stochastic matrix: rows sum to 1.
