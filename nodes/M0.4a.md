# M0.4a Binomial Theorem / 二项式定理

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.4](M0.4.md)

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Expand $(x+y)^{n}$ and extract coefficients.

展开 $(x+y)^{n}$ 并提取系数。

---

## Formula / 公式

$$(x+y)^{n}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}x^{k}y^{n-k}.$$

Also explains why binomial PMF sums to 1.

---

## Practice / 练习

Coefficient of $x^{2}$ in $(1+x)^{5}$?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>$\binom{5}{2}=10$.</details>
