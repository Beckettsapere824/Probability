# M0.6 Derivatives for MGFs / MGF 用到的求导

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.5](M0.5.md)

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Moment generating functions yield moments by differentiation at 0.

MGF 在 0 点求导得到矩。

---

## Key facts / 要点

$\frac{d}{dx}e^{ax}=ae^{ax}$; product rule; $\frac{d}{dt}M_X(t)|_{t=0}=E(X)$ when MGF exists.

---

## Practice / 练习

If $M(t)=(1-t)^{-2}$ for $t<1$, $M'(0)$?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>$M'(t)=2(1-t)^{-3}$, $M'(0)=2$.</details>
