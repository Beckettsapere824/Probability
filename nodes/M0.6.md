# M0.6 Derivatives for MGFs / MGF 用到的求导

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.5](M0.5.md)

**Source anchors**: $M_X^{(r)}(0)=E(X^{r})$; product and chain rules; domain of MGF.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

The moment generating function $M_X(t)=E(e^{tX})$ encodes all moments of $X$ (when it exists in a neighbourhood of 0). Differentiating and evaluating at $t=0$ recovers $E(X)$, $E(X^{2})$, … without summing the original series by hand.

矩母函数在 0 的邻域存在时，把各阶矩打包在一起。对 $t$ 求导再令 $t=0$ 即可取出 $E(X)$、$E(X^{2})$，无需重求和。

---

## Step 1: Key differentiation rules / 第一步：求导规则

- $\dfrac{d}{dx}e^{ax}=ae^{ax}$
- Product rule: $(uv)'=u'v+uv'$
- Chain rule: $\dfrac{d}{dt}f(g(t))=f'(g(t))g'(t)$
- $\dfrac{d}{dt}(1-t)^{-a}=a(1-t)^{-a-1}$ (for $|t|<1$ when needed)

---

## Step 2: Moments from MGF / 第二步：从 MGF 取矩

If $M(t)$ exists in an open interval containing 0, then

$$
M^{(r)}(0)=E(X^{r}).
$$

In particular $M'(0)=E(X)$, $M''(0)=E(X^{2})$, and

$$
\mathrm{Var}(X)=M''(0)-(M'(0))^{2}.
$$

---

## Step 3: Worked example / 第三步：例子

Let $M(t)=(1-t)^{-2}$ for $t<1$. Then

$$
M'(t)=2(1-t)^{-3},\qquad M'(0)=2,
$$

$$
M''(t)=6(1-t)^{-4},\qquad M''(0)=6.
$$

So $E(X)=2$, $E(X^{2})=6$, $\mathrm{Var}(X)=6-4=2$ (Gamma$(2,1)$ / Erlang).

Another common form: $M(t)=\exp(\mu t+\sigma^{2}t^{2}/2)$ (normal) yields $M'(0)=\mu$, $M''(0)=\mu^{2}+\sigma^{2}$.

---

## Step 4: Domain reminder / 第四步：定义域

MGF may exist only for $t$ in an interval. Always state the domain before differentiating (e.g. $|pe^{t}|<1$ for geometric-type MGFs).

先写清 $t$ 的范围再求导。

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Differentiation under the expectation (justified when MGF exists near 0) turns $E(e^{tX})$ into $E(X^{r}e^{tX})$.  
2. Evaluating at 0 strips the exponential and leaves the moment.  
3. Variance needs both first and second derivatives.  
4. Algebraic forms like $(1-t)^{-a}$ differentiate by the power rule with a sign.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Differentiating without checking domain | State $|t|<t_{0}$ first |
| Computing only $M'(0)$ and calling it variance | Variance uses $M''(0)-(M'(0))^{2}$ |
| Sign error on $(1-t)^{-a}$ | Derivative is $+a(1-t)^{-a-1}$ |
| Mixing MGF with characteristic function | MGF uses $e^{tX}$, real $t$ |

---

## Practice / 练习（选择题）

**1.** If $M(t)=(1-t)^{-2}$ for $t<1$, then $M'(0)=$

A. 1  
B. 2  
C. 3  
D. 4

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$M'(t)=2(1-t)^{-3}$, $M'(0)=2$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**2.** $M''(0)-[M'(0)]^{2}$ equals

A. $E(X)$  
B. $E(X^{2})$  
C. $\mathrm{Var}(X)$  
D. $M(0)$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Definition of variance via first two moments from the MGF.  
Answer: **C**.
</details>

**3.** $\dfrac{d}{dt}e^{at}=$

A. $e^{at}$  
B. $ae^{at}$  
C. $t e^{at}$  
D. $a^{t}$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Chain rule / standard exponential derivative.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**4.** For $M(t)=(1-2t)^{-1}$ ($t<1/2$), $M'(0)=$

A. 1  
B. 2  
C. 4  
D. 1/2

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$M'(t)=2(1-2t)^{-2}$, $M'(0)=2$. (Exponential rate 1/2, mean 2.)  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**5.** $M(0)$ is always

A. 0  
B. 1  
C. $E(X)$  
D. undefined

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$M(0)=E(e^{0\cdot X})=E(1)=1$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**6.** Product rule $(uv)'=$

A. $u'v'$  
B. $u'v+uv'$  
C. $u'v-uv'$  
D. $(u+v)'$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Standard product rule.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**7.** If $M(t)=\exp(\mu t+\sigma^{2}t^{2}/2)$, $M'(0)=$

A. 0  
B. $\mu$  
C. $\sigma^{2}$  
D. $\mu+\sigma^{2}$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$M'(t)=(\mu+\sigma^{2}t)M(t)$, so $M'(0)=\mu$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**8.** $E(X^{2})$ equals

A. $M'(0)$  
B. $M''(0)$  
C. $M''(0)-[M'(0)]^{2}$  
D. $M(0)$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Second derivative at 0 is the second moment.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>
