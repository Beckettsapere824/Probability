# M2.19 Inclusion-Exclusion Counting / 容斥计数

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M1.2](M1.2.md), [M2.11](M2.11.md)

**Source anchors**: $|A_1\cup\cdots\cup A_n|$ via alternating intersections; derangements as special case.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Count the size of a union of sets defined by “bad” properties, when intersections are easier than the union directly.

用容斥原理计算由若干“坏性质”定义的并集大小。

---

## Step 1: IE formula / 第一步：公式

$$\Bigl|\bigcup_{i=1}^{n}A_i\Bigr|=\sum|A_i|-\sum|A_i\cap A_j|+\cdots+(-1)^{n+1}|A_1\cap\cdots\cap A_n|.$$

---

## Step 2: Derangements / 第二步：错排

$A_i=$ permutations fixing $i$; IE yields $!n$.

---

## Step 3: Hand check / 第三步：手算

Two sets: $|A\cup B|=|A|+|B|-|A\cap B|$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Add singles, subtract doubles, add triples, …  
2. Works for counting and for probability.  
3. Symmetry often makes all $k$-fold intersections equal.  
4. Truncation gives Bonferroni bounds.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Stopping after first term | Alternating full sum |
| Wrong sign pattern | $(-1)^{k+1}$ for $k$-fold |
| Applying when intersections unknown | Need computable intersections |
