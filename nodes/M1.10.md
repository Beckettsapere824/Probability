# M1.10 Joint Discrete Distributions, Marginals, Independence / 联合离散分布、边缘与独立

**Source anchors**: SDST2601A Ch7 discrete joint; continuous joint in [M8.10](M8.10.md); covariance link.

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Two numerical measurements on the same experiment — two dice, two test indicators, counts of red and white in a sample — need a **joint** law. From it we recover each variable alone (marginals) and test whether they factor (independence).

同一次试验上的两个数值需要**联合**律。由此得到各自边缘，并检验是否因子分解（独立）。

---

## Step 1: Joint PMF / 第一步：联合 PMF

$$p_{X,Y}(x,y)=\Pr(X=x,\,Y=y).$$

Requirements: $p_{X,Y}(x,y)\ge 0$ and $\sum_x\sum_y p_{X,Y}(x,y)=1$.  
The joint PMF determines every probability involving $(X,Y)$:

$$\Pr\bigl((X,Y)\in A\bigr)=\sum_{(x,y)\in A}p_{X,Y}(x,y).$$

---

## Step 2: Marginals / 第二步：边缘

$$p_X(x)=\sum_y p_{X,Y}(x,y), \qquad p_Y(y)=\sum_x p_{X,Y}(x,y).$$

“Sum out” the variable you do not need. The marginal of $X$ is a valid PMF on its own; it forgets $Y$.

把不需要的变量求和消掉。边缘是合法的单变量 PMF。

---

## Step 3: Independence of random variables / 第三步：随机变量独立

$X$ and $Y$ are independent iff

$$p_{X,Y}(x,y)=p_X(x)\,p_Y(y) \quad\text{for all }x,y.$$

Equivalent: the joint table is an outer product of the marginal vectors.  
Then for suitable $g,h$,

$$E\bigl[g(X)h(Y)\bigr]=E[g(X)]E[h(Y)].$$

**Important:** linearity $E(X+Y)=E(X)+E(Y)$ never needs independence; the product identity does.

联合表是边缘向量的外积。期望线性性不需独立；乘积恒等式需要。

---

## Step 4: Hand example / 第四步：手算

**Independent dice.** Two fair dice: $p(x,y)=1/36$ for $x,y\in\{1,\ldots,6\}$. Marginal of $X$ is uniform on $\{1,\ldots,6\}$.

**Dependent table.** $p(0,0)=0.1$, $p(0,1)=0.2$, $p(1,0)=0.3$, $p(1,1)=0.4$.  
$p_X(1)=0.7$, $p_Y(0)=0.4$.  
$p_X(0)p_Y(0)=0.3\cdot 0.4=0.12\neq 0.1=p(0,0)$ → not independent.

---

## Step 5: From joint to covariance (preview) / 第五步：到协方差的预告

$E(XY)=\sum_{x,y}xy\,p(x,y)$. With independence this factors as $E(X)E(Y)$, so $\mathrm{Cov}(X,Y)=0$. Dependence can produce nonzero covariance (e.g. hypergeometric colour counts).

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Joint PMF = complete discrete law of the pair.  
2. Marginal = sum out the other variable.  
3. Independence ⇔ joint = product of marginals for **every** cell.  
4. Linearity never needs independence; $E(XY)=E(X)E(Y)$ does.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Joint table not summing to 1 | Always normalise / check |
| Claiming independence from a few cells | Check all pairs |
| $E(XY)=E(X)E(Y)$ without independence | False in general |
| Confusing marginal of $X$ with a row of the joint | Marginal sums the row |

---

## Worked exam-style example / 考试风格例题

**Problem.** Joint: $p(0,0)=0.1$, $p(0,1)=0.2$, $p(1,0)=0.3$, $p(1,1)=0.4$. Find $p_X(1)$ and test independence.

**Solution.** $p_X(1)=0.7$. $p_X(0)p_Y(0)=0.12\neq 0.1$ → not independent.
