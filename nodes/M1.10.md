# M1.10 Joint Discrete Distributions, Marginals, Independence / 联合离散分布、边缘与独立

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M1.6 Independence](M1.6.md), [M1.8 Random Variables, PMF, CDF](M1.8.md)

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Two numerical measurements on the same experiment—two dice, height and weight class, two test indicators—need a **joint** law. From it we recover each variable alone (marginals) and test whether they factor (independence).

同一次试验上的两个数值——两颗骰子、两类指示——需要**联合**律。由此得到各自的边缘，并检验是否因子分解（独立）。

---

## Step 1: Joint PMF / 第一步：联合 PMF

$$p_{X,Y}(x,y)=\Pr(X=x,\,Y=y).$$

Non-negative and $\sum_x\sum_y p_{X,Y}(x,y)=1$.

---

## Step 2: Marginals / 第二步：边缘

$$p_X(x)=\sum_y p_{X,Y}(x,y), \qquad p_Y(y)=\sum_x p_{X,Y}(x,y).$$

“Sum out” the variable you do not need.

把不需要的变量求和消掉。

---

## Step 3: Independence of RVs / 第三步：随机变量独立

$X,Y$ independent iff

$$p_{X,Y}(x,y)=p_X(x)\,p_Y(y) \quad\text{for all }x,y.$$

Then $E[g(X)h(Y)]=E[g(X)]E[h(Y)]$ for suitable $g,h$.  
**Linearity of expectation never needs independence**; products of expectations do.

期望线性性从不需要独立；期望的乘积才需要。

---

## Step 4: Hand example / 第四步：手算

Two independent fair dice: $p(x,y)=1/36$. Marginal of $X$ is uniform on $\{1,\ldots,6\}$.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

- Reading a joint table without checking that entries sum to 1.  
- Claiming independence from a few cells instead of all pairs.  
- Using $E(XY)=E(X)E(Y)$ without independence (false in general).

---

## Practice / 练习

Joint PMF: $p(0,0)=0.1$, $p(0,1)=0.2$, $p(1,0)=0.3$, $p(1,1)=0.4$. Find $p_X(1)$ and check independence.

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

$p_X(1)=0.3+0.4=0.7$.  
$p_X(0)p_Y(0)=(0.3)(0.4)=0.12\neq 0.1=p(0,0)$ → not independent.

</details>
