# M1.10 Joint Discrete Distributions, Marginals, Independence / 联合离散分布、边缘与独立

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M1.6 Independence](M1.6.md), [M1.8 Random Variables, PMF, CDF](M1.8.md)

**Source anchors**: SDST2601A Ch7 discrete joint; continuous joint in [M8.10](M8.10.md).

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Two numerical measurements on the same experiment need a **joint** law. From it we recover marginals and test whether they factor (independence).

同一次试验上的两个数值需要**联合**律。由此得到边缘，并检验是否因子分解（独立）。

---

## Step 1: Joint PMF / 第一步：联合 PMF

$$p_{X,Y}(x,y)=\Pr(X=x,\,Y=y).$$

$p\ge 0$ and $\sum_x\sum_y p=1$. $\Pr((X,Y)\in A)=\sum_{(x,y)\in A}p(x,y)$.

---

## Step 2: Marginals / 第二步：边缘

$$p_X(x)=\sum_y p_{X,Y}(x,y),\qquad p_Y(y)=\sum_x p_{X,Y}(x,y).$$

---

## Step 3: Independence of RVs / 第三步：随机变量独立

$X,Y$ independent iff $p_{X,Y}(x,y)=p_X(x)p_Y(y)$ for all $x,y$.

Linearity $E(X+Y)=E(X)+E(Y)$ never needs independence; $E(XY)=E(X)E(Y)$ does.

---

## Step 4: Hand example / 第四步：手算

Independent dice: $p(x,y)=1/36$.  
Dependent: $p(0,0)=0.1$, $p(0,1)=0.2$, $p(1,0)=0.3$, $p(1,1)=0.4$.  
$p_X(1)=0.7$; $p_X(0)p_Y(0)=0.12\neq 0.1$ → not independent.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Joint PMF = complete discrete law of the pair.  
2. Marginal = sum out the other variable.  
3. Independence ⇔ joint = product of marginals for every cell.  
4. Linearity never needs independence; product of expectations does.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Joint table not summing to 1 | Always check |
| Claiming independence from a few cells | Check all pairs |
| $E(XY)=E(X)E(Y)$ without independence | False in general |

## Practice / 练习（选择题）

**1.** Two independent fair dice. $\Pr(X=6,Y=6)=$

A. $1/6$  
B. $1/12$  
C. $1/36$  
D. $1/2$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$(1/6)\cdot(1/6)=1/36$.  
Answer: **C**.
</details>

**2.** Joint $p(0,0)=0.1$, $p(0,1)=0.2$, $p(1,0)=0.3$, $p(1,1)=0.4$. $p_X(1)=$

A. $0.3$  
B. $0.4$  
C. $0.7$  
D. $1$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$0.3+0.4=0.7$.  
Answer: **C**.
</details>

**3.** Same table. $p_Y(1)=$

A. $0.2$  
B. $0.4$  
C. $0.6$  
D. $0.8$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$0.2+0.4=0.6$.  
Answer: **C**.
</details>

**4.** Independence requires joint =

A. sum of marginals  
B. product of marginals for every cell  
C. max of marginals  
D. $1$ only

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$p(x,y)=p_X(x)p_Y(y)$ all $x,y$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**5.** In the dependent table, $p_X(0)p_Y(0)=$

A. $0.1$  
B. $0.12$  
C. $0.3$  
D. $0$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$0.3\cdot 0.4=0.12\neq 0.1$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**6.** $E(X+Y)=E(X)+E(Y)$

A. requires independence  
B. always holds (linearity)  
C. requires $E(XY)=0$  
D. only for dice

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Linearity is an identity on the joint sum.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**7.** Joint PMF must satisfy

A. $\sum\sum p=0$  
B. $\sum\sum p=1$ and $p\ge 0$  
C. $p=1$ everywhere  
D. $p_X=p_Y$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Normalisation and non-negativity.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**8.** Marginal $p_X(x)$ is obtained by

A. multiplying rows  
B. summing the joint over $y$  
C. taking max over $y$  
D. $p(x,x)$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Sum out $y$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>
