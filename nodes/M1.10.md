# M1.10 Independence of Random Variables, Joint Distribution and Marginalization / 随机变量独立、联合分布与边缘化

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M1.6 Independence](M1.6.md), [M1.8 Random Variables, PMF, CDF](M1.8.md)

---

## Content / 内容

The joint PMF of discrete random variables X and Y is p_{X,Y}(x,y) = P(X=x, Y=y). The marginal PMF of X is obtained by summing out y: p_X(x) = Σ_y p_{X,Y}(x,y).

离散随机变量 X、Y 的联合 PMF 为 p_{X,Y}(x,y) = P(X=x, Y=y)。X 的边缘 PMF 通过对 y 求和得到：p_X(x) = Σ_y p_{X,Y}(x,y)。

X and Y are independent when p_{X,Y}(x,y) = p_X(x) p_Y(y) for all x,y. Equivalently, P(X ∈ A, Y ∈ B) = P(X ∈ A) P(Y ∈ B) for all events A,B. Independence implies E[g(X)h(Y)] = E[g(X)] E[h(Y)] for (suitable) functions g,h.

X 与 Y 独立当且仅当对所有 x,y 有 p_{X,Y}(x,y) = p_X(x) p_Y(y)。等价地，对任意事件 A,B 有 P(X ∈ A, Y ∈ B) = P(X ∈ A) P(Y ∈ B)。独立蕴含对（合适的）函数 g,h 有 E[g(X)h(Y)] = E[g(X)] E[h(Y)]。

Linearity of expectation does not require independence. The joint probability of several independent trials is the product of the individual probabilities.

期望线性性不需要独立。多个独立试验的联合概率是各项概率的乘积。

## Example / 例子

Two independent fair dice. P(X=1, Y=2) = (1/6)·(1/6) = 1/36. Marginal of X is uniform on {1,…,6}.

两枚独立公平骰子。P(X=1, Y=2) = (1/6)·(1/6) = 1/36。X 的边缘在 {1,…,6} 上均匀。

## Practice Problem / 练习题

The joint PMF of (X,Y) is uniform on the four points (0,0),(0,1),(1,0),(1,1). Are X and Y independent? Compute the marginals to decide.

(X,Y) 的联合 PMF 在四点 (0,0),(0,1),(1,0),(1,1) 上均匀。X 与 Y 是否独立？计算边缘来判断。

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

Each point has probability 1/4. Marginal of X: P(X=0)=1/2, P(X=1)=1/2; same for Y. Product of marginals equals the joint, so they are independent.

每点概率 1/4。X 的边缘：P(X=0)=1/2，P(X=1)=1/2；Y 同样。边缘乘积等于联合，故独立。

</details>
