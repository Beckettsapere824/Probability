# M4.9 Discrete Uniform and Non-uniform Dice Models / 离散均匀/非均匀骰子模型

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M1.9 Classical Probability](M1.9.md), [M2.19 Non-uniform Dice](M2.19.md)

---

## Content / 内容

A fair n-sided die produces the discrete uniform distribution on {1,…,n}; the total number of outcomes of k independent rolls is n^k. When the faces carry unequal probabilities the ordinary generating function (or direct multinomial expansion) is used to obtain the distribution of the sum. Conditions such as a≤b≤c are counted by multisets; strict inequalities a<b<c are counted by ordinary combinations. Difference constraints are handled by enumerating admissible difference values. Consecutive identical faces are often most easily treated via the complement.

公平 n 面骰产生 {1,…,n} 上的离散均匀分布；k 次独立投掷的总结果数为 n^k。当面概率不等时，用普通生成函数（或直接多项式展开）得到和的分布。条件如 a≤b≤c 用多重集合计数；严格不等式 a<b<c 用普通组合计数。差约束通过枚举合法差值处理。连续同面通常最易用补事件处理。

## Example / 例子

Two fair dice. P(sum=7)=6/36. Number of outcomes with a≤b≤c for three ordered dice faces: C(6+3−1,3).

两枚公平骰。P(和=7)=6/36。三枚有序骰面 a≤b≤c 的结果数：C(6+3−1,3)。

## Practice Problem / 练习题

Three fair dice are rolled. How many outcomes satisfy a < b < c?

三枚公平骰。有多少结果满足 a < b < c？

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

C(6,3)=20 (each strictly increasing triple corresponds to one combination).

C(6,3)=20（每个严格递增三元组对应一个组合）。

</details>
