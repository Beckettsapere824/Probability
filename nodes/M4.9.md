# M4.9 Discrete Uniform / 离散均匀

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M1.8](M1.8.md), [M1.9](M1.9.md)

**Source anchors**: equal mass on $\{1,\ldots,n\}$; mean $(n+1)/2$; classical dice.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

$X$ takes values $1,\ldots,n$ each with probability $1/n$. Baseline fair die / fair spinner model.

$X$ 在 $\{1,\ldots,n\}$ 上等可能；公平骰子的基线模型。

---

## Step 1: PMF and mean / 第一步：PMF 与期望

$p(k)=1/n$, $E(X)=(n+1)/2$.  
$\mathrm{Var}(X)=(n^{2}-1)/12$.

---

## Step 2: CDF / 第二步：CDF

$F(x)=\lfloor x\rfloor/n$ for $x$ in range (care at integers).

---

## Step 3: Hand check / 第三步：手算

Fair die $n=6$: $E=3.5$, $\mathrm{Var}=35/12$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Equal atoms — classical probability on a finite set.  
2. Mean is the midpoint of the support.  
3. Variance grows like $n^{2}/12$.  
4. Continuous uniform is the density analogue on an interval.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Using continuous uniform variance $n^{2}/12$ without checking discrete formula | Discrete is $(n^{2}-1)/12$ |
| Starting support at 0 without adjusting mean | State the set |
| Treating outcomes as ordered samples | Single draw from the set |

---
