# M2.5 Stars and Bars / 隔板法

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M2.1](M2.1.md)

**Source anchors**: non-negative integer solutions; multisets; $\binom{n+r-1}{r-1}$.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Number of non-negative integer solutions to $x_1+\cdots+x_r=n$, or equivalently the number of multisets of size $n$ from $r$ types (unordered sampling with replacement).

非负整数解 $x_1+\cdots+x_r=n$ 的个数，或从 $r$ 类中取大小为 $n$ 的多重集。

---

## Step 1: Formula / 第一步：公式

$$\binom{n+r-1}{r-1}=\binom{n+r-1}{n}.$$

Place $n$ stars in a row and insert $r-1$ bars to split into $r$ groups.

---

## Step 2: Positive solutions / 第二步：正整数解

If $x_i\ge 1$, set $y_i=x_i-1\ge 0$, then $\sum y_i=n-r$, so $\binom{(n-r)+r-1}{r-1}=\binom{n-1}{r-1}$.

---

## Step 3: Hand checks / 第三步：手算

$x+y+z=5$, $x,y,z\ge 0$: $\binom{5+3-1}{3-1}=21$.  
Two dice sum to 7 (ordered faces already handled by $6^{2}$ model; stars-and-bars is for indistinguishable units into bins).

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Stars and bars = unordered with replacement = non-negative solutions.  
2. Bars create $r$ bins; stars are identical units.  
3. Positive constraint shifts by $r$ and changes the upper index.  
4. Do not use this when order of the $x$-assignment is already distinguished by labels in a different model.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Using $\binom{n}{r}$ instead of $\binom{n+r-1}{r-1}$ | Wrong cell |
| Forgetting $\ge 0$ vs $\ge 1$ | Apply the shift |
| Using stars and bars when sequences are ordered | Use $n^{k}$ |

---

## Practice / 练习（选择题）

**1.** Non-negative solutions to $x+y+z=5$:

A. $\binom{5}{3}=10$  
B. $\binom{7}{2}=21$  
C. $5^{3}$  
D. $P(5,3)$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$\binom{5+3-1}{3-1}=21$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**2.** Stars and bars counts

A. ordered without replacement  
B. non-negative solutions / multisets  
C. only permutations  
D. $\binom{n}{k}$ only

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Unordered with replacement.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**3.** Formula for non-negative solutions of $x_1+\cdots+x_r=n$:

A. $\binom{n}{r}$  
B. $\binom{n+r-1}{r-1}$  
C. $n^{r}$  
D. $r^{n}$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Standard.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**4.** Positive integer solutions to $x+y+z=5$:

A. $21$  
B. $\binom{4}{2}=6$  
C. $10$  
D. $15$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$\binom{5-1}{3-1}=\binom{4}{2}=6$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**5.** Multiset of size 3 from 5 types (order irrelevant, repeats OK):

A. $\binom{5}{3}$  
B. $5^{3}$  
C. $\binom{5+3-1}{3}=35$  
D. $P(5,3)$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Stars and bars.  
Answer: **C**.
</details>

**6.** Number of bars for $r$ variables is

A. $r$  
B. $r-1$  
C. $n$  
D. $n-1$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$r-1$ dividers.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**7.** $\binom{n+r-1}{n}$ equals $\binom{n+r-1}{r-1}$ by

A. definition of $P$  
B. symmetry of binomial coefficients  
C. replacement  
D. order

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$\binom{m}{k}=\binom{m}{m-k}$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**8.** Stars and bars is the wrong tool when

A. variables are non-negative integers summing to $n$  
B. you need ordered sequences with replacement  
C. you count multisets  
D. $x_i\ge 0$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Ordered sequences use $n^{k}$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>
