# M2.5 Stars and Bars / 隔板法

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M2.1](M2.1.md)

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Number of non-negative integer solutions to $x_1+\cdots+x_r=n$ is $\binom{n+r-1}{r-1}$.

---

## Practice / 练习

Non-neg solutions to $x+y+z=5$?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>$\binom{5+3-1}{3-1}=21$.</details>
