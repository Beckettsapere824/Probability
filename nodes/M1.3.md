# M1.3 Conditional Probability and Multiplication Rule / 条件概率与乘法公式

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M1.1 Sample Space, Events, Probability Axioms](M1.1.md)

**Source / 来源**: SDST2601A Chapter 3 §3.2

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Often you learn something about the experiment and must update probabilities. “Given that the die showed an even face, what is the chance it is a six?” The sample space effectively shrinks to the conditioning event.

你常常先知道试验的一部分信息，再更新概率。「已知骰子是偶数点，是六点的机会多大？」样本空间在效果上缩到条件事件上。

---

## Step 1: Definition / 第一步：定义

If $\Pr(B)>0$,

$$\Pr(A\mid B)=\frac{\Pr(A\cap B)}{\Pr(B)}.$$

The denominator is the **conditioning event**, not $\Omega$. On the restricted world “$B$ occurred”, $\Pr(\cdot\mid B)$ still obeys the probability axioms.

分母是**条件事件**，不是全集。在「$B$ 已发生」的世界里，$\Pr(\cdot\mid B)$ 仍满足概率公理。

---

## Step 2: Multiplication rule / 第二步：乘法公式

Rearrange the definition:

$$\Pr(A\cap B)=\Pr(A\mid B)\Pr(B)=\Pr(B\mid A)\Pr(A).$$

Chain rule for three events:

$$\Pr(A\cap B\cap C)=\Pr(A)\,\Pr(B\mid A)\,\Pr(C\mid A\cap B).$$

---

## Step 3: Hand example / 第三步：手算

Fair die. $A=$“even”, $B=$“greater than 3”.  
$A\cap B=\{4,6\}$, $\Pr(A\cap B)=1/3$, $\Pr(B)=1/2$, so $\Pr(A\mid B)=(1/3)/(1/2)=2/3$.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

- Swapping $\Pr(A\mid B)$ with $\Pr(B\mid A)$ (base-rate / Bayes confusion).  
- Dividing by $\Pr(A)$ when the condition was $B$.  
- Conditioning on a null event ($\Pr(B)=0$).

---

## Practice / 练习

Two fair coins. Given at least one head, probability both are heads?

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

$\Omega$ four points. “At least one head” has three points; both heads is one of them → $1/3$ (not $1/2$).

</details>
