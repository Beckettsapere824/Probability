# M3.12 Covariance and Variance of Sums / 协方差与和的方差

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M3.3 Variance](M3.3.md), [M1.10 Joint discrete](M1.10.md)

**Source anchors**: SDST2601A Ch7 covariance / correlation; independent ⇒ uncorrelated; hypergeometric negative Cov.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

$\mathrm{Var}(X+Y)$ is **not** always $\mathrm{Var}(X)+\mathrm{Var}(Y)$. When $X$ and $Y$ tend to move together (or in opposite directions), a correction term appears: **covariance**.

$\mathrm{Var}(X+Y)$ **不总是** $\mathrm{Var}(X)+\mathrm{Var}(Y)$。当 $X$ 与 $Y$ 同向或反向联动时，修正项是**协方差**。

---

## Step 1: Definition / 第一步：定义

$$\mathrm{Cov}(X,Y)=E\bigl[(X-\mu_X)(Y-\mu_Y)\bigr]=E(XY)-E(X)E(Y).$$

The second form is the computational workhorse (needs $E(XY)$ via joint law or LOTUS on the pair).

计算常用 $E(XY)-\mu_X\mu_Y$。

---

## Step 2: Variance of a sum / 第二步：和的方差

$$\mathrm{Var}(X+Y)=\mathrm{Var}(X)+\mathrm{Var}(Y)+2\mathrm{Cov}(X,Y).$$

$$\mathrm{Var}(X-Y)=\mathrm{Var}(X)+\mathrm{Var}(Y)-2\mathrm{Cov}(X,Y).$$

If $\mathrm{Cov}(X,Y)=0$ (uncorrelated), variances add.  
**Independence ⇒ uncorrelated** (because $E(XY)=E(X)E(Y)$). The converse is false in general: zero covariance does not imply independence.

独立 ⇒ 不相关；不相关 ⇏ 独立。

---

## Step 3: Correlation / 第三步：相关系数

$$\rho_{X,Y}=\frac{\mathrm{Cov}(X,Y)}{\sigma_X\sigma_Y}\in[-1,1]$$

(when $\sigma_X\sigma_Y>0$). Scale-free measure of **linear** association. $|\rho|=1$ iff $Y$ is an affine function of $X$ almost surely.

相关系数是线性关联的无量纲度量。

---

## Step 4: Properties / 第四步：性质

- $\mathrm{Cov}(X,X)=\mathrm{Var}(X)$.  
- $\mathrm{Cov}(aX+b,cY+d)=ac\,\mathrm{Cov}(X,Y)$.  
- Bilinearity: $\mathrm{Cov}(X_1+X_2,Y)=\mathrm{Cov}(X_1,Y)+\mathrm{Cov}(X_2,Y)$.

---

## Step 5: Hand example / 第五步：手算

$\mathrm{Var}(X)=4$, $\mathrm{Var}(Y)=9$, $\mathrm{Cov}(X,Y)=1$.  
$\mathrm{Var}(X+Y)=4+9+2=15$, $\mathrm{Var}(X-Y)=4+9-2=11$.

Hypergeometric colour counts in a fixed-size sample typically have $\mathrm{Cov}<0$: more red leaves fewer slots for white.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Covariance corrects the variance of a sum for dependence.  
2. Independence ⇒ Cov = 0; Cov = 0 ⇏ independence.  
3. Correlation is Cov scaled to $[-1,1]$.  
4. Fixed-size sampling induces negative covariance between type counts.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Adding variances for dependent summands | Include $\pm 2\mathrm{Cov}$ |
| Concluding independence from Cov = 0 | Only one direction holds |
| Forgetting $E(XY)$ needs the joint | Marginal means are not enough |
| Mixing $\rho$ with Cov in formulas | Check dimensions / scaling |

---

## Worked exam-style example / 考试风格例题

**Problem.** $\mathrm{Var}(X)=4$, $\mathrm{Var}(Y)=9$, $\mathrm{Cov}(X,Y)=1$. Find $\mathrm{Var}(X+Y)$ and $\mathrm{Var}(X-Y)$.

**Solution.** $15$ and $11$.

## Practice / 练习

1. If $X,Y$ independent, $\mathrm{Var}(X+Y)$?

2. $\mathrm{Cov}(3X,2Y)$ in terms of $\mathrm{Cov}(X,Y)$?

3. Why can Cov be zero without independence?

<details>
<summary>Solution sketches / 解答提示</summary>

1. $\mathrm{Var}(X)+\mathrm{Var}(Y)$.

2. $6\,\mathrm{Cov}(X,Y)$.

3. Dependence can be nonlinear; Cov only sees linear association.

</details>
