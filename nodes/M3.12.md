# M3.12 Covariance and Variance of Sums / 协方差与和的方差

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M3.3 Variance](M3.3.md), [M1.10 Joint discrete](M1.10.md)

**Source / 来源**: SDST2601A Chapter 7 § covariance / correlation ideas

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

$\mathrm{Var}(X+Y)$ is **not** always $\mathrm{Var}(X)+\mathrm{Var}(Y)$. The correction term is covariance—how $X$ and $Y$ move together.

$\mathrm{Var}(X+Y)$ **不总是** $\mathrm{Var}(X)+\mathrm{Var}(Y)$。修正项是协方差——$X$ 与 $Y$ 如何一起动。

---

## Step 1: Definition / 第一步：定义

$$\mathrm{Cov}(X,Y)=E\bigl[(X-\mu_X)(Y-\mu_Y)\bigr]=E(XY)-E(X)E(Y).$$

---

## Step 2: Variance of a sum / 第二步：和的方差

$$\mathrm{Var}(X+Y)=\mathrm{Var}(X)+\mathrm{Var}(Y)+2\mathrm{Cov}(X,Y).$$

If $\mathrm{Cov}(X,Y)=0$ (uncorrelated), variances add. Independence $\Rightarrow$ uncorrelated; the converse is false in general.

独立 ⇒ 不相关；不相关 ⇏ 独立。

---

## Step 3: Correlation / 第三步：相关系数

$$\rho=\frac{\mathrm{Cov}(X,Y)}{\sigma_X\sigma_Y}\in[-1,1]$$

(when $\sigma_X\sigma_Y>0$). Scale-free measure of linear association.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

- Adding variances for dependent summands without the $2\mathrm{Cov}$ term.  
- Concluding independence from $\mathrm{Cov}=0$.

---

## Practice / 练习

$\mathrm{Var}(X)=4$, $\mathrm{Var}(Y)=9$, $\mathrm{Cov}(X,Y)=1$. Find $\mathrm{Var}(X+Y)$ and $\mathrm{Var}(X-Y)$.

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

$\mathrm{Var}(X+Y)=4+9+2=15$.  
$\mathrm{Var}(X-Y)=4+9-2=11$.

</details>
