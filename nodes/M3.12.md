# M3.12 Covariance and Variance of Sums / 协方差与方差加法

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M3.3 Variance](M3.3.md)

---

## Content / 内容

The variance of a sum expands as Var(X+Y) = Var(X) + Var(Y) + 2 Cov(X,Y), where Cov(X,Y) = E(XY) − E(X)E(Y). Independence implies uncorrelatedness (covariance zero), but the converse is false. When a sum of indicators is considered, the variance therefore contains all pairwise covariance terms unless the indicators are independent.

和的方差展开为 Var(X+Y) = Var(X) + Var(Y) + 2 Cov(X,Y)，其中 Cov(X,Y) = E(XY) − E(X)E(Y)。独立蕴含不相关（协方差为零），但逆命题不成立。指示和的方差因此包含所有成对协方差项，除非指示独立。

The identity is the precise reason why “variance adds for independent summands”.

该恒等式正是“独立时方差可加”的精确理由。

## Example / 例子

Two independent Bernoulli(p) variables: Cov = 0, Var(X+Y) = 2p(1−p). If they are the indicators of the same event, Cov = p(1−p) and Var(X+Y) = 4p(1−p) wait—no: if identical then Var(2X) = 4 Var(X).

两个独立 Bernoulli(p)：Cov = 0，Var(X+Y) = 2p(1−p)。若它们是同一事件的指示，则变为 Var(2X) = 4 Var(X)。

## Practice Problem / 练习题

X and Y are indicators of events A and B with P(A)=P(B)=1/2 and P(A∩B)=1/3. Compute Cov(X,Y) and Var(X+Y).

X、Y 分别为事件 A、B 的指示，P(A)=P(B)=1/2，P(A∩B)=1/3。求 Cov(X,Y) 与 Var(X+Y)。

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

E(X)=E(Y)=1/2, E(XY)=1/3, Cov=1/3−1/4=1/12. Var(X)=Var(Y)=1/4. Var(X+Y)=1/4+1/4+2·(1/12)=2/3.

E(X)=E(Y)=1/2，E(XY)=1/3，Cov=1/3−1/4=1/12。Var(X)=Var(Y)=1/4。Var(X+Y)=1/4+1/4+2·(1/12)=2/3。

</details>
