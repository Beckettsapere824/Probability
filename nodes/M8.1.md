# M8.1 Continuous Random Variables and pdf / 连续随机变量与 pdf

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M1.8 Random Variables, PMF, CDF](M1.8.md)

**Source / 来源**: SDST2601A/STAT2601A Chapter 5 §5.1 (K.P. WAT), Definition 5.1, Example 5.1

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

In [M1.8](M1.8.md) a discrete random variable puts positive mass on isolated points: you read probabilities from a pmf $p(x)=\Pr(X=x)$ and **sum** them.

在 [M1.8](M1.8.md) 里，离散随机变量把正概率贴在孤立点上：用 pmf $p(x)=\Pr(X=x)$，**求和**得到事件概率。

Many quantities in the course are not like that—height, waiting time, monthly salary. Between 170.0 cm and 170.1 cm there are infinitely many possible values. If you tried to give every exact height a positive probability, the total would blow up.

课程里大量量不是这样——身高、等待时间、月薪。170.0 cm 与 170.1 cm 之间有无穷多个可能取值。若给每个精确身高都塞一点正概率，总和会爆掉。

So the core question is:

核心问题是：

> **How do we say, rigorously, that a continuous quantity is more likely to fall in one interval than another—when every single point has probability zero?**
>
> **在「单点概率为 0」的前提下，如何严格比较连续量落在不同区间的机会？**

The answer is a **density** $f$ and **area under the curve**, not height at a point.

答案是**密度** $f$ 与曲线下的**面积**，不是某一点的高度。

---

## Step 1: From histogram to density / 第一步：从直方图到密度

Lecture notes open Chapter 5 with a histogram of monthly salaries. Each bar’s **area** (relative frequency density × class width) is proportional to how often that salary band appeared; total area is 1.

课件用月薪直方图开场。每个柱的**面积**（相对频率密度 × 组距）对应该薪资段出现的频率；总面积为 1。

If you refine the bins, the stepped histogram can be imagined approaching a smooth curve $f(x)$. That curve is still read by **area**:

组距越细，直方图可以想象成逼近一条光滑曲线 $f(x)$。读它的方式仍然是**面积**：

$$
\Pr(a < X < b) \approx \text{area under } f \text{ between } a \text{ and } b.
$$

Height $f(x)$ alone is **not** a probability. Units are “probability per unit of $x$”.

单独的高度 $f(x)$ **不是**概率。单位是「单位 $x$ 上的概率」。

---

## Step 2: Formal definition (Def 5.1) / 第二步：形式定义

A random variable $X$ is of the **(absolute) continuous type** if its cdf can be written

若 cdf 可写成下式，则称 $X$ 为（绝对）**连续型**：

$$
F(x) = \Pr(X \le x) = \int_{-\infty}^{x} f(t)\,dt, \qquad -\infty < x < \infty,
$$

for some $f:\mathbb{R}\to[0,\infty)$. The function $f$ is the **probability density function (pdf)**.

其中 $f$ 称为**概率密度函数 (pdf)**。

Discrete behaviour was “sum the pmf on points”. Continuous behaviour is “integrate the pdf on sets”.

离散是「对点上的 pmf 求和」；连续是「对集合上的 pdf 积分」。

---

## Step 3: Properties you will use every week / 第三步：每周都会用的性质

1. Where $F$ is differentiable, $f(x)=F'(x)$ (density is the derivative of the cdf).  
2. $f(x)\ge 0$ for all $x$.  
3. $\displaystyle\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f(x)\,dx = 1$.  
4. $\Pr(X\in A)=\displaystyle\int_{A} f(x)\,dx$.  
5. For continuous $X$:
   - (a) $\Pr(X=a)=0$ for every $a$;
   - (b) endpoints do not matter:
     $$\Pr(a\le X\le b)=\Pr(a<X<b)=\int_{a}^{b} f = F(b)-F(a);$$
   - (c) $F$ is continuous everywhere.

1. $F$ 可微处 $f=F'$。  
2. $f\ge 0$。  
3. 全实轴积分为 1。  
4. 事件概率 = 在事件上积分 $f$。  
5. 单点概率为 0；区间端点开闭不改变概率。

**Intuition to keep:** continuous probability lives in **intervals as area**. Point masses are a discrete idea.

**要记住的直觉：** 连续概率在**区间面积**里；点质量是离散世界的概念。

## Step 4: Hand calculation — normalize a pdf (Example 5.1) / 第四步：手算归一化

Suppose the notes give an un-normalized shape on $(0,2)$:

课件给一个尚未归一的形状：

$$
f(x) = \begin{cases} c(2x-x^{2}) & 0<x<2, \\ 0 & \text{otherwise.} \end{cases}
$$

Enforce property 3:

用性质 3：

$$
\int_{0}^{2} c(2x-x^{2})\,dx = c\Bigl[x^{2}-\frac{x^{3}}{3}\Bigr]_{0}^{2} = c\cdot\frac{4}{3} = 1
\implies c=\frac{3}{4}.
$$

Now interval probabilities are ordinary integrals, e.g. $\Pr(0<X<1)=\int_{0}^{1}\frac{3}{4}(2x-x^{2})\,dx$.

之后区间概率就是普通积分。

## Summary / 小结

| Discrete (M1.8) | Continuous (this node) |
|-----------------|------------------------|
| pmf $p(x)=\Pr(X=x)$ | pdf $f$ (not a probability) |
| $\sum p=1$ | $\int f=1$ |
| $\Pr(A)=\sum_{x\in A}p(x)$ | $\Pr(A)=\int_{A}f$ |
| Points can carry mass | Every single point has mass 0 |

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

- Reading $f(x)$ as $\Pr(X=x)$.  
- Changing an endpoint and expecting the probability to change.  
- Computing “means” from a formula that was never normalized ($\int f\neq 1$).

## Practice / 练习

Let

$$
f(x)=\begin{cases} kx(1-x) & 0<x<1, \\ 0 & \text{otherwise.} \end{cases}
$$

1. Find $k$.  
2. Write $F(x)$ for $0<x<1$.  
3. Compute $\Pr(0.2<X<0.5)$.

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

$\int_{0}^{1} kx(1-x)\,dx = k/6 = 1\Rightarrow k=6$.  
$F(x)=\int_{0}^{x} 6t(1-t)\,dt = 3x^{2}-2x^{3}$ on $(0,1)$.  
$\Pr(0.2<X<0.5)=F(0.5)-F(0.2)=0.5-0.104=0.396$.

</details>
