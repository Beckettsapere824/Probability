# M2.12a Generating Function Constraints / 生成函数约束

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M2.12](M2.12.md)

**Source anchors**: factors for “at least one”, “at most m”, unrestricted nonnegative.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Translate constraints on how many times a type appears into factors of an ordinary generating function.

把“至少 1 个”“至多 m 个”等约束写成 OGF 因子。

---

## Step 1: Unrestricted nonnegative / 第一步：非负无限制

$1+x+x^{2}+\cdots=1/(1-x)$.

---

## Step 2: At least one / 第二步：至少一个

$x+x^{2}+\cdots=x/(1-x)$.

---

## Step 3: Hand check / 第三步：手算

Two variables each ≥1, sum $n$: GF $x^{2}/(1-x)^{2}$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Factor per type / per variable.  
2. Product of factors for independent constraints.  
3. Lower bounds shift by multiplying $x^{m}$.  
4. Upper bounds truncate the geometric series.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Using $1/(1-x)$ when at least one required | Start from $x$ |
| Forgetting product structure | One factor per constrained quantity |
| Extracting wrong coefficient index | Track the total exponent |

---
