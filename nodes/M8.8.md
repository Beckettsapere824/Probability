# M8.8 Beta Distribution / Beta 分布

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M8.1](M8.1.md)

**Source anchors**: law on $(0,1)$; conjugate prior for Bernoulli; mean $\alpha/(\alpha+\beta)$.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Model a continuous proportion on $(0,1)$, and use Beta as the **conjugate prior** for a Bernoulli/binomial likelihood in Bayesian updating.

在 $(0,1)$ 上为比例建模；Beta 作为 Bernoulli 的共轭先验。

---

## Step 1: Density / 第一步：密度

$$f(x)=\frac{\Gamma(\alpha+\beta)}{\Gamma(\alpha)\Gamma(\beta)}x^{\alpha-1}(1-x)^{\beta-1},\quad 0<x<1,\ \alpha,\beta>0.$$

---

## Step 2: Mean / 第二步：期望

$$E(X)=\frac{\alpha}{\alpha+\beta}.$$

$\mathrm{Beta}(1,1)=U(0,1)$.

---

## Step 3: Conjugacy sketch / 第三步：共轭简述

Prior $\mathrm{Beta}(\alpha,\beta)$, $s$ successes in $n$ Bernoulli trials ⇒ posterior $\mathrm{Beta}(\alpha+s,\beta+n-s)$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Beta is flexible on $(0,1)$ via two shape parameters.  
2. Uniform is the special case $\alpha=\beta=1$.  
3. Conjugacy keeps the posterior in the same family.  
4. Mean is a weighted average of prior mean and MLE.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Using Beta outside $(0,1)$ without transform | Support is unit interval |
| Confusing $\alpha,\beta$ with mean/variance directly | Use formulas |
| Forgetting posterior updates add counts | $\alpha+s$, $\beta+n-s$ |

---
