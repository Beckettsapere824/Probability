# M8.8 Beta Distribution / Beta 分布

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M8.1](M8.1.md)

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Continuous law on $(0,1)$ for proportions and Bayesian conjugate priors to Bernoulli.

---

## Formula / 公式

$$f(x)=\frac{\Gamma(\alpha+\beta)}{\Gamma(\alpha)\Gamma(\beta)}x^{\alpha-1}(1-x)^{\beta-1}, \quad 0<x<1.$$

$E(X)=\alpha/(\alpha+\beta)$.

---

## Practice / 练习

$\mathrm{Beta}(1,1)$ is which familiar law?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>$U(0,1)$.</details>
