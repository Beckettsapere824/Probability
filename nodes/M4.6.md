# M4.6 Poisson Distribution and Rare-Event Limit / 泊松分布与稀有事件极限

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.5 Series](M0.5.md), [M4.2 Binomial](M4.2.md)

---

## Content / 内容

The Poisson distribution with parameter λ has PMF
P(X=k) = e^{−λ} λ^k / k!, k=0,1,2,… .
The series identity Σ λ^k / k! = e^λ supplies the normalisation. Both the mean and the variance equal λ. The distribution arises as the limit of Binomial(n,p) when n→∞, p→0 and np→λ. It also appears as the limiting distribution of a sum of many rare, weakly dependent indicators (the classic secretary/matching problem yields Poisson(1)).

参数 λ 的泊松分布 PMF 为
P(X=k) = e^{−λ} λ^k / k!，k=0,1,2,… 。
级数恒等式 Σ λ^k / k! = e^λ 给出归一化。均值与方差都等于 λ。它作为 Binomial(n,p) 在 n→∞、p→0、np→λ 时的极限出现，也作为大量稀有、弱相依指示之和的极限分布出现（经典秘书/匹配问题给出 Poisson(1)）。

## Example / 例子

λ=2. P(X=0)=e^{−2}≈0.135. E(X)=Var(X)=2.
