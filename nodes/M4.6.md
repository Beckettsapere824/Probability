# M4.6 Poisson Distribution / 泊松分布

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M4.2 Binomial](M4.2.md)

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Count rare events in a large number of trials (or in continuous time with a constant rate): defects on a long wire, calls per minute, mutations. The Poisson law is the limit of $\mathrm{Binomial}(n,p)$ when $n\to\infty$, $p\to 0$, $np\to\lambda$.

在大量试验中数稀有事件（或连续时间常速率）：长导线瑕疵、每分钟来电、突变。泊松是 $n\to\infty$、$p\to 0$、$np\to\lambda$ 时二项的极限。

---

## Step 1: PMF / 第一步：PMF

$$X\sim\mathrm{Poisson}(\lambda), \qquad
\Pr(X=k)=e^{-\lambda}\frac{\lambda^{k}}{k!}, \quad k=0,1,2,\ldots.$$

---

## Step 2: Mean and variance / 第二步：均值与方差

$$E(X)=\lambda, \qquad \mathrm{Var}(X)=\lambda.$$

(Equal mean and variance is a quick diagnostic feature of Poisson.)

均值方差相等是泊松的快速识别特征。

---

## Step 3: Binomial limit sketch / 第三步：二项极限

$\binom{n}{k}p^{k}(1-p)^{n-k}$ with $p=\lambda/n$ tends to $e^{-\lambda}\lambda^{k}/k!$ as $n\to\infty$.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

- Using Poisson when events are strongly dependent or rates are not stable.  
- Forgetting the $e^{-\lambda}$ factor.  
- Confusing $\lambda$ (mean count) with a Bernoulli $p$.

---

## Practice / 练习

$X\sim\mathrm{Poisson}(2)$. Find $\Pr(X=0)$ and $E(X)$.

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

$\Pr(X=0)=e^{-2}\approx 0.1353$, $E(X)=2$.

</details>
