# M3.4 Linear Transforms of Mean and Variance / 线性变换的均值方差

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M3.3](M3.3.md)

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

$Y=aX+b$: $E(Y)=aE(X)+b$, $\mathrm{Var}(Y)=a^{2}\mathrm{Var}(X)$.

---

## Practice / 练习

$E(X)=2$, $\mathrm{Var}(X)=4$. $E(3X-1)$ and $\mathrm{Var}(3X-1)$?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>$5$ and $36$.</details>
