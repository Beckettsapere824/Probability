# M3.4 Linear Transforms of Mean and Variance / 线性变换的均值方差

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M3.3](M3.3.md)

**Source anchors**: $E(aX+b)=aE(X)+b$; $\mathrm{Var}(aX+b)=a^{2}\mathrm{Var}(X)$.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

How mean and variance transform under an affine map $Y=aX+b$.

仿射变换 $Y=aX+b$ 下期望与方差如何变化。

---

## Step 1: Mean / 第一步：期望

$$E(aX+b)=aE(X)+b.$$

---

## Step 2: Variance / 第二步：方差

$$\mathrm{Var}(aX+b)=a^{2}\mathrm{Var}(X).$$

---

## Step 3: Hand check / 第三步：手算

$E(X)=2$, $\mathrm{Var}(X)=4$. Then $E(3X-1)=5$, $\mathrm{Var}(3X-1)=36$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Mean tracks both scale and shift.  
2. Variance ignores shift; scales by $a^{2}$.  
3. $\mathrm{SD}(aX+b)=|a|\,\mathrm{SD}(X)$.  
4. Same formulas when moments exist.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| $\mathrm{Var}(aX+b)=a\mathrm{Var}(X)+b$ | Wrong; $a^{2}$, no $b$ |
| Using $|a|$ inside variance | Squared already |
| Applying without finite $\mathrm{Var}(X)$ | Existence first |

**2.** $\mathrm{Var}(aX+b)=$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Affine variance.  
Answer: **B**.

**3.** $E(X)=2$, $\mathrm{Var}(X)=4$. $E(3X-1)=$

A. $5$  
B. $6$  
C. $1$  
D. $4$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$3\cdot2-1=5$.  
Answer: **A**.
</details>

**4.** Same data: $\mathrm{Var}(3X-1)=$

A. $12$  
B. $36$  
C. $4$  
D. $9$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$9\cdot4=36$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**5.** $\mathrm{SD}(aX+b)=$

A. $a\,\mathrm{SD}(X)$ always  
B. $|a|\,\mathrm{SD}(X)$  
C. $\mathrm{SD}(X)+b$  
D. $0$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Square root of $a^{2}$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**6.** Adding a constant to $X$

Shift invariance of Var.

Constant random variable.
