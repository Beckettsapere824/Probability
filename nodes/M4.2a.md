# M4.2a Binomial Mode and Term Ratios / 二项众数与项比

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M4.2](M4.2.md)

**Source anchors**: $\Pr(k+1)/\Pr(k)$ ratio; mode near $(n+1)p$.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Locate the most probable value(s) of $\mathrm{Bin}(n,p)$ by studying when consecutive term ratios cross 1.

用相邻项比值是否大于 1，定位二项分布的众数。

---

## Step 1: Ratio / 第一步：比值

$$\frac{\Pr(k+1)}{\Pr(k)}=\frac{n-k}{k+1}\cdot\frac{p}{q},\quad q=1-p.$$

$\Pr(k+1)>\Pr(k)$ iff $k<(n+1)p-1$.

---

## Step 2: Mode / 第二步：众数

Mode is $\lfloor(n+1)p\rfloor$ (and possibly the previous integer when $(n+1)p$ is integer — two modes).

---

## Step 3: Hand check / 第三步：手算

$n=5$, $p=1/2$: $(n+1)p=3$, modes at 2 and 3 (symmetric).

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Ratio method avoids computing every binomial coefficient.  
2. Mode tracks the mean $np$ but is not always equal to it.  
3. When $(n+1)p$ is integer, two adjacent modes.  
4. Same idea works for other one-parameter discrete families.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Claiming mode always equals $np$ | Use $(n+1)p$ rule |
| Forgetting dual mode case | Check integer boundary |
| Ratio algebra sign errors | Rewrite inequality carefully |
