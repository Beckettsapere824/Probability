# M3.10 Conditional Expectation as RV / 条件期望作为随机变量

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M3.11](M3.11.md), [M5.1](M5.1.md)

**Source anchors**: $E(X\mid Y)$ is a function of $Y$; tower property $E(E(X\mid Y))=E(X)$.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

$E(X\mid Y=y)$ is a number; as $Y$ varies, $E(X\mid Y)$ is a **random variable**. Taking expectation again recovers $E(X)$.

$E(X\mid Y)$ 是随机变量；塔性质得 $E(X)$。

---

## Step 1: Two levels / 第一步：两层

- $E(X\mid Y=y)$: number.  
- $E(X\mid Y)$: RV $g(Y)$.

---

## Step 2: Tower property / 第二步：塔性质

$$E\bigl(E(X\mid Y)\bigr)=E(X).$$

---

## Step 3: Hand idea / 第三步：思路

Discrete: $E(X\mid Y)=\sum_y E(X\mid Y=y)I_{\{Y=y\}}$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Conditioning yields a random object.  
2. Tower is total probability for means.  
3. $E(X\mid Y)$ is best L2 predictor given $Y$.  
4. Independence ⇒ $E(X\mid Y)=E(X)$ a.s.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Treating $E(X\mid Y)$ as a number always | It is a RV |
| Forgetting tower when computing $E(X)$ | Iterate expectation |
| Writing $E(X\mid Y)=X$ always | Only if $X$ is $Y$-measurable |

---

**1.** $E(X\mid Y)$ is

B. a random variable (function of $Y$)  
C. equal to $X$ always  
D. undefined

Function of $Y$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**2.** Tower property:

A. $E(E(X\mid Y))=0$

D. only continuous

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>
