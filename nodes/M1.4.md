# M1.4 Law of Total Probability / 全概率公式

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M1.3 Conditional Probability](M1.3.md)

**Source / 来源**: SDST2601A Chapter 3 Theorem 3.2–3.3

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

$\Pr(B)$ may be hard, but if a partition $A_1,\ldots,A_n$ of $\Omega$ makes each $\Pr(B\mid A_i)$ easy, total probability rebuilds $\Pr(B)$ by weighting those pieces with the prior $\Pr(A_i)$.

$\Pr(B)$ 可能难算；若有划分 $A_1,\ldots,A_n$ 使每块 $\Pr(B\mid A_i)$ 容易，全概率用先验 $\Pr(A_i)$ 加权拼回 $\Pr(B)$。

---

## Step 1: Partition / 第一步：划分

Events $A_1,\ldots,A_n$ form a **partition** of $\Omega$ if they are pairwise disjoint and $\bigcup A_i=\Omega$.

---

## Step 2: Formula / 第二步：公式

$$\Pr(B)=\sum_{i=1}^{n}\Pr(B\mid A_i)\Pr(A_i).$$

Continuous analogue later: integrate $\Pr(B\mid Y=y)f_Y(y)$.

---

## Step 3: Hand sketch / 第三步：示意

Disease prior $\Pr(D)=0.01$, $\Pr(+\mid D)=0.95$, $\Pr(+\mid D^{c})=0.10$.  
$\Pr(+)=0.95\cdot 0.01+0.10\cdot 0.99=0.1085$.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

- Weights that do not sum to 1 (not a real partition).  
- Using $\Pr(A_i\mid B)$ where the formula needs $\Pr(B\mid A_i)$.

---

## Practice / 练习

Two factories: 60% of items from F1, 40% from F2. Defect rates 2% and 5%. Probability a random item is defective?

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

$0.02\cdot 0.6+0.05\cdot 0.4=0.032$.

</details>
