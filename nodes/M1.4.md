# M1.4 Law of Total Probability / 全概率公式

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M1.3 Conditional Probability](M1.3.md)

**Source anchors**: SDST2601A Ch3 Theorems 3.2–3.3; two-factory defect; disease screening marginal; continuous version with density.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

$\Pr(B)$ may be hard to see directly, but if a **partition** $A_1,\ldots,A_n$ of the sample space makes each piece $\Pr(B\mid A_i)$ easy, total probability rebuilds $\Pr(B)$ by weighting those pieces with the prior weights $\Pr(A_i)$.

$\Pr(B)$ 可能不好直接看；若有样本空间的**划分** $A_1,\ldots,A_n$ 使每块 $\Pr(B\mid A_i)$ 容易，全概率用先验权重 $\Pr(A_i)$ 把这些块拼回 $\Pr(B)$。

---

## Step 1: What is a partition? / 第一步：什么是划分？

Events $A_1,\ldots,A_n$ form a **partition** of $\Omega$ when:

1. They are pairwise disjoint: $A_i\cap A_j=\emptyset$ for $i\neq j$.  
2. They cover everything: $\bigcup_i A_i=\Omega$.

Examples: $\{D,D^{c}\}$; “item from factory 1 / factory 2 / factory 3”; “$Y=0,1,2,\ldots$” for a discrete random variable.

两两不交且并集为全集。

---

## Step 2: The formula / 第二步：公式

$$\Pr(B)=\sum_{i=1}^{n}\Pr(B\mid A_i)\Pr(A_i).$$

**Derivation.** $B=\bigcup_i (B\cap A_i)$ and the pieces $B\cap A_i$ are disjoint, so

$$\Pr(B)=\sum_i\Pr(B\cap A_i)=\sum_i\Pr(B\mid A_i)\Pr(A_i)$$

by the multiplication rule.

Continuous analogue: $\Pr(B)=\int \Pr(B\mid Y=y)f_Y(y)\,dy$.

由 $B$ 分解为不交并 $B\cap A_i$，再对每项用乘法公式。

---

## Step 3: Hand examples / 第三步：手算

**Two factories.** 60% of items from F1 (defect rate 2%), 40% from F2 (defect rate 5%).  
$\Pr(\text{defective})=0.02\cdot 0.6+0.05\cdot 0.4=0.032$.

**Disease screening (marginal of the data).**  
$\Pr(D)=0.01$, $\Pr(+\mid D)=0.95$, $\Pr(+\mid D^{c})=0.10$.  
$\Pr(+)=0.95\cdot 0.01+0.10\cdot 0.99=0.1085$.  
This $\Pr(+)$ is exactly the denominator of Bayes’ theorem ([M1.5](M1.5.md)).

---

## Step 4: Why partitions matter / 第四步：划分为何重要

If the $A_i$ overlap or miss part of $\Omega$, the sum either double-counts or omits probability mass. Always check:

- disjoint?  
- union = $\Omega$?  
- weights $\Pr(A_i)$ sum to 1?

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Total probability = weighted average of conditional probabilities, weights = prior slice probabilities.  
2. It is the denominator of Bayes.  
3. Partition quality determines whether the formula is usable (easy conditionals).  
4. Continuous version replaces sum by integral against the density of the conditioning variable.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Weights that do not sum to 1 | Not a real partition |
| Using $\Pr(A_i\mid B)$ where formula needs $\Pr(B\mid A_i)$ | Direction of conditioning |
| Overlapping “cases” | Enforce pairwise disjointness |
| Forgetting a slice of the partition | Union must be all of $\Omega$ |

---

## Worked exam-style example / 考试风格例题

**Problem.** Three machines produce 20%, 30%, 50% of items, with defect rates 1%, 2%, 3%. Probability a random item is defective?

**Solution.** $0.01\cdot 0.2+0.02\cdot 0.3+0.03\cdot 0.5=0.023$.

## Practice / 练习

1. Two factories: 60% F1, 40% F2; defect 2% and 5%. $\Pr(\text{defective})$?

2. Why is $\{D,D^{c}\}$ always a partition?

3. Write the continuous total-probability formula for $\Pr(B)$ when conditioning on a continuous $Y$.

<details>
<summary>Solution sketches / 解答提示</summary>

1. $0.032$.

2. Disjoint by construction, union is $\Omega$.

3. $\Pr(B)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\Pr(B\mid Y=y)f_Y(y)\,dy$.
