# M1.4 Law of Total Probability / 全概率公式

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M1.3 Conditional Probability and Multiplication Rule](M1.3.md)

---

## Content / 内容

If the events A_i form a partition of the sample space (they are pairwise disjoint and their union is Ω), then for any event B
P(B) = Σ_i P(B|A_i) P(A_i).

若事件 A_i 构成样本空间的一个分割（两两互斥且并集为 Ω），则对任意事件 B
P(B) = Σ_i P(B|A_i) P(A_i)。

The practical recipe is: first partition according to the “cause” or “type”, then compute the conditional probability of the observed event inside each cell, and finally weight by the prior probabilities of the cells. Diagnostic testing is the classic illustration: partition into diseased / not diseased.

实用步骤：先按“原因”或“类别”分割，再在每一类内计算观测事件的条件概率，最后用各类先验加权。诊断测试是经典例子：按患病/未患病分割。

The law of total probability supplies the denominator of Bayes’ formula. Always verify that the partition is exhaustive and mutually exclusive.

全概率公式是贝叶斯公式的分母。始终检查分割是否互斥且穷尽。

## Example / 例子

Two factories produce the same item. Factory 1 makes 60 % of the items and has defect rate 2 %; factory 2 makes 40 % and has defect rate 5 %. Overall defect probability = 0.6·0.02 + 0.4·0.05 = 0.032.

两家工厂生产同一产品。工厂 1 占 60 %、次品率 2 %；工厂 2 占 40 %、次品率 5 %。总体次品率 = 0.6·0.02 + 0.4·0.05 = 0.032。

## Practice Problem / 练习题

A test has sensitivity 0.95 and specificity 0.90. Disease prevalence is 0.01. What is the probability of a positive test?

某检测敏感度 0.95、特异度 0.90。疾病患病率 0.01。求检测阳性的概率。

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

P(+) = P(+|D)P(D) + P(+|D^c)P(D^c) = 0.95·0.01 + 0.10·0.99 = 0.1085.

P(+) = P(+|D)P(D) + P(+|D^c)P(D^c) = 0.95·0.01 + 0.10·0.99 = 0.1085。

</details>
