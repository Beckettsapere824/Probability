# M1.4 Law of Total Probability / 全概率公式

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M1.3 Conditional Probability](M1.3.md)

**Source anchors**: SDST2601A Ch3 Theorems 3.2–3.3; two-factory defect; disease screening marginal.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

$\Pr(B)$ may be hard to see directly, but if a **partition** $A_1,\ldots,A_n$ of the sample space makes each piece $\Pr(B\mid A_i)$ easy, total probability rebuilds $\Pr(B)$ by weighting those pieces with the prior weights $\Pr(A_i)$.

$\Pr(B)$ 可能不好直接看；若有样本空间的**划分** $A_1,\ldots,A_n$ 使每块 $\Pr(B\mid A_i)$ 容易，全概率用先验权重 $\Pr(A_i)$ 把这些块拼回 $\Pr(B)$。

---

## Step 1: What is a partition? / 第一步：什么是划分？

Events $A_1,\ldots,A_n$ form a **partition** of $\Omega$ when:

1. Pairwise disjoint: $A_i\cap A_j=\emptyset$ for $i\neq j$.  
2. Cover everything: $\bigcup_i A_i=\Omega$.

Examples: $\{D,D^{c}\}$; “item from factory 1 / 2 / 3”.

两两不交且并集为全集。

---

## Step 2: The formula / 第二步：公式

$$\Pr(B)=\sum_{i=1}^{n}\Pr(B\mid A_i)\Pr(A_i).$$

**Derivation.** $B=\bigcup_i (B\cap A_i)$ disjoint, so $\Pr(B)=\sum_i\Pr(B\cap A_i)=\sum_i\Pr(B\mid A_i)\Pr(A_i)$.

Continuous analogue: $\Pr(B)=\int \Pr(B\mid Y=y)f_Y(y)\,dy$.

---

## Step 3: Hand examples / 第三步：手算

**Two factories.** 60% from F1 (defect 2%), 40% from F2 (defect 5%).  
$\Pr(\text{defective})=0.02\cdot 0.6+0.05\cdot 0.4=0.032$.

**Disease screening.** $\Pr(D)=0.01$, $\Pr(+\mid D)=0.95$, $\Pr(+\mid D^{c})=0.10$.  
$\Pr(+)=0.95\cdot 0.01+0.10\cdot 0.99=0.1085$ (Bayes denominator).

---

## Step 4: Why partitions matter / 第四步：划分为何重要

If the $A_i$ overlap or miss part of $\Omega$, the sum double-counts or omits mass. Check: disjoint? union $=\Omega$? weights sum to 1?

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Total probability = weighted average of conditionals, weights = prior slice probabilities.  
2. It is the denominator of Bayes.  
3. Partition quality determines usability (easy conditionals).  
4. Continuous version replaces sum by integral against the density.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Weights that do not sum to 1 | Not a real partition |
| Using $\Pr(A_i\mid B)$ where formula needs $\Pr(B\mid A_i)$ | Direction of conditioning |
| Overlapping “cases” | Enforce pairwise disjointness |
| Forgetting a slice | Union must be all of $\Omega$ |
