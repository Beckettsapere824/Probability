# M2.1 Ordered/Unordered, With/Without Replacement / 有序/无序、有放回/无放回

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.3 Factorials, Permutations, Combinations](M0.3.md), [M1.9 Classical Probability](M1.9.md)

---

## Content / 内容

Four basic sampling models:
- Ordered with replacement: n^k
- Ordered without replacement: P(n,k) = n!/(n-k)!
- Unordered without replacement: C(n,k)
- Unordered with replacement: C(n+k-1,k)

四种基本抽样模型：
- 有序有放回：n^k
- 有序无放回：P(n,k) = n!/(n-k)!
- 无序无放回：C(n,k)
- 无序有放回：C(n+k-1,k)

First decide which of the four cells applies, then count. Whether sampling is with or without replacement changes both the denominator and all subsequent conditional probabilities.

先判断属于四格中的哪一格，再计数。抽样是否放回同时影响分母和后续条件概率。

## Example / 例子

From 10 distinct books choose 3. Ordered without replacement (ranked list): P(10,3). Unordered without replacement (set): C(10,3). If books may be repeated and order matters: 10^3.

从 10 本不同书中选 3 本。有序无放回（有序名单）：P(10,3)。无序无放回（集合）：C(10,3)。允许重复且顺序有关：10^3。

## Practice Problem / 练习题

An urn has 5 red and 7 blue balls. Three balls are drawn. Give the size of the sample space under each of the four models (treat colors as distinguishable only by color, or assume all balls distinguishable—state your assumption).

瓮中有 5 红 7 蓝。抽 3 球。给出四种模型下样本空间的大小（说明你对球是否可区分的假设）。

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

If all 12 balls are distinguishable: ordered with replacement 12^3, ordered without P(12,3), unordered without C(12,3), unordered with C(12+3-1,3). If only color matters the counts become multinomial or stars-and-bars variants.

若 12 球可区分：有序有放回 12^3，有序无放回 P(12,3)，无序无放回 C(12,3)，无序有放回 C(12+3-1,3)。若只按颜色则变为多项式或 stars-and-bars 变体。

</details>
