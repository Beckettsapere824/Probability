# M2.1 Four Sampling Models / 四种抽样模型

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.3](M0.3.md)

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Count samples by: ordered or not × with or without replacement.

有序/无序 × 有放回/无放回。

---

## Table / 表

| | Ordered | Unordered |
|--|---------|-------------|
| With replacement | $n^{k}$ | $\binom{n+k-1}{k}$ (stars and bars) |
| Without | $P(n,k)$ | $\binom{n}{k}$ |

---

## Practice / 练习

5 distinct books, pick 2 unordered without replacement?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>$\binom{5}{2}=10$.</details>
