# M2.1 Four Sampling Models / 四种抽样模型

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M0.3](M0.3.md), [M0.2](M0.2.md)

**Source anchors**: ordered vs unordered × with vs without replacement; passwords, hands, multisets.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Almost every counting problem is one of four models: whether order matters, and whether replacement is allowed. Choosing the wrong cell of the table is the most common exam error.

几乎每个计数题都属于四种模型之一：顺序是否有关、是否有放回。选错格子是最常见的考试错误。

---

## Step 1: The four cells / 第一步：四格表

| | **Ordered** | **Unordered** |
|--|-------------|---------------|
| **With replacement** | $n^{k}$ | $\binom{n+k-1}{k}$ (stars and bars) |
| **Without replacement** | $P(n,k)=n!/(n-k)!$ | $\binom{n}{k}$ |

---

## Step 2: How to choose / 第二步：如何选择

- **Order matters** if sequences, rankings, passwords, or “first then second” are distinguished.  
- **Replacement** if the same item can appear more than once (die rolls, sampling with put-back).  
- Hands of cards, committees: unordered without replacement → $\binom{n}{k}$.  
- Multiset of size $k$ from $n$ types: unordered with replacement → stars and bars.

---

## Step 3: Hand checks / 第三步：手算

5 distinct books, pick 2 unordered without replacement: $\binom{5}{2}=10$.

Die rolled 3 times (ordered, with replacement): $6^{3}=216$.

Password length 4 from 26 letters, repetition allowed: $26^{4}$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Always decide order and replacement **before** writing a formula.  
2. $n^{k}$ and $P(n,k)$ count sequences; binomials count sets.  
3. Stars and bars is the multiset count, not “combinations with repetition” as a vague phrase.  
4. Numerator and denominator must use the same model if you form a classical probability.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Using $\binom{n}{k}$ for ordered passwords | Use $n^{k}$ or $P(n,k)$ |
| Mixing $P$ in denominator with $C$ in numerator | Same model both sides |
| Stars and bars when order matters | Wrong cell |
| Forgetting “without replacement” for hands | Cards are not replaced |

---

## Practice / 练习（选择题）

**1.** 5 distinct books, pick 2 unordered without replacement. Number of outcomes:

A. $5^{2}$  
B. $P(5,2)$  
C. $\binom{5}{2}=10$  
D. $\binom{6}{2}$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Unordered, no replacement → $\binom{5}{2}$.  
Answer: **C**.
</details>

**2.** Die rolled 3 times. Number of outcome sequences:

A. $\binom{6}{3}$  
B. $6^{3}=216$  
C. $P(6,3)$  
D. $3^{6}$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Ordered with replacement → $n^{k}$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**3.** $P(n,k)$ equals

A. $n^{k}$  
B. $n!/(n-k)!$  
C. $\binom{n}{k}$  
D. $k^{n}$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Permutations without replacement.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**4.** Unordered samples with replacement from $n$ types, size $k$:

A. $n^{k}$  
B. $\binom{n}{k}$  
C. $\binom{n+k-1}{k}$  
D. $P(n,k)$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Stars and bars.  
Answer: **C**.
</details>

**5.** Password of length 4 from 26 letters, repetition allowed:

A. $\binom{26}{4}$  
B. $P(26,4)$  
C. $26^{4}$  
D. $4^{26}$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Ordered with replacement.  
Answer: **C**.
</details>

**6.** A 5-card poker hand from 52 is counted by

A. $52^{5}$  
B. $P(52,5)$  
C. $\binom{52}{5}$  
D. $\binom{52+5-1}{5}$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Unordered without replacement.  
Answer: **C**.
</details>

**7.** First decide in a counting problem:

A. the numerical answer  
B. whether order and replacement matter  
C. Bayes formula  
D. the mean

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Model selection comes first.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**8.** Mixing $P(n,k)$ in the denominator with $\binom{n}{k}$ in the numerator is

A. always fine  
B. a consistency error for classical probability  
C. required for cards  
D. stars and bars

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Same counting model on both sides.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>
