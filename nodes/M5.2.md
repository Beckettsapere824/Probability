# M5.2 Sensitivity, Specificity, Bayes / 敏感度、特异度与贝叶斯

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M1.5](M1.5.md)

**Source anchors**: medical diagnosis; PPV vs sensitivity; prevalence.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

A diagnostic test is characterised by **sensitivity** $\Pr(+\mid D)$ and **specificity** $\Pr(-\mid D^{c})$. The clinically useful quantity $\Pr(D\mid +)$ (PPV) still needs the prevalence $\Pr(D)$.

诊断试验用敏感度、特异度描述；阳性预测值还依赖患病率。

---

## Step 1: Four rates / 第一步：四个率

- Sensitivity: $\Pr(+\mid D)$  
- Specificity: $\Pr(-\mid D^{c})$  
- False positive rate: $\Pr(+\mid D^{c})=1-\text{specificity}$  
- False negative rate: $\Pr(-\mid D)=1-\text{sensitivity}$

---

## Step 2: Bayes / 第二步：贝叶斯

$$\Pr(D\mid +)=\frac{\Pr(+\mid D)\Pr(D)}{\Pr(+)},\quad \Pr(+)=\Pr(+\mid D)\Pr(D)+\Pr(+\mid D^{c})\Pr(D^{c}).$$

---

## Step 3: Hand numbers / 第三步：数字

$\Pr(D)=0.01$, sens $=0.95$, FPR $=0.10$ ⇒ $\Pr(+)=0.1085$, $\Pr(D\mid +)\approx0.0876$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Sensitivity ≠ positive predictive value.  
2. Rare disease ⇒ most positives can be false.  
3. Specificity high helps when prevalence is low.  
4. Same arithmetic as [M1.5](M1.5.md) with medical names.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Reporting sensitivity when asked PPV | Conditioning direction |
| Ignoring prevalence | Base rate |
| Mixing FPR with FNR | Label carefully |
