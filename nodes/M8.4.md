# M8.4 Exponential Distribution and Memorylessness / 指数分布与无记忆性

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M8.1 Continuous RV and pdf](M8.1.md)

**Source / 来源**: SDST2601A Chapter 5 §5.2.2 Definition 5.4; memoryless remarks; Examples 5.5–5.6

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Waiting times—bus arrival, machine failure, time until the next Poisson event—often have a stable **rate**. A natural model puts more density near zero and a long right tail.

等待时间——班车、机器故障、下一次泊松事件——常常有一个稳定的**到达率**。自然的模型在 0 附近密度较大，右边拖长尾。

A second, sharper question appears in the notes: if the machine has already run for a year, does that change how much longer it will last? For one special continuous law the answer is **no**.

课件里还有更锋利的一问：机器已经跑了一年，还会改变「还能再跑多久」的分布吗？对某一种连续律，答案是**不会**。

---

## Step 1: Rate parametrization (Def 5.4) / 第一步：速率参数化

$$
f(x) = \begin{cases} \lambda e^{-\lambda x} & x>0, \\ 0 & x\le 0, \end{cases}
\qquad \lambda>0.
$$

Write $X\sim\mathrm{Exp}(\lambda)$ with **rate** $\lambda$. Then

$$
F(x)=1-e^{-\lambda x}\ (x>0), \quad
E(X)=\frac{1}{\lambda}, \quad
\mathrm{Var}(X)=\frac{1}{\lambda^{2}}.
$$

Large $\lambda$ means events come quickly: mean wait $1/\lambda$ is small.

$\lambda$ 大表示来得快：平均等待 $1/\lambda$ 小。

---

## Step 2: Memorylessness / 第二步：无记忆性

For all $a>0$, $b>0$:

$$
\Pr(X>a+b\mid X>a)=\Pr(X>b).
$$

Derivation sketch: $\Pr(X>a+b)=e^{-\lambda(a+b)}=e^{-\lambda a}e^{-\lambda b}=\Pr(X>a)\Pr(X>b)$, so the conditional residual equals a fresh copy of $X$.

推导要点：尾概率乘积分解，条件残寿与从零开始同分布。

Among continuous distributions supported on $(0,\infty)$, **only** the exponential is memoryless.

在 $(0,\infty)$ 上的连续分布里，**只有**指数具有无记忆性。

---

## Step 3: Rate vs mean parametrization / 第三步：rate 与 mean 两种记法

Some texts set mean $\beta=1/\lambda$ and write $f(x)=\frac{1}{\beta}e^{-x/\beta}$. Same family. Always check whether a problem’s parameter is a **rate** or a **mean** before plugging into $E(X)$.

有的书用均值 $\beta=1/\lambda$。同一族。代入期望前先分清参数是**速率**还是**均值**。

---

## Step 4: Hand example (residual life) / 第四步：残寿手算

If lifetime is exponential, $\Pr(X>16\mid X>12)=\Pr(X>4)$ by memorylessness—past runtime drops out of the residual calculation (numerical rate as in the notes’ machine example).

寿命为指数时，已运行时间在残寿计算中被消掉。

---

## Summary / 小结

- Exp = continuous waiting time with constant rate.  
- Memoryless: the future does not care how long you already waited.  
- Name your parametrization (rate vs mean) every time.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

- Mixing $\lambda$ (rate) with $\beta=1/\lambda$ (mean).  
- Applying memorylessness to non-exponential lifetimes.  
- Writing $\Pr(X=x)$ for a continuous lifetime.

---

## Practice / 练习

$X\sim\mathrm{Exp}(\lambda=2)$ (rate). Find $E(X)$, $\Pr(X>1)$, and $\Pr(X>3\mid X>1)$.

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

$E(X)=1/2$.  
$\Pr(X>1)=e^{-2}$.  
Memoryless: $\Pr(X>3\mid X>1)=\Pr(X>2)=e^{-4}$.

</details>
