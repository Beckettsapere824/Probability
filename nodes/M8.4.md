# M8.4 Exponential Distribution and Memorylessness / 指数分布与无记忆性

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M8.1 Continuous RV and pdf](M8.1.md), [M4.4 Geometric memorylessness](M4.4.md)

**Source anchors**: SDST2601A Ch5 Def 5.4; memoryless remarks; residual-life examples; hazard link in Ch later; exam median, $\Pr(X>2m)$, $E(X\mid X<1)$, rate vs mean.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Waiting times with a stable rate — time to failure of a component, time until the next call, lifetime of a radioactive atom — often look “memoryless”: the component that has already run for a year is not more likely to die in the next month than a brand-new one of the same type. The **exponential distribution** is the unique continuous law on $(0,\infty)$ with that property, and it is the continuous twin of the geometric.

具有稳定速率的等待时间——元件寿命、下一次来电、放射性原子——常常表现为「无记忆」：已经运行一年的元件，在下个月坏掉的机会并不比同款新元件更高。**指数分布**是 $(0,\infty)$ 上唯一具有该性质的连续律，也是几何分布的连续对应。

---

## Step 1: Rate parametrization / 第一步：速率参数化

$$f(x)=\lambda e^{-\lambda x}\quad(x>0),\qquad \lambda>0.$$

Write $X\sim\mathrm{Exp}(\lambda)$ with **rate** $\lambda$. Then

$$F(x)=1-e^{-\lambda x}\ (x>0),\qquad S(x)=\Pr(X>x)=e^{-\lambda x},$$

$$E(X)=\frac{1}{\lambda},\qquad \mathrm{Var}(X)=\frac{1}{\lambda^{2}}.$$

Large $\lambda$ → events come quickly → small mean wait.

**Mean parametrization (other books):** some texts set $\beta=1/\lambda$ (mean) and write $f(x)=\frac{1}{\beta}e^{-x/\beta}$. Same family. **Always check whether the problem’s parameter is a rate or a mean** before plugging into $E(X)$.

有的书用均值 $\beta=1/\lambda$。同一族。代入前先分清参数是**速率**还是**均值**。

---

## Step 2: Derive mean and variance quickly / 第二步：快速求均值方差

Integration by parts (or Gamma function):

$$E(X)=\int_{0}^{\infty}x\lambda e^{-\lambda x}\,dx=\frac{1}{\lambda}.$$

$$E(X^{2})=\int_{0}^{\infty}x^{2}\lambda e^{-\lambda x}\,dx=\frac{2}{\lambda^{2}}\implies\mathrm{Var}(X)=\frac{2}{\lambda^{2}}-\frac{1}{\lambda^{2}}=\frac{1}{\lambda^{2}}.$$

---

## Step 3: Memorylessness — statement and proof / 第三步：无记忆性——陈述与证明

**Statement.** For all $a>0$, $b>0$,

$$\Pr(X>a+b\mid X>a)=\Pr(X>b).$$

**Proof.** Survival multiplies:

$$\Pr(X>a+b)=e^{-\lambda(a+b)}=e^{-\lambda a}e^{-\lambda b}=\Pr(X>a)\Pr(X>b).$$

Therefore

$$\Pr(X>a+b\mid X>a)=\frac{\Pr(X>a+b)}{\Pr(X>a)}=\Pr(X>b).$$

The residual lifetime $X-a$ given $\{X>a\}$ is again $\mathrm{Exp}(\lambda)$, independent of $a$. Past runtime drops out.

**Uniqueness (fact to remember):** among continuous distributions supported on $(0,\infty)$, only the exponential is memoryless. (Among discrete distributions on $\{1,2,\ldots\}$, only the geometric.)

**陈述.** $\Pr(X>a+b\mid X>a)=\Pr(X>b)$。  
**证明.** 生存函数乘积分解。  
**唯一性：** 连续 $(0,\infty)$ 上只有指数无记忆；离散上只有几何。

---

## Step 4: Median and percentiles / 第四步：中位数与分位数

Median $m$ solves $F(m)=1/2$:

$$1-e^{-\lambda m}=1/2\implies e^{-\lambda m}=1/2\implies m=\frac{\ln 2}{\lambda}.$$

So $\Pr(X>2m)=e^{-\lambda\cdot 2\ln 2/\lambda}=e^{-2\ln 2}=1/4$.  
(Past-paper style: “median $m$, find $\Pr(X>2m)$” → answer $0.25$ without knowing $\lambda$.)

中位数 $m=(\ln 2)/\lambda$。$\Pr(X>2m)=1/4$，不需要知道 $\lambda$ 的具体值。

---

## Step 5: Conditional expectations that exams like / 第五步：考试爱考的条件期望

**Residual after survival:** $E(X\mid X>a)=a+E(X)=a+1/\lambda$ by memorylessness.

**Truncation from above:** $E(X\mid X<c)$ is **not** memoryless and must be computed from the truncated density:

$$f(x\mid X<c)=\frac{\lambda e^{-\lambda x}}{1-e^{-\lambda c}}\quad(0<x<c),$$

then integrate $x f(x\mid X<c)$. Numerical values (e.g. second moment given, find $E(X\mid X<1)$) appear in past papers — set up the integral carefully.

截断上侧 $E(X\mid X<c)$ **不能**用无记忆性，要从截断密度积分。

---

## Step 6: Link to Poisson process and geometric / 第六步：与泊松过程、几何的联系

- In a rate-$\lambda$ Poisson process, waiting time to first event $\sim\mathrm{Exp}(\lambda)$; number of events in time 1 $\sim\mathrm{Po}(\lambda)$ ([M4.6](M4.6.md)).  
- Discrete analogue: geometric memorylessness ([M4.4](M4.4.md)). Bernoulli trials every $\Delta t$ with $p=\lambda\Delta t$ produce exponential waits in the continuous limit.  
- Min of independent $\mathrm{Exp}(\lambda_{i})$ is $\mathrm{Exp}(\sum\lambda_{i})$ — same product-of-survivals idea as min of geometrics.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Exp = continuous constant-rate wait; only continuous memoryless law on $(0,\infty)$.  
2. Rate $\lambda$ vs mean $\beta=1/\lambda$ — name which one you are using.  
3. Residual life ignores the past; truncated life $E(X\mid X<c)$ does **not**.  
4. Median is $(\ln 2)/\lambda$; $\Pr(X>2m)=1/4$ is a pure number.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Treating the parameter as mean when the problem says rate | $E(X)=1/\lambda$ for rate $\lambda$ |
| Applying memorylessness to $E(X\mid X<c)$ | Only residual life $X-a\mid X>a$ is memoryless |
| Writing $\Pr(X=x)$ for continuous $X$ | Density, not mass |
| Claiming Weibull/Gamma are memoryless | Only Exp among the usual continuous families |

## Worked exam-style example / 考试风格例题

**Problem.** $X\sim\mathrm{Exp}(\lambda)$ with rate $\lambda$, median $m$.

(a) Express $m$ in terms of $\lambda$.  
(b) $\Pr(X>2m)$?  
(c) $E(X\mid X>m)$?

**Solution.**

(a) $m=(\ln 2)/\lambda$.  
(b) $e^{-\lambda\cdot 2m}=e^{-2\ln 2}=1/4$.  
(c) By memorylessness, $E(X\mid X>m)=m+1/\lambda=(\ln 2)/\lambda+1/\lambda=(1+\ln 2)/\lambda$.

**Another past-paper flavour.** Second moment $E(X^{2})=2$. Then $2/\lambda^{2}=2\implies\lambda=1$ (rate). $E(X\mid X<1)$ requires the truncated integral with this $\lambda$.

## Practice / 练习

1. $X\sim\mathrm{Exp}(\lambda=2)$ (rate). Find $E(X)$, $\Pr(X>1)$, $\Pr(X>3\mid X>1)$.

2. Independent $X\sim\mathrm{Exp}(\lambda)$, $Y\sim\mathrm{Exp}(\mu)$. Law of $\min(X,Y)$?

3. Median of $X$ is 4. Find $\Pr(X>8)$ without finding $\lambda$ first.

<details>
<summary>Solution sketches / 解答提示</summary>

1. $1/2$; $e^{-2}$; memoryless $\Pr(X>2)=e^{-4}$.

2. $\Pr(\min>t)=e^{-\lambda t}e^{-\mu t}=e^{-(\lambda+\mu)t}$, so $\mathrm{Exp}(\lambda+\mu)$.

3. $8=2\times$ median $\implies\Pr(X>8)=1/4$.

</details>
