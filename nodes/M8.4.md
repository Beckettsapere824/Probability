# M8.4 Exponential Distribution and Memorylessness / 指数分布与无记忆性

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M8.1 Continuous RV and pdf](M8.1.md)

**Source / 来源**: SDST2601A Chapter 5 §5.2.2 Definition 5.4; memoryless remarks; Examples 5.5–5.6

---

## Content / 内容

### Definition 5.4 (rate parametrization)

Positive continuous \(X\) with pdf

\[
f(x) = \begin{cases} \lambda e^{-\lambda x} & x > 0, \\ 0 & x \le 0, \end{cases}
\qquad \lambda > 0.
\]

Write \(X \sim \mathrm{Exp}(\lambda)\) (rate \(\lambda\)).

### CDF, mean, variance, MGF

\[
F(x) = 1 - e^{-\lambda x}\ (x>0), \quad
E(X)=\frac{1}{\lambda}, \quad
\mathrm{Var}(X)=\frac{1}{\lambda^2}, \quad
M_X(t)=\frac{\lambda}{\lambda-t}\ (t<\lambda).
\]

### Memoryless property

For all \(a>0,\ b>0\):

\[
\Pr(X > a+b \mid X > a) = \Pr(X > b).
\]

Among continuous distributions on \((0,\infty)\), **only** the exponential is memoryless. Knowing the process has not failed for time \(a\) does not change the law of residual life.

### Scale parametrization (remark in notes)

Some texts use mean \(\beta=1/\lambda\):

\[
f(x)=\frac{1}{\beta}e^{-x/\beta}\ (x>0).
\]
Same family; identify whether a problem uses **rate** or **mean**.

## Example / 例子

**Example 5.5–5.6 (machine lifetime).**  
If lifetime is exponential with rate such that residual calculations give
\(\Pr(X>16\mid X>12)=\Pr(X>4)=e^{-8}\approx 0.000335\) under the notes’ numerical rate — the structural point is memorylessness: condition on survival to 12 months replaces the problem by an unconditional tail of length 4.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

- Confusing rate \(\lambda\) with mean \(\beta=1/\lambda\).
- Applying memorylessness to **non-exponential** lifetimes.
- Using \(\Pr(X=x)\) for continuous lifetime (always 0).

## Practice Problem / 练习题

\(X \sim \mathrm{Exp}(\lambda=2)\) (rate). Find \(E(X)\), \(\Pr(X>1)\), and \(\Pr(X>3\mid X>1)\).

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

\(E(X)=1/2\).  
\(\Pr(X>1)=e^{-2}\).  
By memorylessness \(\Pr(X>3\mid X>1)=\Pr(X>2)=e^{-4}\).

</details>
