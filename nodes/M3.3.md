# M3.3 Variance and Standard Deviation / 方差与标准差

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M3.1 Expectation](M3.1.md), [M3.2 LOTUS](M3.2.md)

**Source anchors**: SDST2601A Var definition and computational form; Bernoulli / Binomial / Poisson fingerprints; scaling; Cov link.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Expectation gives a centre. Two distributions can share the same mean and still feel completely different: one is tightly concentrated, the other spreads over a wide range. **Variance** measures that spread as the expected squared deviation from the mean; **standard deviation** is its square root, restoring the same physical units as $X$.

期望给出中心。两个分布可以同均值但完全不同：一个高度集中，一个铺得很开。**方差**用相对均值的平方偏差的期望来量分散；**标准差**开方后与 $X$ 同单位。

---

## Step 1: Definition and computational form / 第一步：定义与计算形式

Let $\mu=E(X)$. Define

$$\mathrm{Var}(X)=E\bigl[(X-\mu)^{2}\bigr].$$

Expand with linearity (LOTUS for $X^{2}$):

$$\mathrm{Var}(X)=E(X^{2}-2\mu X+\mu^{2})=E(X^{2})-2\mu E(X)+\mu^{2}=E(X^{2})-\mu^{2}.$$

This **computational form** $E(X^{2})-\mu^{2}$ is what you use in almost every hand calculation. Standard deviation $\sigma_X=\sqrt{\mathrm{Var}(X)}$.

展开得计算形式 $E(X^{2})-\mu^{2}$，手算几乎总用这个。标准差 $\sigma=\sqrt{\mathrm{Var}}$。

---

## Step 2: Scaling and shift / 第二步：尺度与平移

$$\mathrm{Var}(aX+b)=a^{2}\mathrm{Var}(X).$$

- Adding $b$ does not change spread.  
- Multiplying by $a$ multiplies variance by $a^{2}$ (and SD by $|a|$).

Proof: $E(aX+b)=a\mu+b$, so deviations scale by $a$, squares by $a^{2}$.

平移不改变分散；乘 $a$ 使方差乘 $a^{2}$。

---

## Step 3: Fingerprints of named families / 第三步：名族指纹

| Law | $\mathrm{Var}$ |
|-----|----------------|
| Bernoulli($p$) | $p(1-p)$ |
| Binomial($n,p$) | $np(1-p)$ |
| Geometric($p$) (trials until first success) | $(1-p)/p^{2}$ |
| Poisson($\lambda$) | $\lambda$ (equal to mean) |
| Uniform$\{1,\ldots,n\}$ | $(n^{2}-1)/12$ |

Equal mean and variance is a quick **Poisson diagnostic**. If sample variance is many times the sample mean, Poisson is a poor fit.

均值=方差是泊松的快速识别特征。

---

## Step 4: Independent sum / 第四步：独立和

If $X,Y$ independent,

$$\mathrm{Var}(X+Y)=\mathrm{Var}(X)+\mathrm{Var}(Y).$$

(Without independence one needs $\mathrm{Var}(X)+\mathrm{Var}(Y)+2\mathrm{Cov}(X,Y)$ — see covariance node.)

This is why binomial variance is $np(1-p)$: sum of $n$ independent Bernoulli variances.

独立则方差可加；否则要加 $2\mathrm{Cov}$。二项方差由此从 Bernoulli 相加得到。

---

## Step 5: Hand example / 第五步：手算

$X$ takes $1,2,3$ each with probability $1/3$.  
$\mu=2$, $E(X^{2})=(1+4+9)/3=14/3$, $\mathrm{Var}(X)=14/3-4=2/3$, $\sigma=\sqrt{2/3}$.

Bernoulli($p$): $E(X^{2})=E(X)=p$ (since $X^{2}=X$), so $\mathrm{Var}=p-p^{2}=p(1-p)$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Variance is expected **squared** deviation — always non-negative; zero iff $X$ is a.s. constant.  
2. Use $E(X^{2})-\mu^{2}$ for calculation; definition for interpretation.  
3. $\mathrm{Var}(aX+b)=a^{2}\mathrm{Var}(X)$ — shifts free, scales quadratic.  
4. Independence ⇒ variances of sums add; dependence requires covariance.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| $E(X^{2})-\mu$ instead of $E(X^{2})-\mu^{2}$ | Square the mean |
| $\mathrm{Var}(aX)=a\,\mathrm{Var}(X)$ | Must be $a^{2}$ |
| Confusing $\sigma$ with $\sigma^{2}$ in $N(\mu,\sigma^{2})$ | Notation is variance inside |
| Adding variances for dependent variables without Cov | Include $2\mathrm{Cov}$ |

---

## Worked exam-style example / 考试风格例题

**Problem.** $E(X)=2$, $E(X^{2})=7$. Find $\mathrm{Var}(X)$ and $\mathrm{Var}(3X-5)$.

**Solution.** $\mathrm{Var}(X)=7-4=3$. $\mathrm{Var}(3X-5)=9\cdot 3=27$.

## Practice / 练习

1. $X$ takes $1,2,3$ with equal probability. $\mathrm{Var}(X)$?

2. Why is $\mathrm{Var}(X)\ge 0$ always?

3. $X,Y$ i.i.d. Bernoulli($1/2$). $\mathrm{Var}(X+Y)$?

<details>
<summary>Solution sketches / 解答提示</summary>

1. $2/3$.

2. It is $E[(X-\mu)^{2}]$ of a non-negative random variable.

3. $1/4+1/4=1/2$ (or $\mathrm{Bin}(2,1/2)$ variance $2\cdot 1/2\cdot 1/2=1/2$).

</details>
