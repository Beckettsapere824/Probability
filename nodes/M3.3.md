# M3.3 Variance and Standard Deviation / 方差与标准差

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M3.1 Expectation](M3.1.md), [M3.2 LOTUS](M3.2.md)

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Expectation locates the centre. We also need a scale: how spread out is $X$ around $E(X)$? Variance is the expected squared deviation; standard deviation is its square root (same units as $X$).

期望定位中心。还需要尺度：$X$ 围绕 $E(X)$ 有多散？方差是平方偏差的期望；标准差是其平方根（与 $X$ 同单位）。

---

## Step 1: Definition / 第一步：定义

$$\mathrm{Var}(X)=E\bigl[(X-\mu)^{2}\bigr], \qquad \mu=E(X).$$

Computational form (expand and use linearity):

$$\mathrm{Var}(X)=E(X^{2})-\mu^{2}.$$

Standard deviation $\sigma=\sqrt{\mathrm{Var}(X)}$.

---

## Step 2: Scaling / 第二步：尺度

$$\mathrm{Var}(aX+b)=a^{2}\mathrm{Var}(X).$$

Shifts $b$ do not change spread; multipliers scale variance by $a^{2}$.

平移不改变分散度；乘子使方差乘 $a^{2}$。

---

## Step 3: Hand example / 第三步：手算

Bernoulli($p$): $E(X)=p$, $E(X^{2})=p$, $\mathrm{Var}(X)=p-p^{2}=p(1-p)$.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

- Reporting $E(X^{2})-\mu$ instead of $E(X^{2})-\mu^{2}$.  
- Using $\mathrm{Var}(aX)=a\,\mathrm{Var}(X)$ (must be $a^{2}$).  
- Confusing $\sigma$ with $\sigma^{2}$ in normal notation $N(\mu,\sigma^{2})$.

---

## Practice / 练习

$X$ takes $1,2,3$ with equal probability $1/3$. Find $\mathrm{Var}(X)$.

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

$\mu=2$, $E(X^{2})=(1+4+9)/3=14/3$, $\mathrm{Var}=14/3-4=2/3$.

</details>
