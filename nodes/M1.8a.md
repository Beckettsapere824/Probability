# M1.8a Building the CDF from a PMF / 由 PMF 构造 CDF

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M1.8](M1.8.md)

**Source anchors**: right-continuous step function; jump size $=p(x)$; flat between support points.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

A discrete PMF is a list of point masses. The CDF $F(x)=\Pr(X\le x)$ turns that list into a right-continuous step function used for interval probabilities and quantile statements.

离散 PMF 是点质量列表。CDF 把它变成右连续阶梯函数，用于区间概率与分位数。

---

## Step 1: From atoms to jumps / 第一步：原子到跳跃

Sort the support $x_1<x_2<\cdots<x_m$.  
At each $x_i$, $F$ jumps by height $p(x_i)$.  
Between $x_i$ and $x_{i+1}$, $F$ is constant.  
Left of $x_1$: $F=0$; right of $x_m$: $F=1$.

---

## Step 2: Right continuity / 第二步：右连续

$F(x)=\Pr(X\le x)$ includes the mass at $x$, so the jump is already taken when you stand at $x$. Conventionally CDF is **right-continuous**: $\lim_{y\downarrow x}F(y)=F(x)$.

---

## Step 3: Interval probabilities / 第三步：区间概率

$$
\Pr(a<X\le b)=F(b)-F(a).
$$

Care with endpoints: $\Pr(X\le a)=F(a)$, $\Pr(X<a)=F(a-)$ (limit from the left).

---

## Step 4: Hand construction / 第四步：手算

$p(0)=0.2$, $p(2)=0.8$.

- $F(x)=0$ for $x<0$  
- $F(x)=0.2$ for $0\le x<2$  
- $F(x)=1$ for $x\ge 2$

Thus $F(1)=0.2$, $F(2)=1$, $F(-1)=0$.

Merge same values: if two sample outcomes map to the same $x$, add their probabilities **before** plotting jumps.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Jump height at $x$ equals $p(x)$.  
2. CDF is flat off the support.  
3. Right-continuous: mass at $x$ is included in $F(x)$.  
4. Always merge probabilities of outcomes that share the same $X$-value.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Double-counting outcomes that map to same $x$ | Merge first |
| Drawing left-continuous steps | Use right-continuous convention |
| $\Pr(a\le X\le b)=F(b)-F(a)$ without care | Prefer $F(b)-F(a-)$ or equivalent |
| Forgetting $F\to 0$ and $F\to 1$ at $\pm\infty$ | Check limits |

## Practice / 练习（选择题）

**1.** $p(0)=0.2$, $p(2)=0.8$. $F(1)=$

A. $0$  
B. $0.2$  
C. $0.8$  
D. $1$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Only mass at $0$ is $\le 1$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**2.** Jump of $F$ at $x$ equals

A. $F(x)$  
B. $p(x)$  
C. $1-p(x)$  
D. $0$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Definition of discrete CDF.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**3.** For discrete $X$, $F$ is

A. continuous  
B. left-continuous  
C. right-continuous  
D. strictly increasing

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Standard convention.  
Answer: **C**.
</details>

**4.** $\Pr(a<X\le b)=$

A. $F(b)-F(a)$  
B. $F(a)-F(b)$  
C. $p(a)+p(b)$  
D. $F(b)+F(a)$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

$F(b)-F(a)=\Pr(X\le b)-\Pr(X\le a)=\Pr(a<X\le b)$.  
Answer: **A**.
</details>

**5.** Support $\{1,3\}$, $p(1)=p(3)=1/2$. $F(2)=$

A. $0$  
B. $1/2$  
C. $1$  
D. $2$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Only $1\le 2$.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**6.** Two outcomes both give $X=5$, with probabilities $0.1$ and $0.15$. Jump at $5$ is

A. $0.1$  
B. $0.15$  
C. $0.25$  
D. $0$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Merge: $p(5)=0.25$.  
Answer: **C**.
</details>

**7.** $\lim_{x\to-\infty}F(x)=$

A. $1$  
B. $0$  
C. $1/2$  
D. undefined

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

No mass far left.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>

**8.** $\lim_{x\to+\infty}F(x)=$

A. $0$  
B. $1$  
C. $p(\max)$  
D. $\infty$

<details>
<summary>详细过程 / Solution</summary>

Total probability.  
Answer: **B**.
</details>
