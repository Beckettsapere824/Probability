# M1.8a Building the CDF from a PMF / 由 PMF 画 CDF

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M1.8](M1.8.md)

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Turn a list of point masses into a right-continuous step function $F$.

把点质量列表变成右连续阶梯 $F$。

---

## Steps / 分步

1. Sort support $x_1<x_2<\cdots$.  
2. $F$ jumps by $p(x_i)$ at $x_i$; flat between.  
3. $F(x)=0$ left of first mass; $F=1$ right of last.

---

## Practice / 练习

$p(0)=0.2$, $p(2)=0.8$. $F(1)$?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>$0.2$.</details>
