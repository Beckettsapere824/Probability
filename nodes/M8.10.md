# M8.10 Continuous Joint and Marginal / 连续联合与边缘

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M8.1](M8.1.md), [M1.10](M1.10.md)

**Source**: SDST2601A Ch7 continuous part

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Two continuous measurements: joint density $f(x,y)$, probability = volume under surface.

---

## Steps / 分步

1. $f\ge 0$, $\iint f=1$.  
2. $\Pr((X,Y)\in A)=\iint_A f$.  
3. Marginal $f_X(x)=\int f(x,y)\,dy$.  
4. Independence: $f(x,y)=f_X(x)f_Y(y)$.

---

## Practice / 练习

If $f(x,y)=1$ on the unit square, $f_X(0.3)$?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>$1$ on $(0,1)$.</details>
