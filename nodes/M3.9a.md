# M3.9a MGF of Independent Sums / 独立和的 MGF

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M3.9](M3.9.md), [M1.10](M1.10.md)

**Source anchors**: $M_{X+Y}=M_X M_Y$ under independence; binomial from Bernoulli.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

For independent $X,Y$, $M_{X+Y}(t)=M_X(t)M_Y(t)$. Builds binomial from Bernoulli and additive independent models.

独立时和的 MGF 等于 MGF 之积。

---

## Step 1: Why product / 第一步：为何相乘

$E(e^{t(X+Y)})=E(e^{tX})E(e^{tY})$ under independence.

---

## Step 2: i.i.d. sum / 第二步：i.i.d. 和

$M_{S_n}(t)=\bigl(M_{X_1}(t)\bigr)^{n}$.

---

## Step 3: Bernoulli → binomial / 第三步：Bernoulli → 二项

$M(t)=(1-p+pe^{t})^{n}$ for $B(n,p)$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Independence is essential for the product.  
2. Same pattern for more than two summands.  
3. Recognize the product MGF to identify the sum law.  
4. Poisson, gamma, normal closed under independent sums via MGF.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Multiplying MGFs without independence | Hypothesis |
| Adding MGFs instead of multiplying | Product, not sum |
| Forgetting the power $n$ for i.i.d. | $(M)^n$ |
