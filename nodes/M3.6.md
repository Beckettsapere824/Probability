# M3.6 Markov and Chebyshev Bounds / 马尔可夫与切比雪夫

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M3.3](M3.3.md)

**Source**: SDST2601A Ch6 inequalities

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Bound tail probabilities with only mean/variance.

---

## Formulas / 公式

Markov: $\Pr(X\ge a)\le E(X)/a$ for $X\ge 0$, $a>0$.  
Chebyshev: $\Pr(|X-\mu|\ge k\sigma)\le 1/k^{2}$.

---

## Practice / 练习

Chebyshev: $\Pr(|X-\mu|\ge 2\sigma)\le 1/4$.
