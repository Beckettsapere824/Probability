# M3.6 Weighted Averages and Size-biased Sampling / 加权平均与 size-biased 抽样

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M3.1 Expectation](M3.1.md)

---

## Content / 内容

The ordinary average of class sizes seen by a professor is Σ n f_n where f_n is the proportion of classes of size n. The average class size experienced by a randomly chosen student is the size-biased mean Σ n·(n f_n) / Σ n f_n. Large classes are more likely to be sampled by students; the two averages therefore differ and both are “correct” for their respective sampling frames.

教授所见班级规模的普通平均为 Σ n f_n，其中 f_n 为规模 n 班级的比例。随机抽取的学生所经历的平均班级规模是 size-biased 均值 Σ n·(n f_n) / Σ n f_n。大班更易被学生抽到；两个平均因此不同，且对各自的抽样框架都“正确”。

Any weighted average requires that the weights themselves sum to 1 (or be normalized).

任何加权平均都要求权重本身和为 1（或先归一化）。

## Example / 例子

Classes of sizes 10,20,50 with frequencies 0.5,0.4,0.1. Professor average = 10·0.5+20·0.4+50·0.1 = 19. Student average = (10²·0.5 + 20²·0.4 + 50²·0.1) / 19 ≈ 28.9.

班级规模 10,20,50，频率 0.5,0.4,0.1。教授平均 = 19。学生平均 ≈ 28.9。

## Practice Problem / 练习题

Three class sizes 20,30,40 occur with equal frequency. Compute the professor average and the student average.

三种班级规模 20,30,40 等频出现。计算教授平均与学生平均。

<details>
<summary>Solution sketch / 解答提示</summary>

Professor: (20+30+40)/3 = 30. Student: (20²+30²+40²)/(20+30+40) = 2900/90 ≈ 32.22.

教授：(20+30+40)/3 = 30。学生：(20²+30²+40²)/(20+30+40) = 2900/90 ≈ 32.22。

</details>
