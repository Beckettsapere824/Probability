# M3.6 Markov and Chebyshev Bounds / 马尔可夫与切比雪夫

**Prerequisites / 前置**: [M3.3](M3.3.md)

**Source anchors**: Markov $\Pr(X\ge a)\le E(X)/a$; Chebyshev $\Pr(|X-\mu|\ge k\sigma)\le 1/k^{2}$.

---

## What problem are we solving? / 我们要解决什么问题？

Bound tail probabilities using only mean (Markov) or mean and variance (Chebyshev).

只用均值或均值+方差控制尾概率。

---

## Step 1: Markov / 第一步：马尔可夫

For $X\ge 0$, $a>0$: $\Pr(X\ge a)\le E(X)/a$.

---

## Step 2: Chebyshev / 第二步：切比雪夫

$\Pr(|X-\mu|\ge k\sigma)\le 1/k^{2}$. Also $\Pr(|X-\mu|\ge t)\le\mathrm{Var}(X)/t^{2}$.

---

## Step 3: Hand check / 第三步：手算

$\Pr(|X-\mu|\ge 2\sigma)\le 1/4$.

---

## Must-build intuitions / 必须建立的直觉

1. Markov needs nonnegativity; Chebyshev needs finite variance.  
2. Bounds are often loose but distribution-free.  
3. Chebyshev is Markov on $(X-\mu)^{2}$.  
4. Sharper bounds need more moment structure.

---

## Common pitfalls / 易错点

| Pitfall | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Markov on signed $X$ | Need $X\ge 0$ or use $|X|$ |
| Chebyshev without finite $\sigma^{2}$ | Hypothesis |
| Treating bound as exact probability | Inequality |

---
